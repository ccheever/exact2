## R. Reviews, and what r2 changed

r1 proposed that the compiler accept builders' common guesses (UIKit-shaped colour names, a quoted number,
`empty()` anywhere, `each` keyed by `id` by default) and name the rewrite for the rest, as the next lever on
the token gap.

| Change | Raised by |
|---|---|
| Not the token gap. Measured in tokens, repairing compile errors (the steps from an error to the next clean build, each costed as the step's base plus the context it re-sends) is 70k a GPT build and 121k a Muse build, 4.4% and 3.4% of Exact2's tokens. The gap is the knowledge builders fetch at the start (LLP 1116.013), and most of it (tests, kept values, data, composition, timers, signatures) is Exact's own protocol, not a synonym a builder could guess. This RFC is now a small, sure improvement, not the program's lever. | both (Grok: the claim; Astra: prove the impact) |
| No aliases. LLP 1081 settled one `-exact-` spelling per role, the browser's real spelling in the schema's alias column, and RULES deletes rather than keeping two; the guesses are blends (`-exact-system-grouped-background`, `-exact-tint`) that are neither UIKit's nor CSS's, and several are not certain (`-exact-tint-background` may mean `Highlight`; `systemBackground` is `Canvas` and `-exact-background`, which differ on the Mac). | Grok (rethink), Astra (bound it) |
| Nothing infers meaning the author did not write: `each` keeps its explicit `key` (an `id` field does not make an identity), `empty()` keeps its one position, a quoted length stays an error (the quoting rule is what start-here teaches). | both |
| The fast loop is a frozen fixture of the failures builders hit, compiled with no model; a model-written corpus from start-here alone measures a colder population and cannot see a better diagnostic. A diagnostic is judged in real builds by whether the step after it is a lookup. | Grok; Astra (held-out, measure repair) |
| An edit that keeps the author's intent: a `buttonStyle` error offers the native button first. | Astra |

## 0. Summary

Over rounds 13, 14 and both arms of LLP 1116.013's comparison (80 Exact2 builds), builders hit 166 compile
errors, about two a build. The common ones:

| Errors (builds) | What the builder wrote | The edit |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | `color="-exact-tint"` | `AccentColor` |
| 9 | `background-color="-exact-system-grouped-background"` | `-exact-grouped-background` |
| 12 | `-exact-tint-background`, `-exact-accent`, `-exact-tint` as a fill, `-exact-system-fill`, `-exact-system-background` | the nearest roles, by meaning |
| 3 | `font-weight="700"` | `font-weight=700` |
| 24 (9) | `buttonStyle` on a button whose face is a custom row or a sized image | keep the native button (its `text` and a symbol `image`), or drop `buttonStyle` for a custom face |
| 19 (12) | a section's `header` inside `when`, or after its rows | the `header` first; a header that changes holds the `when` |
| 9 (9) | `state queue: list<Item> = …` | `state queue = …` |
| 5 (3) | `empty()` outside a resource's `else` | `[]` |
| 3 (3) | `each x in xs` with no `key` | `key=x.id` (the item's identity) |

Today's messages state the rule, at length, and often not the edit: the colour error is a paragraph of every
colour syntax with no role named; the header error omits how to keep a changing header. So the step after an
error is often a lookup.

## D1. The edit first, in one line

Every error in the table above, and every diagnostic a builder's mistake can reach, leads with the edit that
fixes it, in the builder's own terms, then the rule in a short clause:

- A colour or other role name that is not one names the role meant: by spelling (`-exact-system-grouped-
  background` → `-exact-grouped-background`, `-exact-system-fill` → `-exact-fill`), and by meaning where the
  spelling is the platform's word for one (`-exact-tint`, `-exact-accent` → `AccentColor`; a tint as a
  background → `AccentColor` for the full accent, `Highlight` for a tinted wash). Then: the roles are listed by
  `contract vocab color`.
- A quoted number where a number is wanted: "write `font-weight=700`".
- A typed state: "drop `: list<Item>`; a state's type is inferred from its value".
- `empty()` outside a resource: "write `[]`".
- `each` without `key`: "add `key=x.id`", naming the item's fields that could key it.
- A section's header placement: the edit, and how to keep a header that changes.
- `buttonStyle` on a custom face: the native-button edit first, then the custom-face one.

Nothing is accepted that was refused; no second spelling enters any table.

## D2. A frozen fixture of what builders wrote

`contract/cli/tests/it/` gains a fixture of the failing lines builders wrote (from the bake-off's logs, each
with its context), and each must fail with its edit leading the message. A new failure the logs show joins it.
It runs with the crate's tests, no model.

## D3. Measured in real builds

The next graded comparison or round reports, per build, compile errors, the steps from an error to the next
clean build, and how many of those were lookups (`stepmix.py` classes). The expected effect is a few percent of
tokens, inside one comparison's noise; it is kept because a message that names its edit is better on its own
terms, and judged by the repair steps, not by total tokens.

## Not in this RFC

The knowledge builders fetch at the start, the program's gap, is a question of what Exact asks a builder to
learn; it goes to Charlie with LLP 1116.013's as-built.
