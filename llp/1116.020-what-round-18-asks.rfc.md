## 0. Summary

Round 18 beat UIKit 10-2 and SwiftUI 9-3, and the tells LLP 1116.019's defaults answered fell from 30 to 6. What
is left is the larger half the reviews of 1116.019 pointed at: chrome builders paint by hand, because Exact has no
short way to say it, or because the docs they copy teach the hand-made version. Three investigations reproduced
round 18's remaining tells on an iOS 27 simulator beside hand-built UIKit and Apple's own apps
(`scratchpad/inv20-{a,b,c}`). Most of the cause is in Exact's own docs, patterns and tools; four are defaults.

## 2. The tests every change must pass

- **T1, the reference.** A hand-built UIKit screen and Apple's own apps on the same iOS version, side by side on the
  simulator; not a grader's word.
- **T2, author > platform, by structure.** A default replaces only what the author left unsaid, and it is chosen by
  structure (a control's role), never by matching a word or a symbol (LLP 1116.019 §R).
- **T3, the web and the Mac.** A default added for iOS keeps the web's and the Mac's behaviour theirs.
- **T4, no programming.** A language addition is a declaration (an event attribute, a slot), never control flow;
  LLP 1116.016 D0 is untouched.

## D1. A list row's delete is one attribute

- `delete=action` on a row of a `list` declares a delete event, named as `press` and `contextmenu` are; the compiler
  expands it to today's swipe form, and `disabled` on the row keeps a locked row from deleting. On iOS the action is
  the grouped cell's own destructive full swipe titled "Delete"; the web and the Mac keep today's scroll form.
- The expansion carries the row's styling and leading inset; a swipe's references resolve within the row, which
  ends the silent refusal of a repeated `id`; a warning names `delete=` outside a list row.
- `list-detail`, `list-rows.md` and `contract vocab swipe` teach it: two lines a row where it was nine. More than a
  delete keeps today's form or the context menu.

## D2. Sheets

- A presented stack's heading is an inline title on iOS at any level, as UIKit's sheets and Apple's are; an authored
  font size still wins.
- `navigationPresentation="modal"` on a column outside a stack is refused with its edit (or presented as a sheet),
  never drawn as an overlay under the status bar.
- The patterns' sheets dismiss with `image "symbol:close"` named "Cancel" and confirm with `image
  "symbol:checkmark"` named "Save", which the host draws as UIKit's xmark and prominent checkmark; the web and the
  Mac show the name. The host keeps drawing an authored word, and an authored back title.

## D3. Search in the bar

`list-detail`'s root header gets `input type="search" value=query input=search`, filtering as you type, and the
tabs pattern one per tab; the card names it; `contract vocab search` answers with it; `keyboard-and-focus.md`
stops teaching a search row in the content.

## D4. `verify` that builders act on

- Large-text findings count in the total without failing the run: the last line reads "verify: 3 problems (3 at
  the largest text size; the tests passed)", exit 0; `verify.accept` stays the escape for a deliberate design.
- The wording: "give the text room; a smaller or fixed font is the tell".
- Sideways overflow is judged against the nearest ancestor that scrolls sideways or clips, on the web and iOS.
- An authored `font-size` in points on text is a finding: "a role (`font="-exact-title1"`) or a `rem` size
  scales"; platform controls and Exact's parts that are fixed on purpose are exempt. The timer recipe drops
  `font-size=72`. `SummaryCard` and `DoneMark` fit at the largest size.
- A grouped row's label beside a menu, stepper or date value restacks at the accessibility sizes, as a label beside
  a field does since LLP 1116.019 D1.

## D5. The shapes the docs teach

- start-here: a labelled entry or settings form is a grouped form (`grouped-form`); a focal result stays a centred
  column or `SummaryCard`. `one-screen`'s history is an inset-grouped section; `RowText` straight in a section draws
  its value trailing; the wrap remedy links a recipe that is not a card.
- The patterns keep loading, failure, empty, invalid input and saving distinct, and a refusal clears when the next
  write succeeds; Save is disabled until valid, with the reason shown; the red row is for a server's refusal.
- `EmptyState` centres in the space it is given, and `list-detail` shows it in place of the list when the list is
  empty; a warning names an `EmptyState` inside a list section.

## D6. The persona menu

The test driver's `tap` presses a bar pull-down's item (activating the item as UIKit would after a real touch), so
the `rest` pattern's test passes on iOS, and one real-touch drive opens the menu itself; `rest-backend.md` moves to the
menu; the pattern shows a display name from the data, not the server's token.

## D7. Chat

`chat-thread.md` draws Messages: a capsule holding a growing `textarea` with the send button inside, solid accent
bubbles with white text, no author name on one's own messages. `check` and the one-scheme warning allow white on an
`AccentColor` fill at the 3:1 floor a filled button's label has (LLP 1116.019 D3); a pale accent is still reported. A `Composer` part is not in this RFC.

## D8. A day's label

`formatDay(thenMs, nowMs, offsetMinutes, mode)`: `"day"` is Today, Yesterday, Tomorrow or the medium date; `"day-time"`
adds the time ("Today at 4:42 PM"); `"list"` is Mail's and Notes' (the time today, Yesterday, the weekday within the
week, else the short date). Pure: the app passes its own `now`, as the dates recipe already ticks one. The runner,
the web runtime and Lean agree; en-US until LLP 1060's tables carry other locales' words. The `dashboard` pattern
uses it.
