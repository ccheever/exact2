## D1. The card answers *how*, and is a file too

The card gains a short how-to for each lookup the round shows, a working snippet of three to eight lines and
the rule beside it: a second screen (a route, `push`, its header), a sheet (`navigationPresentation="modal"`,
Cancel and Save), a confirmation (`dialog role="alertdialog"`, `showModal`), a timer (`task every`, the clock
read at the write), keeping a value (`persist` and its limits), colour (the accent on the root, the roles
for text, fills and surfaces), reading and writing data (`resource`, `mutation … refreshes`), and the test
file with `verify`'s flags. Its cap rises from 8 to 12 KB; a test still compiles every snippet. The same text
is checked in as `docs/card.md` (a test keeps the two identical), and start-here and `AGENTS.md` name both, so
a builder that reads files reads it too.

Measure: lookups after the card (R13: GPT 8.5 web, 10.9 iOS), the `tests` recipe's opens, and Muse's lookups.

## D2. SwiftUI's quality: four of the platform's

1. **`RoundButton` never truncates its label.** It is the Clock's circle for a word (Start, Stop, Lap); a
   longer label grows it into a capsule rather than cutting it, and the card says a screen's main action of
   more than a word is a prominent button. (trivia: "Start q...".)
2. **Rows added while an app runs lay out as the first ones did** on iOS (stopwatch's laps: uneven heights,
   the label pinned to the top while the times sat lower). Find the cause and fix it in the host.
3. **No blank screen at cold launch.** The first frame comes up on the app's own background, as a hand-built
   app's launch screen does (stopwatch: "Blank white screen for ~1 s on cold launch").
4. **Rows hold at accessibility text sizes.** At the larger Dynamic Type sizes a row's text wraps and its
   neighbours make room, as UIKit's do (weather: the day label over its icon, "Low" and "High" run together).

## D3. The web's page scrolls as a page

On the web, when a route's scroller fills the page under its header, the document scrolls instead of an
inner box: the address bar and the browser's own scrolling, find, keyboard and full-page capture behave as
on any website (recipe-finder). Inner scrollers that are part of the design (a sidebar, a carousel, a chat
thread) stay inner. LLP 1116.005 D3 chose per-route scrollers over a document scroll; this narrows it: the
page's one scroller is the document's.
