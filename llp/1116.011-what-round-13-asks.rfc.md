## 0. Summary

Round 13 was the first under LLP 1116.010 D4's design: the eight Expo specs built against SwiftUI, so a round
is 16 SwiftUI pairs and 8 React, read by stack and cohort.

| | R12 | R13 |
|---|---|---|
| React (8) | 3-4-1, minutes 0.87×, tokens 1.40× | **5-2-1, minutes 0.88×, tokens 1.30×** |
| SwiftUI, the original eight | 4-4, minutes 0.65×, tokens 1.32× | 3-5, minutes 0.68×, **tokens 1.01×** |
| SwiftUI, the eight that were Expo | (7-1 against Expo) | 4-3-1, minutes 0.67×, **tokens 1.00×** |

**Exact2's own arm moved for the first time since round 9: tokens 0.84×, steps 0.84×, minutes 0.83×** on the
same specs (commands 1,281 → 1,056). Against SwiftUI, Exact2 is now level on tokens and a third faster; against
React it is ahead on quality and 1.3× on tokens. What is left is SwiftUI's quality (7-8-1 over 16 pairs) and
React's tokens.

The card worked as far as it went. Every GPT build read it early, and lookups fell (GPT iOS 15.5 → 10.9 a
build; web 11.0 → 8.5). The lookups left are not *what exists* but *how to do*: `contract vocab` for `color`
(11 times in 14 GPT builds), `screen` (9), `task` (7), `state` (6), `background-color` (6), `modal`, `confirm`,
`resource`, `persist` (5 each); the `tests` recipe in 12 of 14 builds, `content-design` in 9, `after-a-write`,
`dates-and-days` and `files-and-grants` in 7; `verify --help` 9 times. Muse builds never ran the card: they
read files, not commands.

The SwiftUI losses are mostly the builders' own (an empty workout saved, a stale error), with four that are
the platform's: trivia's start button, a `RoundButton`, truncated to "Start q..."; stopwatch's lap rows,
added while the app runs, laid out at uneven heights, and a blank screen for about a second at cold launch;
weather's rows overlapping at accessibility text sizes. On the web, recipe-finder's grader noted the content
"scrolls in an inner container rather than the document, so page-level scrolling and full-page capture behave
differently from a normal website".

## D1. The card answers *how*, in one read

The card gains a short how-to for each lookup the round shows, a working snippet of three to eight lines and
the rule beside it: a second screen (a route, `push`, its header), a sheet (`navigationPresentation="modal"`,
Cancel and Save), a confirmation (`dialog role="alertdialog"`, `showModal`), a timer (`task every`, the clock
read at the write), keeping a value (`persist` and its limits), colour (the accent on the root and roles, the
rest unsaid), reading and writing data (`resource`, `mutation … refreshes`), a dated record, and the test file
with `verify`'s flags, written to replace the `tests` and `content-design` opens. Its cap rises from 8 to
12 KB; a test compiles every snippet. The same text is checked in as `docs/card.md` (a test keeps them
identical). start-here names one read: the command for a builder that runs commands, the file for one that
reads files.

Measure: total and fresh tokens and wall time against React; lookups after the card (R13: GPT 8.5 web, 10.9
iOS); the `tests` and `content-design` opens; Muse's lookups.

## D2. SwiftUI's quality: four of the platform's

1. **`RoundButton` is for a word.** It stays the Clock's circle (Start, Stop, Lap). The compiler warns on a
   literal label longer than a word, naming the prominent button (`buttonStyle="filled"`) for a screen's main
   action, and the card and content-design say so. (trivia: "Start q...".)
2. **Rows added while an app runs lay out as the first ones did** on iOS (stopwatch's laps: uneven heights,
   the label pinned to the top while the times sat lower). Find the cause and fix it in the host.
3. **No blank screen at cold launch.** The launch screen is the app's own background, as a hand-built app's
   is; how long the first frame takes is measured and reported apart (stopwatch: "Blank white screen for ~1 s
   on cold launch").
4. **Rows hold at accessibility text sizes.** At the larger Dynamic Type sizes a row's text wraps and its
   neighbours make room, as UIKit's do (weather: the day label over its icon, "Low" and "High" run together).

## D3. (Cut in r2.)

The web page as the document's scroller waits for a decision that keeps fixed chrome, per-route offsets, iOS's
content box, Back restoration, split panes and a sheet's isolation (QUEUE).

## D4. The starters' first composition

start-here and the top of `pattern rest` and `pattern list-detail` say, a line each, the composition the
SwiftUI winners had: a row pushes its detail; create is a sheet; a narrow window gets one column, a wide one two
(`responsive-columns`, by the viewport's width, so an open foldable can have two; a list and its detail on iOS are
UIKit's split, LLP 1127); an edit is a sheet, not a long inline form. `pattern rest` keeps its detail route and
add sheet prominent so a builder trimming it keeps them. (polls, team-kanban, book-club, workout-log.)

## D5. A reload note, not a reload problem, for state kept in memory

`verify`'s reload of each end screen reports a screen whose texts change as a note: shown, not counted toward a
clean run, with the advice that a saved value must survive and a game in progress may not. A field the app
saved (it reads it back from storage) that reloads different stays a finding. (trivia moved its test endings to
quieter screens to silence the check.)

## Not in scope

The builders' own bugs; `RoundButton`'s light-hue label colour (the grader wanted the Clock's green label,
which fails contrast in light: left as LLP 1116.008 D6 built it).

## As built (2026-10-12)

Five lanes, merged on `bake/1116.011-integration` with origin/main; lane log `~/bakeoff/lanes-1116.011.md`.

- **D1.** The card is 12.2 KB (cap 12,288) and ends with a "how" section: a second screen and a sheet, a
  confirmation, a timer, keeping a value, colour on roles, reading and writing data, a dated record, a test and
  `verify`'s flags, and composing a screen; a test compiles every snippet. `docs/card.md` is the same text
  (regenerate with `bun scripts/exact.mjs contract vocab > docs/card.md`; a test compares them). start-here and
  `AGENTS.md` name one read, the command or the file.
- **D2.1.** `lower-round-button-label` warns on a literal `RoundButton` label over seven characters (the
  circle's 68-point inner width), naming `buttonStyle="filled"` for a longer main action; the part keeps its
  shape.
- **D2.2.** A kernel bug, not the grouped list's: the leaf pass shared one flattened-runs buffer between
  measurements keyed by slot, and a fitted text (`-exact-fit: shrink`) overwrote it without clearing the key,
  so a row label measured after the readout took the readout's runs (76.5-point text, a 92-point row). The fit
  branch clears the key (`kernel/src/layout.rs`); it struck any host with a fitted text over a list.
- **D2.3.** The white was the system's launch screen: the bake records the first route's ground
  (`graph.launchBackground`) and a grouped first screen launches on the grouped grey in both schemes; the
  manifest's `background_color` still wins. Time to first frame in the simulator is mostly image mapping before
  `main` (1.1–1.25 s), measured on a device at about 0.1 s by LLP 1047.001.000; untouched.
- **D2.4.** The host already scales text roles with the content size, measures them scaled and grows rows;
  weather's overlap came from the app's own px widths (author wins). One host bug: an overlong line was flushed
  the wrong way on Apple hosts; it starts at its start edge now (CSS Text 3 §7.1). `agent-pitfalls` says to
  size a box beside role text in `rem`; QUEUE holds a warning for it.
- **D3.** Cut (QUEUE).
- **D4.** start-here's idea item: "Compose as the platform does: a row pushes its detail; create and edit are
  sheets; a phone gets one column, a wide window two." `pattern rest` (which has a detail route and an add
  sheet) and `pattern list-detail` say it at their tops, with "keep both when trimming".
- **D5.** A reload difference is a note, not counted; it stays a finding when a `persist` state reloads
  different, or a field the test typed into that the app still holds shows something else.
