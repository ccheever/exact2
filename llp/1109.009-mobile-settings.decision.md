## Pending local choices

[decision] Keyboard, Follow-ups and Organization use one queued choice mutation.
Their pending valid intents overlay the existing global preferences resource in
send order, independently by field. The overlay preserves read readiness,
errors, revision and unrelated preferences. A failed reply contributes no value;
a later queued intent still contributes, including the same value. A successful
write stops contributing after a fresh post-acknowledgement read. The queue's
failure notice reads its own reply slot.
