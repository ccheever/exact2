## 1. D1's "left the app" refusal cannot be answered by the app

A tap that opens Safari hangs the next operation for 120 seconds because the driver gives up waiting for a reply (`REPLY_MS` in `scripts/agent-wire.mjs`), not because `clock` runs out a bound it could cut short. Once iOS suspends the process, the agent socket stops answering, so a later operation cannot refuse itself.

The tap reply is also too early to carry `left: "background"`. `UIApplication.shared.open` is asynchronous. Settings and declared schemes already stay inside the process under the agent: they are journaled and not opened (`host/apple/Sources/ExactIOS/main.swift`, `OpenURL.swift`). `http`, `https`, `mailto`, and `tel` still call `UIApplication.shared.open` with no agent check. A share sheet is already held under the agent unless the drive asked for the real sheet.

Journal those four schemes the same way, and do not open them. The drive keeps its socket, and `logs` shows the URL. For a real share-sheet extension that must leave the app, push one line as the scene resigns active, and have the driver fail the operation it is already waiting on. Do not wait for the next round trip.

## 2. D1's four causes use a narrower signal than the host already has

`state.navigation` already reports the route, the stack, the top sheet, and a transition phase. That phase is `in-progress` while `navigation.inTransition`, `modals.inTransition`, or `menus.inTransition` is set. `inTransition` includes `nativePopPending`: UIKit removes the controller before `willShow`, which is the Back ordering the diaries found by logging. In that window `transitionCoordinator` is often already nil, so D1's "moving" test (coordinator, `isBeingPresented`, `isBeingDismissed`) does not match, and the refusal falls through to "unmounted" and tells the agent to `tap <list> into <key>`.

The sheet-over-a-sheet case is the one `awaitModalTransitions` was written for (`AgentIOS.swift`): the input that presented the sheet waits, and if the wait expires the reply is already `settled: false`, `reason: "transition"`. That wait does not start for a push alone. The miss refusal should quote this same observation — phase, top route, top sheet — and whether the runner still has the node. One definition, the one `clock settle` already uses.
