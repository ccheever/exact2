1. **must-fix** — `host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/TextBreaks.swift:50`. The content-end walk treats a trailing forced break as ink, so the spaces in front of it never hang and a CRLF boundary never fits. `CTTypesetterSuggestClusterBreak` stops between CR and LF, and `CharacterSet.whitespaces` does not include CR/LF, so the boundary’s content end stays on the LF, one unit past the cluster fit. At Helvetica 17, `aa hello   \nworld` at width 70 is one CoreText line (`aa hello   \n` / `world`) and two here (`aa ` / `hello   \n` / `world`). `aa hello\r\nworld` wraps after `aa ` even at width 400, and `break-word` then returns the cluster fit, so the next line is a lone LF. GL characters (U+00A0, U+2007, U+202F) stay part of the content.
