# Q2. Logical operators

- **Recommendation:** Keep the words `and`/`or`/`not` as the one spelling; delete `&&`/`||`/`!`, and add the words as a row in 1116.016 D1's closed deviation table in the same landing.
- **Confidence:** 60.
- **Reason:** With no refusal evidence either way and a respelling already measured level (tokens 1.03, minutes 1.00, [1116.015:342](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1116.015-what-exact-asks-builders-to-learn.rfc.md:342)), the decision is migration cost plus reading. The words are the corpus (~670 lines) and are also CSS's logic words, in the language whose author writes CSS. D1's rule governs what an accepted expression *evaluates to* ([1116.016:130](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1116.016-contract-expressions-are-typescripts.rfc.md:130)); `a and b` is not a TypeScript expression, so it needs a declared deviation row ([1116.016:134](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1116.016-contract-expressions-are-typescripts.rfc.md:134)), one line in start-here — cheaper honesty than rewriting the corpus to remove the need for the row. Both spellings parse to the same nodes today ([expr.rs:103](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/contract/syntax/src/parser/expr.rs:103), [expr.rs:146](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/contract/syntax/src/parser/expr.rs:146)), so nothing semantic is at stake.
- **Migration cost under "Delete; don't deprecate"** ([RULES.md:48](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/rules/RULES.md:48)): words-only deletes three parser arms and rewrites the symbol uses — two `&&` lines in `apps/` and `packages/` ([widget.contract:25](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/apps/caltrain/widget.contract:25), [app.contract:664](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/apps/native-fixture/app.contract:664)), plus a refusal naming the word, docs, and the D1 row. Symbols-only costs a token-aware rewrite of ~670 operator lines (never the ~1,296 comment lines or string contents), the wider embedded-Contract corpus and out-of-repo apps, and frees three reserved binder words ([names.rs:21](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/contract/syntax/src/parser/names.rs:21)) — a second, quieter language change. Keeping both costs no migration and is the one outcome the rule forbids: a permanent second spelling.
- **Strongest reason against:** Every TypeScript-trained builder guesses the symbols first, and 1116.016's stated goal is TypeScript's spelling for expressions ([1116.016:110](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1116.016-contract-expressions-are-typescripts.rfc.md:110)). Words-only manufactures first-round refusals that symbols-only never sees.
- **What flips it:** A guess-and-repair run of the words-only arm (§7's instrument, with T6 read first per [1116.016:303](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1116.016-contract-expressions-are-typescripts.rfc.md:303)) showing operator refusals beyond the run-to-run spread — they should repair in one edit by construction; if they do not, take the symbols.
