NOT READY

1. **must-fix — Inline text ignores its own scheme.** [BatchShapes.swift:140](/tmp/x23/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/BatchShapes.swift:140), [inline.rs:18](/tmp/x23/host/linux/src/paint/inline.rs:18). Apple discards `color_scheme` when decoding inline styles; Linux passes the paragraph’s `dark` unchanged through every run. A light paragraph containing dark inline text with its own `light-dark()` colour therefore paints the light branch. Preserve the computed scheme in Apple’s inline representation and resolve each Linux run’s foreground, background, shadow and stroke using that run’s scheme.

2. **must-fix — Dynamic `normal` does not consistently mean unset.** [kernel.rs:175](/tmp/x23/kernel/src/kernel.rs:175), [style.rs:556](/tmp/x23/host/web-js/src/style.rs:556). Under a dark ancestor in a light session, an explicit runtime `normal` stops kernel inheritance and makes `dark_in()` fall back to light. The JS target writes literal `color-scheme:normal`, bypassing the Rust web serializer’s `inherit` mapping. Both contradict §8. Normalize runtime `normal` to an unset row—or skip it during scheme resolution—and add the corresponding JS mapping. Test a bound value changing to `normal` beneath a dark ancestor.

3. **must-fix — Lifted Apple views lose logical inheritance.** [style.rs:838](/tmp/x23/host/apple/src/style.rs:838), [DialogsMac.swift:68](/tmp/x23/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/Mac/DialogsMac.swift:68). Boxes receive only their own scheme, but dialogs and popovers move outside their authored ancestors. A dialog under a dark column inherits the viewport’s light appearance after opening; its text can remain dark because text receives computed rows. Send computed schemes to projected subtree roots, or explicitly preserve and update their logical inherited appearance while lifted. Cover opening, ancestor changes, clearing and closing.

4. **must-fix — SVG descendants have no per-element resolution context.** [SvgScene.swift:499](/tmp/x23/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/SvgScene.swift:499), [svg.rs:344](/tmp/x23/host/linux/src/paint/svg.rs:344). Contract admits `color-scheme` on SVG groups and shapes, but Apple passes one `dark` value through the scene and Linux paints shapes using the outer SVG’s scheme. A dark group inside a light SVG therefore resolves its static `light-dark()` paints incorrectly. Carry the computed scheme through scene items and use it for their paints, gradients, text and masks.

5. **must-fix — Apple’s initial appearance reports can be lost.** [NodeViewIOS.swift:538](/tmp/x23/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/NodeViewIOS.swift:538), [Session.swift:1304](/tmp/x23/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/Session.swift:1304). Initial styles and trait callbacks run during `apply(batch)`, before `rebooted()` reports the session scheme. `noteAppearance` drops those callbacks while `schemeDark` is nil. A background-only keyframe animation inside an initially dark subtree need never report again: subsequent background updates do not change `text_color`. It consequently keeps the engine’s session scheme, including on Apple. Replay initial per-view reports after establishing the session scheme, and test cold boot with background-only keyframes.

6. **must-fix — Report precedence cannot represent a report matching the session.** [paint.rs:25](/tmp/x23/kernel/src/motion/paint.rs:25), [paint.rs:258](/tmp/x23/kernel/src/motion/paint.rs:258). `appearance()` gives reports precedence over computed schemes, but `set_view_scheme()` discards reports equal to the session. With a light session and computed dark node, reporting light either returns immediately or removes the entry; subsequent resolution selects dark again. Preserve explicit reports independently of session equality, update `ExactSession.noteAppearance` accordingly, and distinguish clearing a report from reporting light.
