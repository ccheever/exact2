## MATERIAL

**1. D2 — the waiting reply is not the one the executor decodes**

Section D2 step 3. Problem: the queued-await reply is specified as `{tag: 1, ticket: 0, waiting: true}`, and the text says `js/src/lib.rs:775` already turns that into `WAITING`. The match requires a `call` and a `ticket` (`lib.rs:774–784`). With no `call`, `num("call")` is `None` and the reply becomes `` `{source}` is pending on no ticket ``. Today’s waiting reply includes the id (`prelude.js:776`). An answer whose own operation is queued behind another owner fails instead of parking. Fix: return `{tag: 1, call: call.id, ticket: 0, waiting: true}`.

**2. D2 — `background` in `storing` is claimed as ticket 0**

Section D2, the move, and the deletion cited at `prelude.js:723–727`. Problem: those lines are the comment. The claim is the scan at `prelude.js:783–785`: any `storing` member whose id is not in `calls` makes this answer return `{tag:1, call, ticket:0}`. `background` is added to `storing` and is never inserted into `calls` (“never replied to, never claimed”). Every still-pending answer with `call.storage == 0` takes that path, including one awaiting a promise chained on background work. Two such answers park two ticket-0 continuations; both call `Session::continuation`, and `admit` wakes every waiter (`vendor/ibex2/src/task.rs:201`). That is the shared-context spin D3 says is gone, and it fails the §5 test that this answer settles `WAITING`. Fix: the scan must not treat `background` as claimable work. That answer takes the existing waiting reply (with `call`). Only `background()` holds the storage ticket while its operation is the head.

**3. D7 — the wasm realm cannot park the waiting reply D2 specifies**

Section D7, and D2’s liveness sentence. Problem: `moduleWide` is `bytesDoor !== undefined` (`prelude.js:730`). `__exact_bytes` exists only in the Hermes shim (`js/src/shim.cc:159`). In `module-glue.js` the door is absent, so the waiting return at `prelude.js:786` does not run. An answer that awaits background work and has `call.storage == 0` falls through to the “pending on nothing” refusal (`prelude.js:792–796`). `finish` then sees a tag other than 1 and retires the owner (`module-glue.js:167`). Returning the specified `{tag:1, ticket:0, waiting:true}` instead does not park either: the turn loop continues while `ticket === 0` (`module-glue.js:199–204`) and `storage.deliver` waits until that owner’s completion queue is non-empty (`storage.js:102–105`). Nothing is going to enqueue there. If the loop is changed to exit, `finish` throws `module awaits a fetch it never made` (`module-glue.js:160–161`), because ticket 0 is not in `context.requests`. Fix: on this realm, settle returns the waiting reply (with `call`) for that answer even though `moduleWide` is false. The loop and `finish` treat `waiting: true` as “park and re-settle after a background delivery,” and do not call `storage.deliver` for it.
