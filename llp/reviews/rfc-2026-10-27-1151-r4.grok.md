## 2. Remaining fundamental issues

**1. The land after a tap is specified as two different steps, and `old` is equated with the previous step.**

A tap and its land are two events, and each event is its own observation block (`semantics/Contract/Observe.lean:159-163`; `semantics/difftest/src/observe.rs:220-258`). The driver's tap returns only after that land (`scripts/agent.mjs:1274-1278`; `host/linux/src/agent.rs:1043-1046`). D1 then says three things that cannot all hold: `after "a"` reads the lines after the land and takes `old` from before the tap; `old` "is the previous step's line"; and `after clock` holds after every `land`. D2 still compares `after` to a step and its predecessor.

Read as two blocks, the predecessor of the land is the tap. `expect state counter == old + 1` is then false on every successful increment, because the increment is already in `old`. Read as every land being a clock step, `after clock` / `only writes` is about the tap's `then` as well as the clock, which is not `advance_keeps_user_state`. Fusing them into one block makes "the only commit was `a`" false whenever the `then` ran, so the pair never covers the case it was added for. No reading keeps both `increment_adds_one` and `advance_keeps_user_state`.

Smallest change: two blocks. `after "a"` reads the holds after the land and `old` from before the tap. Check it only when the tap's `ran` lines are exactly `handler a` and the land's traced lines are only the `then`s and queue `next`s that tap armed. That land is not an `after clock` step. `after clock` is `clock +N` and a land that delivers `clock data` or a settle step. Rewrite D2's predecessor sentence to say this.

**2. D9's template does not prove the frame line r4 now checks.**

`only writes` fails when any other root slot changes, a `queue` mutation's included. A queued send writes that slot and no other (`semantics/Contract/Invariant.lean:1005-1014`). `Reachable.advance_untouched` and `Reachable.slotIn` both require `isQueue p x = false` (`Invariant.lean:1044`, `1093-1096`), so they do not show a queue slot untouched. D9 says the line is unproved when it names a queue slot. A line that does not name one still depends on that slot not changing. Skipping queue slots and calling the template a proof proves a weaker statement than the check.

Smallest change: the line is proved only when every other root slot is shown untouched. A queue slot in that set is the unproved case whether or not the line names it. Delete "therefore a fixed Lean template" as a claim that the template is the check.
