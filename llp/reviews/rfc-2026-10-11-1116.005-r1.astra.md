**D3 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** Document scrolling is appropriate for a page, but inferring it from any window-filling scroller risks silently changing authored CSS layout. Define a bounded, documented page-scroller projection, respecting authored containment and preserving nested/split scrollers. Crucially, moving several retained routes onto one document scroller loses their independent offsets unless ownership and restoration are specified. [LLP 1038 §7](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1038-router.rfc.md:643) deliberately relies on mounted rows retaining scroll. Explicitly reconcile that contract, including Back and resizing into a split. Find-in-page and full-page capture also cannot imply that virtualized, unmounted list content exists. Sound direction; an automatic layout rewrite would be a trap.

**D4 — RIGHT.** Stale painted text and disappearing titles are framework correctness failures. Fix the underlying invalidation and bar association across content transitions. The stopwatch’s correct accessibility tree alongside incorrect pixels demonstrates why success must mean the presented screen is correct.

**D5 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** A consistent seed range and documented integer mixer are sensible. However, a 32-bit seed multiplied by `2654435761` can still exceed JavaScript’s exact-integer range. Remove the claim that ordinary arithmetic becomes exact. Define the guarantee around the proposed `Math.imul`/unsigned mixing recipe, preserving deterministic replay and incorporating the round before precision can be lost.

**D6 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** Make this a platform-specific advisory about duplicated projected titles, not a universal prohibition on repeating a heading. A web page can legitimately have both a chrome label and a content heading. Diagnose the native presentation without automatically removing authored content or encouraging agents to impoverish the web screen.
