Three other directional changes matter:

- **D4 needs a better definition of success than “text grew.”** The predecessor deliberately fixed `RoundButton` and `RingProgress` text sizes; those remain in [content.contract](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/contract/lib/content.contract:104). Native controls also cap growth. Flag unexplained fixed body/content text, while recognizing platform-owned behavior. Conversely, [agent-check.mjs](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/scripts/agent-check.mjs:97) exempts authored clamps and even recommends ellipses. Counting findings without checking retained content creates exactly the incentive §9 worries about.

- **D5 should teach complete interaction states.** The records show errors alongside “empty” messages in chat and polls, unexplained disabled Add in water-tracker, and inaccessible Save/Cancel in contacts. These are substantial quality failures. Patterns should distinguish loading, failure, successful emptiness, invalid input, and saving using existing Contract constructs. Disabled Save should retain an understandable reason. Grouped forms are a useful starting point; “cards are nonnative” would become the next overgeneralization. See [polls.json](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r18/records/polls.json), [water-tracker.json](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r18/records/water-tracker.json), and [contacts.json](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r18/records/contacts.json).

- **D6 is a larger lever than its persona count suggests.** Builders abandon native controls when they cannot verify them. Fixing that improves fidelity, tokens, and time together. However, direct menu-item activation must not become evidence that opening and operating the actual menu works. D7 should similarly distinguish accepting a platform color pairing from suppressing contrast findings for arbitrary pale accents; the current [contrast policy](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/scripts/agent-check.mjs:52) already makes contextual distinctions.

D8 is a reasonable bounded, pure formatter and does not meaningfully move Contract toward general programming. Its single-offset, en-US contract should remain an explicit limitation: it cannot establish locale/calendar fidelity across historical DST boundaries. [dates-and-days.md](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/docs/recipes/dates-and-days.md) already exposes the underlying limitation.

§7 can demonstrate progress with these changes:

- **Separate rendering from detection.** Unchanged-app replay can establish default/part improvements. D4 should detect known defects in that replay; it cannot make their screenshots improve without builder edits.
- **Add a small docs comparison:** fresh builders on the same new engine and tools, using old versus revised compatible examples. Replay alone holds the builder fixed and therefore cannot measure documentation.
- **Measure observable failures per relevant opportunity**, plus task completion and severity. A deleted feature must not count as a removed tell. Repeat a blinded sample to estimate grader noise, include a few held-out prompts, and report paired token/time intervals with failures and timeouts retained. Requiring every small cause category to fall in one round is too noisy.
