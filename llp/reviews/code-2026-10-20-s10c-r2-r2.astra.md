The unconditional final unlink can remove a successor’s lock too; age-based reclamation also evicts live holders. Make reclamation/release ownership-safe. The test at [tamber-acme.test.mjs:111](scripts/tamber-acme.test.mjs:111) runs two calls in one process and misses this race.
