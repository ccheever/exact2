Nothing here is blocking. The circle fit matches a rounded rectangle, the 0.5 coverage cut is the right edge for linear coverage, and device scale is recovered and put back correctly. The holes are cases the two tests never draw.

The row-center model is the right one. A radius `r` meets row `row` at inset `r - sqrt(r² - dy²)` with `dy = r - (row + 0.5)`, and inset 0 once the row center is at or below the circle center, where a rounded rect's side is straight. `edges` interpolates the 0.5 crossing between pixel centers (`dx - 0.5 + t`). That is what the scale-1 raster test is checking, and it is why radii 4–26 come back within 0.2 px. Scale is `image.width / viewport.w`: the PNG is the viewport in device pixels (iOS `pageSize` at screen scale, macOS backing pixels, web at `deviceScaleFactor` 1, Linux one pixel a point), and the fitted radius is divided by that scale before the 1 pt compare. A channel that matches the background is ignored on purpose: `coverage` keeps the channel with the largest `|fill - ground|`, which is why red on white still fits.

1. **should-fix** — Padding is the next later id in the southeast quadrant, and only the smaller inset. `scripts/scale-check.mjs:87-89`. Layout nodes are in ascending id order and carry no parent, and the predicate is `id > box.id && x >= box.x && y >= box.y`. A childless box therefore selects the next sibling (a column sibling shares `x`, so the reported padding is 0, and an expectation of 0 passes). A child pulled past the top or left edge by a negative margin fails the predicate, so the offset is never negative and a grandchild or sibling is measured instead. `Math.min(dx, dy)` then lets a box whose insets are 16 and 40 pass an expectation of 16. Both axes of a node whose top-left lies inside the box, or the tree's first child, is the rule the comment states.

2. **should-fix** — A border or shadow is read as the fill. `scripts/scale-check.mjs:26-33`. `coverage` is the blend fraction on the single strongest channel, clamped to `[0, 1]`. On white, a blue fill's strongest channel is red or green, and a black or dark-gray pixel scores 1 there, so the corner cutout's drop shadow (it sits inside the layout rect and outside the curve) pulls the 0.5 point outward and shrinks the fitted radius. A border that stays under 0.5 on that channel is skipped, and the probe locks onto the inner fill, inset by the border width; the circle fit turns that uniform inset into a radius more than 1 px off an 8 px corner with a 2 px border. A fill that equals the background never moves any channel, `edges` returns no samples, and `fitRadius` reports 0.

3. **should-fix** — The radius search stops at 400 device pixels. `scripts/scale-check.mjs:57`. `fitRadius` steps `r` from 0 to 400 in the screenshot's pixels. A pill is half the shorter side, so the cap is 400 pt at 1x, 200 pt at 2x, and about 133 pt at 3x. Past that the fitter returns 400 and the gate fails a correctly reduced `radius.full`. iOS screenshots are 3x; a card taller than about 267 pt is already over the cap. The search bound needs to be at least half the bitmap.

4. **should-fix** — A corner that is not fully inside the bitmap is sampled off the image. `scripts/scale-check.mjs:39-48`. `at` indexes `y * width + x` with no bounds check. A negative origin (a box scrolled off the top or left; `Math.round` of a small negative) reads the previous row, and a scan that runs past the right edge (`dx < w/2` on a box wider than the viewport) reads the next row. A row that never hits 0.5 is omitted, and `fitRadius` then treats the survivors as rows 0, 1, … so a missing first row is fitted with the wrong `dy`. A box whose border box sits fully in the bitmap, including one flush with the origin, is fine: the empty part of the corner is inside the rect. An ancestor clip is whatever the screenshot shows, which is the drawn edge.

For `r <= 0.5` every row-center inset is 0, the squared error ties, and the search keeps the smallest `r`. A sharp corner is 0, which is right. A radius of 0.4 px is also 0. The 1 pt tolerance still passes both. `scripts/scale-check.test.mjs` only draws radii 4–26 at 1x and one 24 px pill at 2x, plus one child already sitting at (16, 16). It never draws scale 3, a radius of 0 or under 1 px, a missing or out-of-box child, a third color, or a corner past the bitmap.
