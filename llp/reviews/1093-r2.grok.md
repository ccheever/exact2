## Findings

1. **MATERIAL — D2, D5.** `used_height` becomes the percentage basis the second pass is required to leave indefinite.
   **Problem.** D5 writes the balanced height and lays out once more, and calls that pass a fixed point because the flow thread depends on W. D2 then applies `used_height` from `taffy_style`. A definite height on the style becomes `styled_based_known_dimensions` (`vendor/taffy/src/compute/block.rs:399-400`) and the children's percentage basis (`:507-510` and `:570-571`). Chrome 154, `column-count: 2; column-fill: balance; width: 400px; column-gap: 0`, a `height: 50%` child beside a `height: 100px` sibling: the child is 18px and the container is 59px, `scrollWidth` 400. The same tree with `height: 59px` makes the child 30px, gives the sibling a third fragment, and makes `scrollWidth` 600. `measure_auto_height` builds that style and then sets `size.height` to `auto` before it reads the result (`kernel/src/kernel/geometry.rs:57-62`), so the returned height is the flow-thread height. The restore compute (`:68-75`) is the pass that resolves the percentages.
   **Evidence.** `/tmp/llp1093-r2.html` cases `pct-auto` and `pct-used`, Chrome 154.0.8037.98. `block.rs:399-400`, `:570-571`, `:615-618`. `geometry.rs:59`.
   **Fix.** Apply `used_height` only to `container_outer_height` after the children are laid out (`block.rs:615-618`). Leave `style.size`, `known_dimensions`, and `container_percentage_resolution_height` on the auto-height path. The second pass from the root moves the container, its ancestors, and what follows it, and passes the same indefinite basis. `measure_auto_height` reads the height the cut just produced.

2. **MATERIAL — D7.** The scrollable overflow is not the right edge of the last column and H, and both hosts recompute it from child frames.
   **Problem.** D7 stores that pair through `set_content` (`kernel/src/layout/publication.rs:92-97`) and says the hosts grow from that field. `content_size` starts there and then takes every descendant's frame plus end padding (`host/apple/src/host.rs:1344-1350`, `host/linux/src/paint.rs:1439-1445`). Chrome 154, six 20px lines, `column-width: 80px; column-gap: 10px; height: 40px; width: 80px; column-fill: auto; overflow: hidden`: `scrollWidth` 260, last column ending at x = 260. With `padding: 10px` the last column ends at 270 and `scrollWidth` is 280, `scrollHeight` 60. Under `direction: rtl` the column edges are 0, −90, and −180, the last column's right edge is −100, and `scrollWidth` is still 260. `overflow: auto` with a classic scrollbar lays the columns out in a 65×25 client box and reports `scrollWidth` 440, one line per column. An abspos with `top: 200px` inside that flow reports `scrollWidth` 620, with the abs box at x = 540. A max of right edges misses the RTL overflow. Taffy reserves a scrollbar gutter only for `Overflow::Scroll` (`block.rs:465-476`).
   **Evidence.** `/tmp/llp1093-r2b.html` cases `scroll`, `pad`, `rtl`, `scroll-auto`, and `abs`. The two `content_size` functions above.
   **Fix.** Store one border-box overflow rect. Its inline span runs from the leftmost column edge through the rightmost, then the end padding, and is at least the client width. Its block span is H plus the block-axis padding, and is at least the client height. Subtract the scrollbar gutter from U and H before N and W for `overflow: auto` and `scroll`. The host walks must see translated frames. The abspos stays in that max, or D10 names it as dropped.

3. **MATERIAL — D2.** The cited flag stops margin collapse and leaves the box in its parent's formatting context.
   **Problem.** A multicol establishes its BFC where `compute_block_layout` chooses a context (`block.rs:369-370` and `:419-425`). `establishes_new_bfc` at `block.rs:516` is a second local, used for margin collapse (`:520-532`) and for float-height contribution (`:611`). D2 tells the patch to set the second one. The first child's `margin-top: 30px` then stays inside, and the container still shares the parent's floats. D2 also computes W once U is known. For an auto width, U at `block.rs:552-558` is the shrink-to-fit width D10.5 says not to solve W from.
   **Evidence.** `vendor/taffy/src/compute/block.rs:369-370`, `:419-425`, `:516-532`, `:552-558`. D1's formatting-context sentence and D2's patch bullet.
   **Fix.** Add the multicol to both conditions, so line 369 allocates a new `BlockFormattingContext` and line 516 keeps the first child's margin inside. When the used width is auto, keep the line 552 result and lay the children out as one column.

4. **MATERIAL — D7, D8.** Stage 1 accepts a hit only inside a fragment, and the boxes D7 gives no fragments to become unhittable.
   **Problem.** D7 stores fragments on boxes that straddle, and a box wholly inside one column gets nothing new. D8 says `hit` and `tap` accept a point only inside a fragment. Linux hits the painted box (`host/linux/src/presenter.rs:1127-1140`), and that box is `node.frame` (`host/linux/src/paint.rs:984-991`), the union. `tap` aims at `PaintedBox::center` (`presenter.rs:1153-1156`), which for a 420-wide union sits in the gap. `Kernel::fragments` is specified to be read the way `sticky_constraint` is read. That function is recomputed from the published frames and the Taffy layout (`kernel/src/kernel/sticky.rs:91-148`). After publication those frames are the translated ones, and the line range is gone.
   **Evidence.** `presenter.rs:1127-1156`, `paint.rs:984-991`, `sticky.rs:91-148`. D7's "needs nothing new" sentence and D8's hit sentence.
   **Fix.** Store the cut's fragments on the kernel and drop them when a box no longer straddles. A box with an empty record is hit as its translated frame. A box with a record is hit only inside a fragment, and `tap` aims at the center of the first fragment.

5. **MATERIAL — D8.** The fragment inverse feeds `lineIndex` and leaves the caret and the highlight in the union's content box.
   **Problem.** `TextSelectionMac.swift` is macOS-only (`:3`). `line` subtracts `contentBox()` and calls `lineIndex` (`:293-300`). `index` then places the caret with the original point against `content.minX + origin` (`:302-309`). A point in column 2 has an x at the column, and the line origin is the unfragmented x, so the offset lands past the glyphs. `draw` paints `selectionRects` in that same content box (`:315-320`), and those rects sit at `bounds.minY + baseline` (`host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/ParagraphGeometry.swift:47-51`), the flow-thread y. iOS has no `TextSelection`. Link hits on both platforms go through `InlineText.textOffset` (`host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/InlineText.swift:116-128`), which uses the same content-box subtraction. The only selection owner is `PresenterMac.swift:60`.
   **Evidence.** Those three Swift sites. D7's fragment rect and `(dx, dy)`.
   **Fix.** Map the point by the fragment rect inside the union and the inverse of `(dx, dy)`, and use that point for `lineIndex` and `stringIndex`. Paint `selectionRects` per fragment, in that fragment's coordinates, for its line range. A point in the union's gap selects nothing. Apply the same map in `textOffset`.

6. **MINOR — D5.** Removing the N−1 bound leaves the auto-height balancer without a cap.
   **Problem.** The loop runs while the cut needs more than N columns and H is below its cap, and it stops when H does not grow. The table names a cap for `max-height` and a ceiling for a definite height. The auto row has no cap. The shortage is "the least space shortage the cut reported," with no value for a forced break that stays past N however tall H gets. Four `break-before: column` paragraphs in `column-count: 2` keep four columns at every height.
   **Evidence.** D5's balancer paragraph and the auto cell of its table.
   **Fix.** The auto cap is the flow thread's single-column height. Shortage is the smallest addition that lets the overflowing fragment fit, and 0 when no addition will. A stretch that does not reduce the column count stops, and the extra columns are overflow columns of that height.

7. **MINOR — §1, D2.** The 1em gap is resolved at layout, from the computed font size, into the gap W is computed with.
   **Problem.** `column_gap` is an `f32` whose unset value is 0 (`kernel/tables/schema.json:2195-2199`). `to_taffy` copies that 0 into the engine gap (`kernel/src/style.rs:1331-1333`). The multicol struct's `gap` is what D1 and the patch use for N and W. Filling it from the stored `f32` builds a 0 gap. Chrome's computed value stays the keyword `normal` while the used gap is the element's computed `font-size` (16px beside a 40px parent; 40px for `font-size: 2em`).
   **Evidence.** `schema.json:2195-2199`, `style.rs:1331-1333`. `/tmp/llp1093-r2.html` cases `gap40` and `gap-em`.
   **Fix.** When the mask bit is unset and the element is a multicol container, the used gap is `style.font_size` at layout. Leave the row at 0 so flex and grid stay on 0, and leave the web emitting nothing.

   **Evidence.** §4's Chrome-fixtures bullet, §5's second deferred row, D10.1, `browser_cases.rs:177-179`.
   **Fix.** Mark D10 rows in the TSV as expected kernel rects. Split the deferred row: height and min-height wait on a cut that can end inside a childless box; max-height waits on fragmenting the overflowing children while the border box stays at the cap.
