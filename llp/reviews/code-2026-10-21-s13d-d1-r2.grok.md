- Font contexts are interned by `format!("{:?} {}", attrs, children)` (`rows.rs:154`). A `Vec` debug string ends in `]`, so the `true` and `false` suffixes cannot collide. `Expr` and `Attr` debug includes spans, so two different source locations are not merged, and two expanded copies that share a span still report that span.
