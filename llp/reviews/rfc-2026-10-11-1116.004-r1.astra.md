**Overall: READY WITH CHANGES.** The repairs are justified, but D1 overgeneralizes the desktop evidence, and D4–D5 need a stronger plan for reducing authoring steps.

**D1 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** Automatic sidebar presentation is sensible for root app navigation, and an explicit one-attribute split can remove substantial boilerplate. Preserve authored layout overrides and the existing navigation state, history, modal behavior and retained screens across resizing. This fits LLP 1115 only within that boundary. Explicitly amend [LLP 1038 D12](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1038-router.rfc.md:549), which currently calls rail selection an app layout decision; keeping the router unchanged is necessary but does not resolve that policy change. Make D1.3 advisory: a narrow calculator or reading surface can be appropriate. Width alone neither diagnoses poor composition nor catches Contacts’ full-width stretched layout.

**D2 — RIGHT.** These are concrete violations of expected platform behavior, with direct grader evidence. Fixing gesture arbitration, cell sizing, backgrounds and accent propagation improves quality without requiring builders to learn more. Keep authored styling authoritative, including whitespace behavior; these should be host corrections, not compensating app recipes.

**D3 — RIGHT.** Prioritize this immediately. Chat Rooms’ diary attributes approximately fifteen minutes to the dismissal failure, followed by removal of the sheet. That directly damages all three objectives. Treat this as a presentation-lifecycle defect across supported dismissal paths, with the underlying screen’s interaction and state preserved.

**D4 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** The warnings are useful, but Flashcards’ modal was partly a documentation failure. Its [diary](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r6/apps/flashcards-exact2/DIARY.md) says it followed the navigation recipe, which still recommends `role="dialog" aria-modal=true` for a custom modal. Correct that guidance and provide one complete opening/closing idiom alongside the diagnostic. Distinguish supported routed sheets from inert role-only overlays. Otherwise Exact2 teaches a pattern and then charges another repair step for following it.

**D5 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** Concept retrieval is useful, but an answer followed by a recipe read is still two lookups. Return the complete minimal idiom, batch related questions, and replace overlapping required reading. Also distinguish choosing a value from switching panels: the Water Tracker and Tip Splitter graders already flag presets marked as tabs. “Two to four choices” is insufficient semantic guidance.

**D6 — RIGHT.** Both [Water Tracker](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r6/grades/water-tracker/grade.md) and [Focus Timer](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r6/grades/focus-timer/grade.md) encountered the fake-clock failure. Compatibility with ordinary browser testing reduces verification friction. Keep the repair bounded to runtime interoperability while retaining Exact2’s clock and source restrictions. Its demonstrated impact is smaller than D3’s.

**The missing quality lever is content composition and state communication.** D1 directly addresses Contacts, but the other examples do not establish the same cause. Habit Tracker already has a wide composition; [Event RSVP’s screenshot](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r6/grades/event-rsvp/A-01-desktop-initial.png) already shows list and detail side by side. Its grader emphasizes Full/Cancelled clarity and enabled actions, not absent columns. Water Tracker needs its ring/actions beside useful history; moving navigation cannot create that. Improve compact dashboard compositions and reusable status/disabled-action treatments in the existing starters. The broad exclusion of comparison “polish” risks excluding meaningful usability improvements alongside decoration.

**The larger token lever is eliminating authoring and verification turns.** The [round-6 findings](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r6/findings.md) show recipe cuts displaced reading elsewhere: total docs fell only 4%, and the React ratio barely moved. Supply the selected starter with only its necessary idioms, and consolidate the existing compile/test/drive workflow into fewer calls with concise results. Reuse authored scenarios for bounded real-clock, persistence and layout verification instead of making builders invent additional flows. Remove recurring control/dialog surprises at their source. Measure total turns, carried context, repair steps and final quality—not vocabulary-response bytes alone. D4 mainly protects quality; D5 alone is unlikely to close 1.59×.

**One concrete omission is development-storage isolation.** [Contacts’ diary](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r6/apps/contacts-exact2/DIARY.md) reports about five minutes diagnosing foreign stored data; Water Tracker reports persisted settings leaking across named stores. Reproduce and resolve those inconsistencies: trustworthy verification saves both time and repeated context.

Required changes: constrain and explicitly reconcile D1; correct D4’s contradictory guidance; broaden D5 to remove steps; include content composition and storage isolation. The principal risks are overriding authored layout, adding noisy aesthetic warnings, and expanding the learning/checking burden faster than the fixes reduce it.
