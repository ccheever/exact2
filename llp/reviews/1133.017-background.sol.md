---

1. **MATERIAL — §4 D6, lines 382–385: the first-unlock gate rejects ordinary locked-phone wakes.** `isProtectedDataAvailable` becomes false when a protected device is locked, including after its first unlock. The proposed check therefore prevents background work during normal locked operation. **Decision needed:** gate on availability of the credentials and files actually required, with appropriate protection classes, rather than treating this property as a first-unlock predicate. [Apple documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiapplication/isprotecteddataavailable)

2. **MATERIAL — §4 D8, line 480 and lines 514–520: the processing requests overwrite one another.** The host registers one `.processing` identifier but submits separate requests for different constraint pairs. Submitting another pending request with the same identifier replaces the previous request; the ten-task allowance does not provide ten independent requests under one identifier. **Decision needed:** predeclare identifiers for constraint classes, or define a single multiplexed request and its constraint policy. [Apple submission semantics](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/backgroundtasks/bgtaskscheduler/submit%28_%3A%29)

3. **MATERIAL — §4 D10, lines 582–588; D6, lines 391–400: completion acknowledgment precedes durable handler effects.** D10 retires the transfer record when its invocation completes, while D6 waits separately for unawaited storage, which LLP 1113 D4 excludes from invocation ownership. A handler can start a write, return, and lose both that write and its redelivery opportunity if the process dies after record retirement. **Decision needed:** acknowledge consumption after the relevant storage durability barrier, or require an explicit durable consumer acknowledgment.

4. **MATERIAL — §3 D3, lines 275–280; §4 D10, lines 552–588: durable recovery depends on purgeable response files.** The default response path is `app:/tmp/background-transfer/…`, which Exact maps beneath `Library/Caches`. The durable record preserves only the path. The system may purge these files while the app is absent, leaving a replayed completion without its response—including Harmony’s upload receipt. **Decision needed:** retain unconsumed response bodies in nonpurgeable transfer storage until acknowledgment, with defined cleanup and handoff. [Apple file-storage guidance](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/FileManagement/Conceptual/FileSystemProgrammingGuide/FileSystemOverview/FileSystemOverview.html)

5. **MATERIAL — §4 D5, lines 353–355; D7, lines 458–463; D8, lines 486–492: invocation supersession has no disposition.** LLP 1113 D4 assumes serialized invocation owners and defines replacement of owned work by foreground work as `superseded`. This RFC permits multiple wake invocations and foreground input during them, but exposes only `completed` and `expired`. Two handlers refreshing the same resource, or foreground navigation replacing its request, can produce the missing outcome. **Decision needed:** define invocation scheduling and supersession, including whether registrations and durable transfer records remain pending for redelivery.

6. **MATERIAL — §11, lines 1087–1098; §4 D10, lines 552–558: photo completion loses its initiating account and conversation.** The consumer starts the transfer, writes no intent, then calls `attachPhoto(persona, room, …)` using current UI state. Harmony’s persona and room are mutable; switching either during upload can place the photo under the wrong account or conversation. The durable transfer record cannot recover the original destination. **Decision needed:** persist an outgoing-photo intent keyed by registration id before submission, and resolve it on completion. Acceptance must exercise navigation, persona switching and cold completion.

7. **MATERIAL — §2 D1, lines 171–178 and 195–199; D2, line 228; §11, lines 1053–1057: the prescribed retry path can spin while connectivity remains available.** Harmony re-registers whenever synchronization fails, and registration fires immediately while the network path reports online. A server outage can therefore cause repeated completed invocations without reaching `lastChance`, which counts expiries only. The web specification distinguishes immediate re-registration from its delayed failure-retry path. **Decision needed:** define failure retry eligibility/backoff and its interaction with re-registration and the wake budget. [Background Sync algorithms](https://wicg.github.io/background-sync/spec/#sync-event)

8. **MATERIAL — §3 D3, line 274; D4, lines 321–324; §13, lines 1199–1202: some supported Android transfers cannot make progress.** Transfers have no framework size cap, but all transfers below API 34 and those without person origin use ordinary WorkManager. Workers stop after ten minutes, and interrupted requests restart from byte zero; long-running workers and resumable transfers are excluded. A sufficiently slow transfer can retry indefinitely without advancing. **Decision needed:** declare a bounded duration and terminal-failure contract, or admit a carrier/resumption mechanism capable of completing these transfers. [WorkManager execution limit](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/work/Worker)

VERDICT: NOT READY

### Dispositions (r2)

1. MATERIAL, `isProtectedDataAvailable` is false whenever locked: FOLDED at D6. The first-unlock test is a Keychain read of the snapshot and of a host marker item, both `AfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`; `errSecInteractionNotAllowed` ends the wake without booting. The property is named as not the test.
2. MATERIAL, processing requests overwrite one another: FOLDED at D8 and D14. One pending request per identifier, so a fixed identifier per constraint class (`<bundle id>.processing.network`, `.processing.network-power`), each multiplexed and due at its soonest request; resubmission on entering the background and at the end of each wake.
3. MATERIAL, retirement before the handler's storage is durable: FOLDED at D10 with the transfer half of grok 7. An ended registration stays until the app calls `retireBackgroundFetch(id=)`; its event is delivered again at each later wake or launch (at most three times) until then. Harmony retires only after its write is admitted (§11, drive 7).
4. MATERIAL, response files in purgeable `app:/tmp`: FOLDED at D3. Unsaid, a response body stays in the host's non-purgeable transfer directory, read as `app:/transfers/<id>/<index>` until retirement; an upload body is copied into the host's custody before `backgroundFetch` answers.
5. MATERIAL, supersession has no disposition: FOLDED at D5. Invocations are delivered one at a time; a commit outside the invocation that replaces its owned work ends it `superseded` (LLP 1113 D4), dispositioned per kind (a `sync` stays pending and counts as an attempt; a `periodicsync` counts as run; a transfer is delivered again). Wakes are serialized per session (grok 9).
6. MATERIAL, the photo loses its account and room: FOLDED at §11. An outgoing photo (persona, room, reply) is written to `storage.kv` under the registration's id before the transfer starts, and `attachPhoto(r)` resolves it by id; drive 6 switches persona and room before a cold completion.
7. MATERIAL, retry spins while online: FOLDED at D1. A re-registered tag waits a backoff (5 minutes doubling to an hour) and then fires at the next chance, never at once; every attempt that does not retire counts towards `lastChance`.
8. MATERIAL, Android transfers that cannot finish: FOLDED at D4. A person's foreground start is a user-initiated job on API 34 and a WorkManager long-running worker (a foreground service of type `dataSync`) on 31–33; ordinary work that is stopped three times without finishing fails `fetch-error` with `android.stopReason`.

1. **IN-DELTA — MATERIAL — The three-delivery ceiling strands successful photos after temporary consumer failures.**  
   **D10, lines 624–632; §11, lines 1185–1194.** After three failed handler deliveries, later launches permanently suppress the completion event. Harmony invokes `attachPhoto` only from that event; its retry button handles `backgroundfetchfail`, whereas this registration remains a successful transfer. Three storage failures can therefore leave an uploaded photo permanently unsent even after storage recovers. Retaining the record prevents deletion but does not make the prescribed consumer recover.  
   **Decision needed:** require reconciliation of ended, unretired registrations, provide a consumer retry mechanism, or continue launch delivery until acknowledgment.

2. **IN-DELTA — MATERIAL — Early carrier reconstruction cannot run the promised configuration hatch.**  
   **D8, lines 547 and 565–578; D10, lines 639–656; D13, line 802.** R2 constructs the host’s `URLSession` synchronously before loading the app module. The retained hatch promises app Swift access to its configuration before construction, with identical configuration on every launch. That Swift resides in the deferred module. The specified ordering therefore cannot reproduce a customized configuration through its promised hatch. Apple requires configuration consistency when reconnecting background sessions. [Apple documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiapplicationdelegate/application(_:handleeventsforbackgroundurlsession:completionhandler:))  
   **Decision needed:** allow an explicitly early native-hatch load, or define a persisted, restricted configuration contract the host can reconstruct independently.

3. **PRE-EXISTING — MATERIAL — Harmony’s outbox wake still loses the initiating persona.**  
   **§11, lines 1141–1147.** Every account shares the literal `"outbox"` tag, whose handler refreshes the current snapshot. Harmony persists the selected persona (`apps/harmony/app/app.contract`, lines 155 and 186), and `snapshot` syncs only that persona’s partition (`apps/harmony/app/app.ts`, lines 166–168). Send offline as A, switch to B, then receive the wake: B syncs successfully and the registration retires while A’s message remains unsent.  
   **Decision needed:** durably track and drain pending personas, or use persona-specific tags whose handlers open the named partition. Acceptance should include switching accounts before a cold outbox wake.

4. **PRE-EXISTING — MATERIAL — One background session cannot honor per-request `isDiscretionary`.**  
   **D4, line 352; D12, line 749.** The carrier mandates one background `URLSession` per app, but tier 2 promises `isDiscretionary` per request. Apple defines this policy on `URLSessionConfiguration`; the session copies that configuration and ignores subsequent mutations. Two foreground transfers therefore cannot independently choose discretionary and immediate scheduling within the prescribed carrier. [Apple policy documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/urlsessionconfiguration/isdiscretionary), [configuration lifecycle](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/urlsessionconfiguration)  
   **Decision needed:** make this policy session-wide, or permit separate stable carrier identifiers for distinct configurations.

1. **PRE-EXISTING — MATERIAL — Photo completion has no durable handoff to message delivery.**  
   **§11, lines 1205–1215; D6, lines 447–470.** `attachPhoto` admits the message to the local outbox and retires the transfer, but prescribes neither a subsequent sync nor an outbox registration. Snapback’s `write` explicitly keeps the write locally; sending requires a later `sync()` (`snapback4/ts/snapback.ts`, lines 690–723).

   A cold boot’s snapshot round precedes attachment. After attachment, the headless session can end with no timer, stream or pending sync registration. If persona B is selected, refreshing its snapshot also cannot drain initiating persona A’s new message. Boot reconciliation repairs local attachment, not delivery. Thus the upload can finish successfully while the promised photo message remains unsent until A is opened again.

   **Decision needed:** define a durable handoff to the initiating persona’s outbox sync after admission, including retry registration if delivery fails, before discarding the transfer’s recovery opportunity. Acceptance should verify receipt on another device after a cold completion with a different persona selected.

2. **IN-DELTA — MATERIAL — Fixing reconstruction by forbidding configuration creates an unapproved framework ceiling.**  
   **D13, line 818; §6, line 841.** R3 permanently excludes session configuration choices, including the resource timeout. This contradicts accepted LLP 1133’s ceiling requirement, lines 30–34, and its explicit provision for construction-time choices, lines 217–222.

   The early-loading constraint explains why the deferred Swift hatch cannot work. It does not establish that stable, host-reconstructible configuration is infeasible. The task hatch cannot replace session-level policy: Apple specifically uses `timeoutIntervalForResource` to bound background transfer and retry duration. [Apple documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/urlsessionconfiguration/timeoutintervalforrequest?changes=_3)

   **Decision needed:** provide reconstructible configuration choices, or explicitly obtain an exception to the parent’s ceiling requirement. Removing the impossible hatch resolves the earlier defect; silently narrowing the architecture does not satisfy the parent.

3. **IN-DELTA — MATERIAL — The new delayed retry carrier cannot retain `android.expedited`.**  
   **D1, lines 183–186; D12, line 801.** Re-registration now requires a new `OneTimeWorkRequest` with a positive `setInitialDelay`, while `registerSync` still accepts `expedited`. Re-registering an expedited sync therefore requests a combination WorkManager rejects: its builder explicitly prohibits delayed expedited jobs. [AndroidX implementation](https://github.com/androidx/androidx/blob/androidx-main/work/work-runtime/src/main/java/androidx/work/WorkRequest.kt#L282-L286)

   **Decision needed:** specify that delayed retries become ordinary work, introduce a delayed eligibility step before expedited execution, or explicitly refuse the combination. Otherwise the prescribed retry path cannot preserve the declared options.

4. **PRE-EXISTING — MINOR — Unchanged summaries still prescribe superseded behavior.**  
   **Summary, lines 41–44 and 62–65; D8, line 557; D15, lines 992–994.** These passages retain current-persona handlers, the configuration hatch, one transfer session, and a three-delivery ceiling across launches. The detailed revised decisions are clear enough to resolve the earlier findings, but these competing instructions should be aligned with them.

VERDICT: NOT READY
