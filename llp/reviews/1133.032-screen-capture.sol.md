1. **The web substitute can open real screen capture — MATERIAL**

   **Where:** [§8, lines 762–766](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ccheever-projects-exact2/f4b5c629-4ed6-4746-9045-74f6106a629d/scratchpad/sc/r1/capsule/llp/1133.032-screen-capture.rfc.md:762).

   The unbound substitute runs self capture through the real browser API. Yet D3, lines 365–369, says Firefox and Safari ignore `preferCurrentTab` and show their normal picker. An agent-driven call can therefore open a real picker and, if answered, capture a real screen, contradicting §8’s guarantee. Chrome also documents `preferCurrentTab` as a suggestion, not a restriction. [Chrome documentation](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/element-capture#self-capture_considerations)

   **Fix:** generate synthetic frames for unbound browser capture. Reserve real own-tab capture for explicitly bound fixture drives with an enforced own-tab boundary.

2. **Android’s single-app picker prevents the recorder from starting — MATERIAL**

   **Where:** [D4, lines 412–415](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ccheever-projects-exact2/f4b5c629-4ed6-4746-9045-74f6106a629d/scratchpad/sc/r1/capsule/llp/1133.032-screen-capture.rfc.md:412), and §9.3, lines 835–841.

   The recorder starts after `getDisplayMedia` returns, but retains `foreground: true`. Android’s single-app picker launches the selected app before returning consent. [AOSP’s implementation](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base.git/+/eb18a6911de6949915fedf12738ee7754ccc2579/packages/SystemUI/src/com/android/systemui/mediaprojection/appselector/MediaProjectionAppSelectorActivity.kt) explicitly waits for that launch.

   Exact marks its activity hidden on stop (`ExactActivity.kt:457–461`), and [admission rejects foreground-only calls while hidden](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ccheever-projects-exact2/f4b5c629-4ed6-4746-9045-74f6106a629d/scratchpad/sc/r1/capsule/runner/src/runner/modules/admit.rs:536). Consequently, the continuation cannot start recording while the chosen app is in front.

   **Fix:** permit attaching a recorder to an already authorized live display track while backgrounded, retaining the capture-start origin checks. Add a bound single-app-selection drive; default-display capture misses this transition.

3. **Disabling video preserves the picture instead of blanking it — MATERIAL**

   **Where:** [D4, lines 373–377](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ccheever-projects-exact2/f4b5c629-4ed6-4746-9045-74f6106a629d/scratchpad/sc/r1/capsule/llp/1133.032-screen-capture.rfc.md:373).

   `setTrackEnabled(false)` explicitly sends no frames and leaves the recorder showing its last picture. Disabled video tracks must produce black content; MediaStream Recording explicitly records black during that interval. [Media Capture](https://w3c.github.io/mediacapture-main/#media-flow), [MediaStream Recording](https://w3c.github.io/mediacapture-record/#dom-mediarecorder-start)

   This changes the meaning of the shared disable control and produces different recordings across native and web targets. A previously sensitive picture remains visible throughout the disabled interval.

   **Fix:** distinguish an unchanged source from a disabled track. Disabling must replace its output with black until re-enabled.

4. **Windows’ programmatic-capture requirement is overstated — MINOR**

   **Where:** [D12, line 716](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ccheever-projects-exact2/f4b5c629-4ed6-4746-9045-74f6106a629d/scratchpad/sc/r1/capsule/llp/1133.032-screen-capture.rfc.md:716).

   Named-window capture does not universally require `graphicsCaptureProgrammatic` or packaged identity. That requirement applies to `TryCreateFromWindowId`; Win32 has `IGraphicsCaptureItemInterop::CreateForWindow`, already named in §9.5. [Microsoft’s Win32 sample](https://github.com/microsoft/Windows.UI.Composition-Win32-Samples/blob/master/cpp/ScreenCaptureforHWND/README.md), [WindowId requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/uwp/api/windows.graphics.capture.graphicscaptureitem.trycreatefromwindowid?view=winrt-26100)

   **Fix:** distinguish those API paths. The independent reasons for deferring Windows remain valid.
