DO NOT LAND

1. **BLOCKING — cancellation bypasses activation and can leak delegates.** [darwin_task.mm:303](/Users/admin/x2p2/apps/exact2-http/vendor/ibex/crates/ibex2/src/transport/darwin_task.mm:303) cancels suspended tasks immediately. A local Foundation probe delivered `metrics, end` without any resume, consistent with [Apple’s cancellation contract](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/urlsessiontask/cancel%28%29). Cleanup can therefore precede activation; [line 287](/Users/admin/x2p2/apps/exact2-http/vendor/ibex/crates/ibex2/src/transport/darwin_task.mm:287) subsequently re-adds the finished delegate to `keep`, creating a retain cycle with no remaining cleanup. Synchronize activation/cancellation, defer preactivation sink delivery, and establish retention before callbacks become possible. Test cancellation and lane destruction before activation.

2. **MATERIAL — an already-aborted request is resumed before cancellation.** [fetch_async.rs:364](/Users/admin/x2p2/apps/exact2-http/vendor/ibex/crates/ibex2/src/stdlib/fetch_async.rs:364) activates even when `wanted == false`, then cancels. An abort during `transport.start` can consequently send a POST after its aborted outcome was published. Cancel unwanted exchanges before activating them; native activation must complete a pending cancellation without resuming network work. Extend the scripted test to assert ordering and zero sends, rather than merely counting both calls.

3. **MINOR — resource-release regressions remain insufficiently pinned.** [fetch_async_tests.rs:478](/Users/admin/x2p2/apps/exact2-http/vendor/ibex/crates/ibex2/src/stdlib/fetch_async_tests.rs:478) exercises upload-release reentrancy only on grant refusal; [darwin_async_tests.rs:221](/Users/admin/x2p2/apps/exact2-http/vendor/ibex/crates/ibex2/src/transport/darwin_async_tests.rs:221) drops the exchange before checking release. Add tests retaining `FetchTask` through stop/end, reentering from exchange destruction, and checking exactly-once sink release for late metrics and expiry.
