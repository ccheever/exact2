## Round 1 (b9d59f7e6)

1. **Must-fix** — `host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/GroupedListIOS.swift:276`. On iOS 27, `contentInsets.top` on a list section with a supplementary header is the gap between the header and the rows, not the space above the header. Setting it to 20 leaves the header at y=0 and moves the first cell from 40.33 to 60.33; `supplementariesFollowContentInsets = true` does not change that. The clone's `TitledSection` (`section margin-top=20 margin-bottom=0` plus a `header`) therefore glues the title to the previous card and opens 20 points under the title. The web's margin is outside the section, above the header. A footer has the same shape: `contentInsets.bottom = 20` on a section that has a footer inserts those 20 points between the rows and the footer, and the next header sits flush under the footer. `margin-bottom=0` happens to match UIKit's 0, so `NotedSection` does not show it. Headerless sections do: there the top inset is above the cells, and `margin-top=20` / `margin-bottom=0` is a real 20-point gap (UIKit's own headerless insets are top 35 then 17.5, bottom 17.5). Fix: when the section has a header, put `margin-top` in `UICollectionLayoutListConfiguration.headerTopPadding` (padding 20 moves that header to y=20 and the cell follows) and leave `contentInsets.top` alone. Keep a footer's `margin-bottom` out of the cell-to-footer inset.

2. **Should-fix** — `host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/GroupedListIOS.swift:277`. "Within a point of UIKit's" holds for the sheet's other gaps and fails for the last footerless section. Measured inset-grouped insets, 402 points wide, three sections, no headers or footers: section 0 top 35 / bottom 17.5, middle top 17.5 / bottom 17.5, last top 17.5 / bottom **20**. The sheet writes 35.33 and 17.33. Those are within a point of 35 and 17.5, so they stay UIKit's. `|17.33 - 20| = 2.67`, so every default grouped or inset-grouped list's last footerless section has its bottom inset replaced with 17.33. Plain stays 0/0. A section with a header stays top 0. A 3-point tolerance cannot paper over this: the clone's `margin-top=20` is only 2.5 from the later section's 17.5 and must still apply. Fix: if the margin is the sheet's 17.33 and UIKit's inset is the last section's 20, keep 20.

3. **Should-fix** — `host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/NavigationRules.swift:88` and `host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/NavigationIOS.swift:611`. An all-zero velocity and translation is allowed, and this delegate is the direction filter (both pop recognizers are documented as horizontal pans; replacing the delegate is what enforces that). `shouldBegin` runs from `touchesMoved`, so a tap that never moves does not start a pop. A vertical drag whose first sample is `(0,0)` — the same reading that made HID back swipes fail — does. `panMayBegin` still refuses that sample past x=20 over a `swiperight` node, and `CanvasInput.owns` still refuses a canvas before this rule. What gets through: the leading 20 points (including the edge of a bubble), and on iOS 26 the content pop anywhere there is no `swiperight` (a pushed profile or details screen). Nothing looks again once the pop has begun, so the scroll never sees the drag and the pop cancels on lift. The travel fallback is right when translation is already non-zero (`velocity == .zero ? travel : velocity`, then `x > abs(y)`). Fix: add a target to both pop recognizers and, on the first non-zero sample, disable and re-enable the recognizer when the pan is not more horizontal than vertical.

4. **Should-fix** — tests miss the regressions above. `host/apple/tests/ExactKitTests/NavigationRulesTests.swift:212` locks in "all-zero is allowed" and never passes a zero velocity with a vertical translation through `gestureRecognizerShouldBegin`. `host/apple/tests/ExactKitTests/GesturePrecedenceIOSTests.swift:182` never checks that a swipe requires failure of only `interactivePopGestureRecognizer`, not another `UIScreenEdgePanGestureRecognizer` and not `interactiveContentPopGestureRecognizer` (the iOS 26 deadlock this commit is for). `contract/cli/tests/it/grouped_list.rs:478` checks the kernel tuple `(35.33, 17.33)` / `(20, 0)` and not the compositional insets, so the header landing under the title and the last section's 20→17.33 are invisible. `swipeDownX` (`host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/NodeViewIOS.swift:176`, used at `:145`) has no seam like `notePopTouchDown`. The edge-over-a-message, mid-bubble reply, and iOS 18 screen-edge paths are otherwise consistent: the swipe records the touch in `shouldReceive`, begins only at `start >= Gesture.edge` (20), and waits for that one pop recognizer (`host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/GesturePrecedenceIOS.swift:37`); a root or a hidden tab's pop still fails `popMayBegin` and releases the swipe; a modal stack is `modalNavigation ?? primaryNavigation`.

5. **Should-fix** — `llp/1084-native-grouped-lists.rfc.md` §6 does not record that iOS now reads section `margin-top` / `margin-bottom` (`kernel/src/grouped.rs:137`, `host/apple/src/abi/commands.rs:266`) and writes them into list insets, nor the one-point exception. That exception is a deviation from the web, which is the standard (`AGENTS.md`). `NavigationRules.panMayBegin`'s zero-vector allow and the swipe's failure dependency are likewise absent from LLP 1035.001 / 1057.001. `NodeViewIOS.swift` is 1491 lines, under the 1500 cap.

Verdict: LAND WITH FIXES

## Round 2 (0b1cc5970)

1. **Section spacing is fixed.** An authored boundary is the larger of the two margins (`authored_space` in `kernel/src/grouped.rs:293`). iOS puts that whole distance above the later section: `headerTopPadding` when that section has a header (`GroupedListIOS.swift:273`), otherwise `contentInsets.top` (`:282`). A footer’s bottom inset is left alone (`:283`), so it stays the row-to-footer gap. The previous footerless section’s bottom inset is cleared when the next section carries that space (`:284`), so the gap is not added twice. The UIKit test checks a header at y = 12 with its row gap unchanged, and 20 points from a footer to the next row (`GroupedListIOSTests.swift:311`).

2. **Sheet versus authored is fixed.** A margin counts as the sheet’s only when it is one of the values the sheet writes in that place (`grouped.rs:271`): 0 beside a header, a footer, or in a plain list; 17.33; 35.33 on top. Anything else is applied as written, including 0.5. The sheet’s 17.33 no longer replaces the last footerless section’s bottom inset of 20, because `space_below` stays `None` and iOS does not touch that inset (`GroupedListIOS.swift:285`).

3. **The zero-motion pop is soundly argued, not a remaining defect.** `gestureRecognizerShouldBegin` uses travel when velocity is `.zero` (`NavigationIOS.swift:611`), and `panMayBegin` allows a zero vector (`NavigationRules.swift:88`). A vertical sample with any translation is refused (`velocity == .zero ? travel : velocity`, then `x > abs(y)`). The only allow is both samples still zero. UIKit asks `shouldBegin` only after it has already accepted the pan as horizontal; the same HID setup that produced the zero sample for a back swipe scrolled, and did not pop, for vertical drags at x = 10 and x = 150. Nothing in this delegate starts a pop unless UIKit has already called `shouldBegin` for that drag.

4. **The tests cover the regressions that landed.** Kernel tests check the sheet/authored split and that 30 against 40 collapses to 40 on the web’s own frames (`grouped_list.rs:478`). `testASwipeWaitsForTheEdgePopOnly` requires failure of `interactivePopGestureRecognizer` only (`GesturePrecedenceIOSTests.swift:202`). The declined `swipeDownX` seam is the two lines that record and read it (`NodeViewIOS.swift:176` and `:145`); a touch that never moves does not reach `shouldBegin`.

5. **The docs match the code.** LLP 1084 §6.3, LLP 1057.001 §7, and `docs/contract-for-humans.md` record the authored space, the sheet exception, and the zero-vector rule.

No new defect in `git diff dfdbb1058 0b1cc5970`. The edge-pop failure dependency does not include the content pop (`GesturePrecedenceIOS.swift:37`), so the iOS 26 cycle stays broken. Past the 20-point edge, a `swiperight` row still refuses the pop in `panMayBegin` before the zero-vector allow.

Verdict: LAND
