# LLP 1125 r1, blind review: Muse Wonder Full 1.0, xhigh

> **Review provenance.** Family: Meta Muse Wonder Full 1.0 (xhigh) · Provider and runtime: `muse exec --model muse-wonder-full-1.0 --reasoning-effort xhigh --workspace <the worktree>` · Date: 2026-10-16 · Redacted: no · Method: blind (shown no other review), direction and fundamentals only, nits excluded; given LLP 1125 r1 at `404863b35` in a read-only detached worktree, with the euxy sources under `~/projects/euxy-src` and `~/projects/euxy-ports` readable. The text below is the reviewer's, unedited; the author's dispositions are in the RFC's §R.

**RIGHT DIRECTION WITH CHANGES** — a declarative, host-played transport plus MIDI belongs in core vocabulary (not 1124 extensions or per-app modules: D1's shared-clock and agent-record arguments are sound), but the transport's execution model, the horizon, clock-in determinism, and two of the small pieces need rework before a spec.

1. **BLOCKING — D3/D5, host-side expansion.** The host expanding the pattern creates a second producer into a mixer whose contract is single-producer ([sound_render.h](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-review-1125/host/apple/soundarm/sound_render.h) SPSC ring; `SoundArm.swift:218` "Every call is the main thread's, the mixer ring's one producer"), bypasses the voice table in [sound.rs](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-review-1125/runner/src/sound.rs:191) (so choke groups and the 32-voice bound between transport steps and `playSound` audition hits are undefined), and means four implementations of polymeter/anchor expansion (Rust runner, JS target mirror, Swift arm, web glue). Resolve: confront the unconsidered alternative in §6 — the runner expands *long* windows into the existing future-dated voice queue, refilling on a `timer_due` wake — or make the scheduler the mixer's sole producer and route one-shots through it, with cross-path group/bound rules stated.

2. **BLOCKING — D3, the 100 ms horizon.** A horizon equal to the 100 ms lookahead has zero slack for the 10 ms wake, the commit-to-arm drain, and §7's own 80 ms main-thread block. It is also contradicted by D4 (stop is *now*) and by LLP 1096, whose `End` ops already retract voices scheduled ahead ("Stop cancels the window already scheduled"). Resolve: define edits at commit time in the record and have hosts retract/replace not-yet-sounded steps (mixer `End`, CoreMIDI flush), accepting millisecond best-effort like a past `at=`; or set horizon = lookahead plus a proven slack. Determinism is a property of the record, not of what was heard.

3. **BLOCKING — D2, `sequence` on a gated task.** LLP 1092 D8/D11 gated tasks only arm or drop a timer; a sequence must re-evaluate, diff, and push `tracks/bpm/clock/output` on *every* commit (including 60 Hz playhead commits). That is the continuous reaction LLP 1017 P4c deferred, smuggled in as a "third schedule." Tasks are also root-only with exactly one schedule (`docs/contract-grammar.md:244–250`). Resolve: either name and cost the new reaction semantics (evaluation point, change detection) or adopt §9 Q2's fallback separate declaration.

4. **BLOCKING — D7/§4, clock-in determinism.** "Pure function of pattern and clock" holds only for the internal clock. External ticks are excluded from dispatch (D8), yet they move the anchor; arm tempo updates are new runner inputs outside LLP 1069.009 D1's tape, and the driver's *preferred-tempo* ticks fabricate a different history. Resolve: record tick/anchor updates as a (batched) runner call on the tape, or explicitly declare clock-following non-replayable and soften D3/D5.

5. **MATERIAL — D4, background behaviour contradicts the consumer.** Euxy's original is "deliberately NOT background audio" (`engine.ts:184`): on backgrounding it sends Stop, panics, and rewinds, because a follower left without Stop wedges (`engine.ts:198–235`). D4's "ambient resumes on the beat it would have reached" would reproduce that bug and sends no Stop before suspension. The locked-screen metronome is plausible only with sounding audio; a MIDI-only (silent-engine) sequence in the background is unproven. Resolve: clean background stop by default; background-continue as an opt-in proved on device, per output type.

6. **MATERIAL — D1/D7, "same instant" and the missing latency offset.** Euxy applies a user `latencyOffsetMs` to MIDI (`engine.ts:247`; `Engine.swift:303`); the RFC has no per-output offset, so sample-vs-MIDI skew is uncorrectable. Clock-in steps "fire at once" (callback plus buffer latency), so they are not sample-accurate. Euxy's note gate is also fixed *milliseconds* (`gateMs`), not D2's beats, which under an external clock the author's `fn` cannot convert. Resolve: add per-output offset (and an ms gate), scope accuracy claims by clock source, and make §7's bar measure audio-vs-MIDI skew.

7. **MATERIAL — D2/D5, finite playback and reactions.** Tracks loop forever and the transport never commits, so a rhythm game or interval timer (§2) cannot learn a song or phase ended, flip its gate, or score without polling via a frame task. The bake refusal "when both gates can hold together" asks for undecidable gate-overlap analysis, and euxy itself has two modes (jam/record, internal/external clock). Resolve: add coarse boundary signals (loop/end, not per-step) or drop those §2 claims; replace the bake check with a loud runtime rule and defined clock-mode switching.

8. **MATERIAL — D8, one commit per MIDI message.** Viable for steering and recording, not as written: pitch-bend/CC floods will saturate commits, and "drop the 257th" can drop the very note-off the no-coalescing rule exists to protect; in→commit→sound latency also makes §2's playable-instrument framing (piano tutor, soundboard) unrealistic. Resolve: coalesce continuous controllers latest-wins per channel/controller, never drop note on/off (shed continuous first), and state the playable-instrument limit. Define whether N mounted handlers mean N commits.

9. **MATERIAL — D9, on-demand decoding.** The arm reads every sound before engine start (`SoundArm.swift:51–105`); calling `exact_sound_set` mid-playback races the render callback's sound pointers, and "starts when decoded or is dropped" silently eats the first loop. Flipping the default regresses existing LLP 1096 apps' first hit. Resolve: keep eager decoding the default, make laziness opt-in per declaration beside `loadSounds`, and design the thread-safe buffer handoff.

10. **MATERIAL — D11, QR is the wrong layer.** Bit-identical output to npm `qrcode` serves port fidelity, not users — any ISO-valid code scans the same link. QR encoding is pure computation with no web name to ship (LLP 1115 does not apply), and the SwiftUI port's existing 228-line encoder proves it works app-side today. §6 never considers a data-module library, which needs no runner, JS-target, and Lean triplication or a perpetual Bun corpus against one JS library. Resolve: ship it as a distributed TS/Rust data library; only take the runner route with an argument for growing the pure-builtin roster, and then drop bit-identity.

11. **MATERIAL — D12, the element picture is unproven on both named hosts.** Web SVG `foreignObject` does not inline computed styles, declared fonts, or images, and has a long Safari canvas-taint history — this is the html2canvas problem, not a stage-1 "day or two." On Apple, "renders the element's layer" assumes a per-element view/layer that exact2's painted presenter does not generally provide. Resolve: spike the real euxy card in Chrome, Safari, and iOS before admission; stage `files=` for `app:/` paths (already designed in LLP 1069.003 D7) first, and prefer a kernel-painter rendering over per-host capture if the spike fails.

12. **MATERIAL — §7 staging and scope.** Stage 2 cannot delete `Engine.swift`'s scheduler as claimed: that scheduler fires MIDI and kit sound from the same tick (`engine.ts` `fireNote`), kits are off by default, so until stage 3 euxy would run two drifting clocks. Stage 2's honest consumer is a drums/metronome app, not euxy. Separately, twelve decisions across six subsystems with no implementer is one euxy shopping list, not one capability (RULES.md: a spec needs an implementer and a date). Resolve: stage 1 = `wakelock` + share-files only; sampler gaps independently; transport as its own RFC; transport+MIDI admitted together for euxy or with drums named as stage 2's consumer.

13. **MINOR — D10.** "Any mounted element" leaks in retained tab containers, where an off-screen recipe screen stays mounted — the leak D10 claims attributes cannot have. Define the hold as mounted *and presented*.

**§9 disagreements:** Q1 — the 2026-10-15 DEFERRED entry waives a take for enumerated *chrome* only; stages 1–2 need their own take or waiver, same as MIDI. Q2 — prefer the fallback declaration (finding 3). Q3 — no fixed horizon (finding 2). Q6 — eager by default, lazy opt-in (finding 9). Q7 — no runner QR at all (finding 10).
