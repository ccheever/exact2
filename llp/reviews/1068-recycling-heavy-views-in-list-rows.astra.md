## Review (verbatim)

Shell reuse is worth building. **r1 is not safe to implement unchanged.** This was a static review; I edited nothing and ran no builds or tests.

1. **BLOCKER — Rebinding needs an explicit lifetime boundary across asynchronous work.** Object identity stops being sufficient once objects survive rows. Video’s queued intrinsic-size and event deliveries check `owner.video === self`, then use the owner’s **current** id; an old callback can therefore affect the replacement row. Native callbacks resolve their nonce only after hopping to main: the proposed parked nonce can become active before a callback emitted while parked is delivered. Web’s document-start `exactAgent` message carries no generation at all. Require captured incarnation tokens, invalidation before reset, and activation only after the new props and identity are installed. Never activate a nonce previously used while parked. [VideoModule.swift:145](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/VideoModule.swift:145), [NativeModule.swift:130](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/NativeModule.swift:130), [WebArm.swift:266](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/webarm/WebArm.swift:266).

2. **BLOCKER — §5.1 changes policy; it is not an invisible implementation optimization.** HTML’s default is eager loading; lazy loading is intersection-based, not “wait until scrolling slows.” `preload="none"` neither removes the video element nor overrides autoplay. Delaying scripts, messages, playback and interaction until rest is observable. [HTML loading rules](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/urls-and-fetching.html#lazy-loading-attributes), [HTML media rules](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/media.html#attr-media-preload).

   D3 permits withholding a **known over-budget row**; it does not authorize withholding every iframe or native module, including cheap ones, under `complete`. Use D3’s measured cost and owed/pending rules, or ask Charlie to approve a separate deviation. Also define cancellation, latest-prop delivery, focus/hit behavior and settle integration. A video’s poster currently lives inside the arm being deferred; authored width or aspect ratio alone does not necessarily make both layout dimensions independent of metadata. [1050.000:21](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/llp/1050.000-choosing-the-fill-tradeoff.rfc.md:21), [1050.000:72](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/llp/1050.000-choosing-the-fill-tradeoff.rfc.md:72), [VideoArm.swift:35](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/videoarm/VideoArm.swift:35).

3. **MATERIAL — §4.0 is implementable, but the current representation cannot express it.** `Tree.views` is a complete preorder array; `take` zips it with every created id. Removing heavy entries without corresponding slot bookkeeping shifts subsequent claims onto the wrong nodes. `shape` also requires `container === view` and rejects private platform subviews: real scrollers, glass containers and material siblings fail before eligibility is checked.

   Store explicit holes or structural positions, walk authored children through their logical containers, and validate the **entire original subtree** before destroying anything—including heavy descendants that might survive or move. Once claims are correct, the existing create fallback and `children` insertion can rebuild the holes; no new children opcode is needed. “About 60 lines” understates the changes. [NodePoolIOS.swift:54](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/NodePoolIOS.swift:54), [NodePoolIOS.swift:117](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/NodePoolIOS.swift:117), [NodePoolIOS.swift:206](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/NodePoolIOS.swift:206), [PresenterIOS.swift:642](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/PresenterIOS.swift:642).

4. **MATERIAL — WKWebView: keep shell-only reuse until isolation is demonstrated.** “Stop loading, then navigate blank” is asynchronous navigation, not synchronous destruction of the old document. Hiding prevents pixels, not old script/audio/network activity before replacement commits. Missing reset state includes `suppliedDocument`, initialization flags, `servePending`, `serving`, `recovering`, `suppressLoad`, `guestFrame`, host `WebEntry` loading/source caches, and outstanding evaluation/snapshot replies. Old navigation failures can initiate recovery against the replacement mount. [WebArm.swift:57](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/webarm/WebArm.swift:57), [WebArm.swift:146](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/webarm/WebArm.swift:146), [WebArm.swift:321](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/webarm/WebArm.swift:321), [WebModule.swift:92](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/WebModule.swift:92).

   Q3 also misstates the oracle: a fresh iframe’s `history.length` is **not necessarily 1**; history includes the containing traversable’s entries. Guests can call `history.back()` themselves, so the difference is potentially behavioral, not merely an inspectable number. Audit traversal, retained documents and storage scope before declaring the complete deviation. Finally, hiding until `load` suppresses progressive rendering that a fresh iframe can show. [HTML History](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/nav-history-apis.html#the-history-interface).

5. **MATERIAL — GPU stage 2 is sound in principle; its visibility protocol is missing.** A fresh Exact surface instance can sequentially wrap the same live CAMetalLayer after the previous surface is dropped. This does **not** create a fresh device: the module owns that. The pinned Metal backend retains the layer, and configuring sets its device, format and drawable size; it does not reset `contentsScale`. Preserve the host’s scale update. [gpu/src/native.rs:170](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/gpu/src/native.rs:170), [GpuIOS.swift:212](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/GpuIOS.swift:212), [wgpu Metal surface:46](/Users/ccheever/.cargo/registry/src/index.crates.io-1949cf8c6b5b557f/wgpu-hal-30.0.1/src/metal/surface.rs:46), [configure:248](/Users/ccheever/.cargo/registry/src/index.crates.io-1949cf8c6b5b557f/wgpu-hal-30.0.1/src/metal/surface.rs:248).

   But `gpu_render` reports “wants another frame,” not “new pixels presented”: timeout/occlusion also returns that result. Specify a generation-specific successful-render/presentation signal, handling of outstanding old presents, and an unhide mechanism that does not wait indefinitely for a hidden drawable to become visible. A readback screenshot alone cannot prove the onscreen layer never flashed old content. [gpu/src/lib.rs:1129](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/gpu/src/lib.rs:1129), [gpu/src/native.rs:266](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-poolllp/gpu/src/native.rs:266).

   **Stage 3 needs more than a pointer cache.** Release targets on ordinary unpooled destruction, refusal, failed creation and session reset—not just parked eviction. Track owning module, exclusive use, device generation and full configuration; invalidate/reconfigure after device loss or another artifact’s use, even at unchanged size. Current recovery visits instances, so parked targets would be missed. 12. **MATERIAL — §3 supports experiments, not the stated conclusions.** The ordering is plausible, but the compared work differs: constructing player+item+layer versus moving a player omits the proposed reset/rebind cost; raw Metal omits wgpu; `didFinish` is not first visible content.    Measure complete retire→reset→take→first-correct-pixel cycles on device, interleaving fresh/reused cases, same/different sources and sizes, cold/warm caches, and real arm configurations. Report p95/p99 hitches, hit rate, settle latency, pending-content area-time and stale pixels. Count deferred leaves as missing content even when their background avoids the existing blank detector.
