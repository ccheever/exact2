## 3. The amendments against the code

**A1, the bound (L205–210).** Sound against the Metal path: the scene is already encoded on the host thread (`metal.rs` 539–617) with the commit sent to the worker (189–210), so acquiring the drawable on main and keeping commit, present and completion on the worker is D11's division unchanged. Two things the text must add:

- *The parity gate needs `framebufferOnly = false`* (L221–224). The layer is `framebufferOnly` (`apple.rs` 52); a texture so marked cannot be a blit source or be sampled, so "copies the presented drawable on the GPU into a readback texture" is refused by Metal as written. Test builds must set it false, and the RFC must say so and that the gate then qualifies that layer configuration, not the shipped one.
- *Formats (L209–211) and Q6 (L302–305) describe a cost already paid.* `attach_layer` builds `setup::pipelines(&device, BGRA8Unorm)` today (`present.rs` 198), which returns both `blend` and `replace` for that format (`setup.rs` 38–43, 68–71) into a separate `bgra.bin` archive (98–104), and adds it to `shaders_ms` (199). The present pass keeps only `replace` (215). A1 needs to keep `blend` from the same call; nothing new is built or archived. Q6 is moot and should go; 0b's phase already includes this set.

Smaller: the retained scene (L212–218) should name the clear (`flush` consumes `draw_clear`, `effects.rs` 19), and say that a D2.5 frame needs no re-render, since its compose target is a readable `Shared` texture (`metal.rs` 752) and today's `finish()` reads it (`ink.rs` 642–667); only a frame rendered into the drawable needs the re-render. Say how the live-resize sequence of D7 (L476; `present.rs` 309–314) runs once the drawable is acquired on main (it simplifies: encode, commit, `waitUntilScheduled`, present, all on main). The pre-scan's cost is unnamed (a per-frame tree walk for `backdrop-filter`, `mix-blend-mode` and islands' modes, `effects.rs` 64–68, unless the kernel keeps a count).

**A2 (L228–237, 315–318) does not determine the implementation, and the one it most naturally names is wrong.** The code's per-draw offset is the row's absolute device origin, `[origin.0 * scale, origin.1 * scale]` (`ink/rows.rs` 312), and instances are stored relative to the *unrounded* recording origin (`ink.rs` 122–126; `rows.rs` 54–56). Rounding the replay offset alone gives a shift of `round(o_new·s) − o_rec·s`, fractional whenever the row was recorded at a fractional origin, which the exact re-record on settling guarantees. The glyph phases (`text.rs` 28–29; `swash.rs` 137, 245–272) are then wrong on every frame after a settled list moves again. What must be whole is the *shift between recording and replay*. Two further facts bear on the rule:

- The kernel disables Taffy's rounding (`kernel/src/layout.rs` 390–393: "Frames are CSS pixel geometry, not a host's raster grid"), so rows have fractional heights. Rounding per row therefore gives adjacent rows different sub-pixel residues, and the sub-pixel gap or overlap between their backgrounds changes frame to frame: a shimmer at every row boundary during a fling, which a hand-built AppKit list, whose clip view keeps one pixel-aligned bounds origin for all its content, does not show.
- The rule that is one line and avoids both: snap the *scroller's* offset, `r = round(s) − s` with `s` the scroller's offset in device pixels (the backend already receives it, `paint/rows.rs` 296–297), and record and replay every row of that scroller at its origin plus `r`. The shift between frames is then `round(s_new) − round(s_rec)`, whole for every row; rows keep their mutual positions; the whole list sits at most half a device pixel from layout while `s` is fractional. A row re-recorded exactly on settling is re-recorded once at the snapped origin when motion resumes (one walk of the visible rows per gesture start). Q5's claim is true of this rule.

Pre-existing, not A2's fault, but the test's assertion (L234–235) must carve it out: `subpixel` and `trunc` are not translation-invariant across zero (`swash.rs` 249–271; `text.rs` 29), so a glyph whose device coordinate changes sign (a descender straddling the viewport's top edge) differs from a fresh walk by a pixel under any whole-pixel replay, today's included.

"Pumped once per display period while the display link runs" reads two ways: a pass at every link tick (`Event::Tick`, 335–348) in addition to `draw`'s own, or `draw`'s pass as the period's pass with `about_to_wait`'s skipped while the link runs.
