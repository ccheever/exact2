### HIGH

1. **Apple teardown leaves a live WebArm holding a dangling callback context.** `WKUserContentController` strongly retains registered handlers; because `WebArm` owns the controller and registers `self`, the cleanup in `deinit` cannot break that retain cycle. Destroy merely removes the manager entry and releases the original retained handle, while the callback was passed into the arm unretained. The detached WKWebView can therefore continue loading or messaging and invoke `takeUnretainedValue()` on a freed `WebCallbackBox`, causing a crash after leaving/re-entering the screen or destroying mid-load. Add an explicit arm `invalidate` operation that stops loading, removes both handlers, clears the delegate, and only then releases the handle; preferably register weak proxy handlers as well.
