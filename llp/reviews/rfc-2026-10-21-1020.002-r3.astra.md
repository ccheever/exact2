**New concerns**

1. **Material — Ordered popup rules bypass external-scheme confirmation.**  
   RFC:375 makes the first matching row authoritative, but the unrestricted tab rows at :383–384 precede the non-web-scheme row at :385. A person clicking `<a target="_blank" href="mailto:…">` therefore reaches `newwindow`, whose documented handler may call `openURL`, without the promised confirmation. Both cited implementations classify non-http(s) URLs **before** tab disposition (`T3BrowserSession+Links.swift:74`; oracle `apps/desktop/src/preview/Manager.ts:490`).

   **Resolve:** Restrict both tab rows to http(s), or classify schemes before disposition routing. Preserve refusal of prohibited schemes and confirmation for permitted external schemes.

2. **Material — The iOS capture formula handles scale but not a displaced viewport.**  
   RFC:498–508 promises that a `getBoundingClientRect()` rectangle can be converted using multiplication alone. After pinch zoom and pan, layout-viewport coordinates and the visible viewport’s origin differ; scaling alone cannot translate between them. The SDK explicitly says zooming adjusts **both offset and scale** (`iPhoneOS.sdk/.../UIKit.framework/Headers/UIScrollView.h:237`), while snapshots require view coordinates (`WKSnapshotConfiguration.h:37`). The cited consumer implementation is macOS-only (`T3BrowserAutomationInput.swift:1`).

   **Resolve:** Define layout-versus-visual-viewport coordinates and the translation into web-view bounds, including applicable insets. Extend the acceptance case to pinch zoom followed by pan, with a nonzero viewport offset—not just zoom 1.5.

3. **Minor — Crash recovery stops before its advertised third retry.**  
   RFC:260 schedules retries at 250, 500 and 1,000 ms, but :261 stops on the third process death. That permits only two retries. The consumer permits three attempts and refuses the next request (`T3BrowserSession.swift:435`, :441).

   **Resolve:** Stop after the third recovery attempt fails—normally the fourth process death—or explicitly choose two retries and remove the 1,000-ms attempt. Make the terminal `recovering: false` row override the generic crash row.
