2. **BLOCKING — Discarded upload buffers lose their charge before destruction.** [Upload ownership begins only at hop invocation](/Users/admin/x2p2/apps/exact2-core/host/apple/src/executor_async.rs:980). A timeout moves the unstarted closure into [trash](/Users/admin/x2p2/apps/exact2-core/host/apple/src/executor_async.rs:1291), then [releases its charge](/Users/admin/x2p2/apps/exact2-core/host/apple/src/executor_async.rs:1332), although that closure still owns the bytes. A blocked disposer permits repeated uploads to accumulate beyond the budget. Carry release accounting with the buffer through disposal; test timeout-before-first-hop with a blocked disposer.

3. **MATERIAL — Retirement can miss reader cancellation.** [Retirement wakes cleanup before firing abort](/Users/admin/x2p2/apps/exact2-core/host/apple/src/executor_core.rs:829). An active reader then continues reading and occupying its permit. Keep retirement cancellation registered for the reader’s lifetime, or synchronize abort before cleanup. Add a gated retirement-during-read test.
