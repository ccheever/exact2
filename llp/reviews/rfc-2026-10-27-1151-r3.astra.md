**Remaining fundamental issues**

1. **D4.1 still changes the meaning of delivered tests.** Its `clock settle` expansion models only landing and near-term tasks. The actual driver first sends the host a settle request, then handles those tasks. The web host advances to animation end times and runs the work due by then. A transition can therefore cause the real settle to fire an app timer that the proposed expansion never reaches. Translating only the written clock operations omits that behavior. [scripts/agent-test.mjs:842](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/scripts/agent-test.mjs:842), [scripts/agent-test.mjs:918](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/scripts/agent-test.mjs:918)

   **Smallest change:** keep `clock settle` unsupported in stage 1 and stop the prefix there. Preserve `clock data → land`. Expand the implicit mutation-clock advances into explicit replay events before counting an authored prefix as delivered.

2. **Queue-slot exclusion contradicts the frame predicate.** D1 defines `writes none` as no root slot changing, while D6 excludes queue mutation slots from frame suggestions. A queue’s first send can answer inside the sending action and change its mutation slot; queue answers do not exclusively land during advances.
