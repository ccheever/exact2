A mapper suspended across turns over a large READY holds the stream for seconds, by the issue's own numbers (fflate at 40 ms per inflated MB, 100 ms turns serialized with the app's other turns). Discord keeps sending during that time. On a busy account 256 frames arrive in seconds, the generation retires, Excord reconnects, READY arrives again, and the loop repeats.
