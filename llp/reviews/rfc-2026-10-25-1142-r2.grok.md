## Concerns

Separately, agent mode still zeros list velocity (`PresenterMac.swift:379`) and skips the post-sync pump (`:460`), and the wheel handler still `settlePump()`s before reply (`AgentMac.swift:717`). r2 never turns those off. On a 60 Hz link the example `wheel 0 -2000 fling` (100 ms of travel, then 0.95 decay from ~333 pt/frame until 0.5 pt, capped at 2 s) outlasts `live 1500`. r2 does not say whether the window extends, clips, or fails.
