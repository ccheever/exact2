# Code review: LLP 1080.000 real iOS taps, stages 0–1 (astra)

**Reviewer:** Astra (codex gpt-6-astra, xhigh), 2026-10-03, diff cd7d2533..5c1cc127

**Verdict:** DO NOT SHIP — activation can masquerade as platform input, and runner waits and ownership are unsafe.

1. **High — Scripted iframe clicks are falsely reported as platform touches.** [scripts/agent.mjs:674](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/scripts/agent.mjs:674) bypasses `realTap` when a selector or coordinates are supplied. An iframe then executes JavaScript `dispatchEvent` and returns no delivery classification ([WebModule.swift:349](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/WebModule.swift:349)). The new capability table supplies `platform` through `s.tap`’s fallback at lines 884 and 939. Thus `s.tap(frame, {selector: '#button'})` claims real input without any XCTest injection or dispatch-log confirmation. **Fix:** refuse unsupported press forms under platform mode before mutation, or implement their real coordinate resolution; only successful `realTap` results may claim platform delivery.

2. **High — A stalled touch runner can hang a drive indefinitely.** [host/apple/touches.mjs:129](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/touches.mjs:129) waits for a reply or socket closure without a deadline. Both foreground checking and injection await it; the one-second barrier starts only afterward. A live runner stuck in XCTest therefore prevents the CLI and smoke from reaching their cleanup. Even barrier reads use the app carrier’s 120-second timeout rather than the remaining one-second budget. **Fix:** bound each runner request and barrier read, invalidate the transport on timeout, reject pending requests, and tear down the recorded runner process.

3. **High — The device lock permits multiple simultaneous owners.** [host/apple/touches.mjs:75](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/touches.mjs:75) checks existence separately from an unconditional write, so two processes can both acquire an absent or stale lock. It also explicitly accepts an existing lock held by the current PID, allowing two library sessions in one process to start runners on the same device. Either session can subsequently remove the other’s lock. **Fix:** make acquisition and stale-owner takeover atomic, reject a second session even within the same process, and release by a unique ownership token after teardown completes.

4. **Medium — Inline targets are tapped at their paragraph’s center.** [TouchIOS.swift:111](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/TouchIOS.swift:111) computes `box(v).midX/midY`, but `view(req)` resolves inline IDs to their owning paragraph. The existing [Agent.swift:38](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/Agent.swift:38) deliberately resolves a visible shaped fragment instead. A requested link away from the paragraph center therefore activates another link or nothing, while the barrier accepts the paragraph hit. **Fix:** reuse `tapPoint` for the requested inline ID and refuse when no visible fragment exists.

5. **Medium — Stage-3 presentation cases are accepted instead of refused.** [TouchIOS.swift:108](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/TouchIOS.swift:108) refuses confirmation items and swipe actions, but admits substituted tab bars, navigation controls, and ordinary popover invokers. Agent mode still hides native tabs ([NavigationTabsIOS.swift:103](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/NavigationTabsIOS.swift:103)). Ordinary popovers require `menus.agentTap`, which real injection bypasses; their native overlays are omitted under agent mode ([MenusIOS.swift:109](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/MenusIOS.swift:109)). This permits misleading successes or nonfunctional menu taps. **Fix:** enforce D7’s stage-1 `unsupported` boundary for every substituted presentation until stage 3 lands.

6. **Medium — Authored tests silently ignore `--touch platform`.** The new flag is parsed at [scripts/agent-launch.mjs:42](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/scripts/agent-launch.mjs:42), but the `--test` branch at [scripts/agent.mjs:1257](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/scripts/agent.mjs:1257) does not forward it. Neither `runTests` nor its `open` call accepts it. Consequently, an explicitly requested real-touch test runs activation input and can pass without starting the runner. **Fix:** propagate `touch` through both calls, or explicitly reject this combination until supported.

7. **Medium — The dispatch barrier treats a truncated log as complete.** [host/apple/touches.mjs:173](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/touches.mjs:173) repeatedly reads from the original baseline and ignores `truncated`. [TouchIOS.swift:74](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/IOS/TouchIOS.swift:74) returns only the first 32 entries. An ending beyond that prefix is never observed; a second identity beyond a completed first touch can be hidden, incorrectly satisfying “exactly one touch.” **Fix:** refuse truncated evidence, or consume bounded pages using the last returned sequence and evaluate all entries before accepting.

8. **Low — Successful startup and teardown leave timeout timers alive.** The `Promise.race` timers at [host/apple/touches.mjs:116](/Users/admin/.config/tuft/sessions/1791014443.232549/worktrees/exact2-1080.000/host/apple/touches.mjs:116) and line 134 are never cleared when another branch wins. A programmatic drive that opens and closes promptly can remain running until the startup timer reaches 30 seconds. The smoke’s explicit `process.exit` masks this. **Fix:** clear both timers in `finally`, following the existing `waitAtMost` implementation.
## Disposition

Claude (Opus 5.5), 2026-10-03, on `tuft/1080.000`. All eight were taken, in
one round. Evidence is in `/tmp/exact-1080-touch/review/`, on the
`exact-1080-touch` simulator.

1. **Taken.** Under `--touch platform` a press is `realTap` or nothing. A press with any guest field (`selector`, `x`/`y`, `entity`, `world`, `under`) is refused before anything is sent: "unsupported under --touch platform: a press into an iframe guest or a world entity reaches no real touch yet" (`scripts/agent.mjs`, iOS `input`). Only `realTap`'s reply carries `platform` for a press. Evidence (`live.out`): `tap brand {selector}` and `tap brand {x,y}` both refused; a plain `tap brand` was `platform`.
2. **Taken.** Every runner request is bounded at 15 s (`RUNNER_MS`). On a timeout the transport is spent: pending requests are rejected, the socket is destroyed, and xcodebuild is stopped. That is a 3 s wait, then SIGTERM to its recorded PID only. Barrier reads are bounded by what remains of the one-second budget. Evidence (`live.out`):
   - The runner's XCTRunner was stopped with SIGSTOP. The tap failed after 15,034 ms with "did not answer foreground within 15 s; it was stopped".
   - xcodebuild was gone, and the lock was released.
   - The next tap failed by name, without hanging.
   - `barrier.out`: a log read that never answered was refused after 1,009 ms.
3. **Taken.** The lock is an exclusive create (`wx`) holding a `pid:random` token. Any live holder refuses, this process included. A dead holder's lock is taken over under an exclusive `.takeover` file and re-read before removal. Release removes the lock only while it still holds this session's token, and only after teardown. Evidence:
   - `lock.out`: a same-process holder was refused and its lock left intact; a dead PID's lock was taken over; a live other process was refused; 8 of 8 two-process races gave exactly one owner.
   - `live.out`: a second session in the same process was refused, and a lock overwritten by another owner survived `close()`.
4. **Taken.** `aim` uses `tapPoint` (`TouchIOS.swift`): a visible shaped fragment for an inline id, the request's point otherwise. If no fragment is visible, it refuses ("no visible text fragment; scroll it into view first"). Not driven live: Caltrain has no inline link. Verified by compilation and by the touch smoke still passing.
5. **Taken.** `Agent.substituted` refuses, as stage 3's, every presentation the agent substitutes:
   - a confirmation's action and a native swipe action, as before;
   - anything that goes through the painted popovers. That is an open popover, a node in or being one, or an invoker of one (`MenusIOS.agentPainted`);
   - the authored tablist in place of UIKit's tab bar (`adoptedTablist` is now kept under the agent; its two readers already check `!agentMode`);
   - the authored header in place of a navigation bar that would show (`stacks[…].showsBar`).

   Not driven live: Caltrain has none of these. Verified by compilation and the smoke.
6. **Taken, by refusal.** `agent.mjs … --test … --touch platform` refuses: "--test runs its own sessions, without real touches". Forwarding is left until real-touch tests are wanted.
7. **Taken.** The barrier pages through the log from the last sequence it read. While `truncated`, it keeps reading before judging, and it judges every entry it read. `lost` refuses. Evidence (`barrier.out`):
   - a second touch hidden past a full 32-entry page was found ("ambiguous: 2 touches");
   - an end on page 2 was accepted only after reading it (reads at 100, then 132);
   - a `lost` log was refused.
8. **Taken.** One helper, `within`, clears its timer in `finally`. It bounds startup, every runner request and the teardown waits. Evidence (`timers.out`): a drive that opened, tapped and closed exited the moment it closed (12,600 ms for both).
