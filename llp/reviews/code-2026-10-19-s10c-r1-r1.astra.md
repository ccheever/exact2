**Blocking (security or correctness)**

- [scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:107](/tmp/rv-r1-1/scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:107): WebPKI verification can be disabled through `NODE_TLS_REJECT_UNAUTHORIZED=0`. The connector omits `rejectUnauthorized: true` and sends its token on `secureConnect` without checking authorization. Explicitly enforce verification. [Bun’s TLS implementation](https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/blob/main/src/js/node/tls.ts) confirms this environment-dependent default.
- [scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:126](/tmp/rv-r1-1/scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:126): A relay can return `REFUSED <connector-token>` and expose the secret in logs and the rejection error. Refusal text also permits terminal control characters. Use controlled diagnostics and redact credentials.
- [scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:139](/tmp/rv-r1-1/scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:139): Every `CLIENT` triggers another outbound TLS connection **before admission**. Repeated `READY`/invalid-`CLIENT` responses cause continuous connection churn without backoff; the 64-socket guard does not bound that work. Bound total connections and throttle rejected handoffs.

**Should-fix**

- [scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:107](/tmp/rv-r1-1/scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:107): No deadline covers the outbound TLS handshake, `READY`, or a partial `CLIENT` header. Four stalled control connections can permanently occupy the pool; before `READY`, the readiness promise also remains pending.
- [scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:121](/tmp/rv-r1-1/scripts/tamber-relay.mjs:121): Complete lines are consumed before the 1,024-byte check, bypassing that limit. Names and reported addresses lack validation; CR/ESC characters reach the pairing log at [tamber-server.mjs:652](/tmp/rv-r1-1/scripts/tamber-server.mjs:652).
