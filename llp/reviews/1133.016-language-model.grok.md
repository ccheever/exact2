2. **MATERIAL** — An aborted or failed reply stays on screen and leaves the session’s transcript. Section D6 (lines 430–451), D1’s message (lines 192–195), §10 (lines 1058–1068), drive 3 (lines 1098–1099), Android background failure (lines 717–719).

   The transcript is the source of truth, and a later `promptStreaming` is a new user turn on that session. §10 creates the session once from history, which only works if each later input is the new message, not the whole history again (resending history would double the transcript and evict every turn). Drive 3 and the Android background rule keep the partial text in the UI. Nothing says that text, or the turn’s tool calls, is appended as the assistant turn. The web’s abort and Apple’s cancelled `streamResponse` do not commit a partial turn on their own. The next send is answered by a model that has not seen what the person sees.
