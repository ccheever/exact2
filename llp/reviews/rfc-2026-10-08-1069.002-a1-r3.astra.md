One blocker remains, plus two should-fix issues.

1. **Blocker — a committed image can still be reported as failed.** [storage.rs:110](/private/tmp/imgrv/wt-d3/js/src/storage.rs:110), [boundary_abi.rs:919](/private/tmp/imgrv/wt-d3/vendor/ibex/crates/ibex2/src/boundary_abi.rs:919), [task.rs:610](/private/tmp/imgrv/wt-d3/vendor/ibex/crates/ibex2/src/task.rs:610); A1.5/A1.9.

   The worker posts its completion and removes its gate, independently of the waiter’s timeout decision. This interleaving remains possible:

   - The waiter’s last readiness check returns false at its deadline.
   - The worker finishes the atomic replacement, queues success, and unregisters the gate.
   - `abandon_image_work()` finds nothing and returns `Nothing`.
   - The waiter reports failure; the prelude rejects with `failed` and ignores the subsequent successful completion.

   `to` has changed despite failure. The five-second `Written` retry has the same window at its next deadline. Queue-head registration and locking across write/sync/rename correctly protect the write itself; they do not coordinate the terminal outcome.

   **Fix:** retain an operation-specific terminal state until completion is acknowledged. Timeout must either revoke that operation before commit or deliver/wait for its committed result. An empty runtime-wide gate registry cannot establish that nothing was written. Test completion between the final readiness check and abandonment, including the five-second retry and overlapping residual work.

2. **Should-fix — a background timeout strands the storage queue.** [background.rs:86](/private/tmp/imgrv/wt-d3/js/src/background.rs:86), [background.rs:63](/private/tmp/imgrv/wt-d3/js/src/background.rs:63); A1.5.

   A background continuation uses the new abandonment mechanism, but its failure handler only sets `stalled` and returns an error. It never rejects the storage promise or calls `landed()`.

   Concrete case: an answer starts compression and another write, then replies. Compression exceeds 30 seconds and loses its write right. Its `.catch()` never runs, and the queued write never starts. Even the codec’s eventual completion remains undelivered until unrelated activity rearms background processing.

   **Fix:** settle the specific abandoned storage operation through a common failure path for awaited and background work, run its rejection reactions, advance the queue, and update background accounting.

3. **Should-fix — animated WebP dimensions use the wrong offsets.** [storage-image.js:114](/private/tmp/imgrv/wt-d3/host/web/storage-image.js:114); A1.5/A1.7.

   Relative to the `ANMF` chunk start, width and height begin at `+14` and `+17`. The implementation reads `+20` and `+23`: duration, then flags plus the following chunk’s FourCC bytes. The [libwebp parser](https://github.com/webmproject/libwebp/blob/main/src/demux/demux.c#L304) confirms the field order.

   I reproduced this entirely in memory with a valid, libwebp-validated 144-byte, 32×32 animation: `headerSize()` returns **101×5,264,899**, and compression rejects `too-large` without decoding.

   **Fix:** correct the offsets and add an actual animated `ANMF` fixture. The existing WebP tests cover standalone `VP8L` frames, not this branch.
