**Blocking**

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:330](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:330): **Reconnect recovery permits takeover by another run.** After expiry or relay restart, any connector on the account can claim the victim’s public names using a different `run`, receive its clients, and prevent its recovery. [The new test explicitly expects this]( scripts/tamber-relay-server.test.mjs:377). Bind reclaimed names to their original run.

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:206](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:206): **Unauthenticated connections remain uncapped.** Silent/partial hellos and greetings bypass both session limits and refusal counting. One IP can exhaust sockets and memory before deadlines expire. This part of round one remains open; bound concurrent pending connections globally and per IP.

**Should-fix**

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:145](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:145): At 100,000 keys, every new key triggers a full-map sweep. Worse, `refuse()` ignores `take()` failure, leaving new refusal IPs unrecorded: `peek()` stays zero and silence never begins. Saturation therefore weakens authentication throttling while amplifying CPU work.

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:483](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:483): Concurrent CLI operations share `accounts.json.tmp`. A revoke can validate and rename another invocation’s add snapshot, print success, and leave the revoked credential active. Serialize the complete account/ledger transaction.

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:213](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:213): The strict first-record boundary remains unfixed: entire incoming chunks, including coalesced later records, are consumed and copied. The [fuzz test]( scripts/tamber-relay-server.test.mjs:322) still trusts the parser’s reported `read`, rather than instrumenting accesses. Opaque forwarding does not prove the approved read boundary.

- [tamber-relay-gate.mjs:133](scripts/tamber-relay-gate.mjs:133): R4 still does not exercise either canvas gateway or cross-account cookie behavior there; it sends cookies only to the tool endpoint. Connector connections also bypass the capture at line 62, making the connector-token capture assertion ineffective.

- [tamber-relay-server.test.mjs:326](scripts/tamber-relay-server.test.mjs:326): The test claiming TXT revocation coverage never issues a TXT request. Pending DNS cancellation and TXT socket teardown remain untested despite being security-sensitive round-one fixes.

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:160](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:160): IPv6 masking splits compressed notation without expanding it. `2001:db8::1234` logs as `2001:db8::1234::`, preserving the entire address. Mask numeric address bits.
