3. **Blocking — TypeScript builds still associate compiled designs with the wrong source digest.** [host/web/dev.mjs:781](/private/tmp/rv-rt-3/host/web/dev.mjs:781)  
   `designBytes()` hashes disk when the producer reply arrives.
