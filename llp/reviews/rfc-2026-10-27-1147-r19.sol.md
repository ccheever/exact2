1. **The fixed corner pixel misclassifies partially visible iframes as offscreen.** [D7](/Users/admin/Developer/exact2-timer-wait-points/llp/1147-timers-that-may-wait.rfc.md:329) treats its nonintersection as stopped rendering and counts moving scrollers as empty. A corner pixel’s disappearance does not establish that the whole viewport disappeared.

By the specified geometry, D7 declares rendering stopped. An uncapped `wait=scrollend` timer becoming due during an already-observed fling inside that visible area can therefore run mid-fling.
