**Blocking**

- [scripts/tamber-acme.mjs:296](scripts/tamber-acme.mjs:296): **CAA can falsely report a pin.** `AccountURI=<ours>; ValidationMethods=dns-01` becomes accepted lowercase parameters here, but Let’s Encrypt recognizes the lowercase spellings only. Those mixed-case parameters leave issuance unrestricted, including TLS-ALPN-01. Match the CA’s parsing semantics. [Boulder implementation](https://github.com/letsencrypt/boulder/blob/main/va/caa.go#L455).

- [scripts/tamber-acme.mjs:294](scripts/tamber-acme.mjs:294), [docs/tamber-protocol.md:264](docs/tamber-protocol.md:264): **Issuer capabilities are assumed.** The validator ignores the issuer and accepts matching parameter text for any CA. CAA compliance alone does not establish support for these restrictions; require known support before reporting `caa: true`. Universal support becomes mandatory only **2027-03-15**. [RFC 8657 §5.2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8657.html#section-5.2), [Baseline Requirements](https://cabforum.org/working-groups/server/baseline-requirements/requirements/#42212-caa-parameters).

- [scripts/tamber-acme.mjs:213](scripts/tamber-acme.mjs:213): **Lock ownership race remains.** Two reclaimers can both finish the comparison; A unlinks and acquires a replacement, then B unlinks A’s replacement. A newly created, still-empty lock can also be mistaken for a dead holder. Both processes then enter issuance. Initialization and reclamation need atomic exclusion.

- [scripts/tamber-remote.mjs:516](scripts/tamber-remote.mjs:516): **Graceful shutdown does not await ACME cleanup.** Publishers receive no cancellation signal, and `close()` awaits only server shutdown. [tamber-server.mjs:1203](scripts/tamber-server.mjs:1203) subsequently calls `process.exit()`, potentially leaving the detached hook running and TXT records uncleared. Track and await issuance cleanup before resolving close.
