## Concerns

**BLOCKING — §2 and §4.7.** “Quick” and “cheap” are one paired run of one model. LLP 1116 §1 needed 24 pairs to report 0.79× minutes and 1.19× tokens, and said the comparison builders already know their stack. A port adds the bias `RESULTS.md` measured: Exact reused 3,182 lines of TypeScript (137 changed) and SwiftUI reused none, while cached input was 103.5 M against 41.6 M and fresh input was 0.72 M against 0.52 M. Two consecutive fresh ports shrink run noise and leave training, reuse and brief bias in place. A graduation on those bars would close Charlie’s token goal on evidence that cannot support it.

Resolve: record wall clock, fresh input, output, cache reads, calls and largest context, and do not graduate on them. A claim that a fix made builds cheaper goes to the authoring bench or the bake-off, as a paired interval (the standard LLP 1116.021’s reviews required), or to a task extracted from the diary and run at least five times per arm.

**BLOCKING — §2.** Runtime bars use the native port as the oracle. §1 and LLP 1115 say the oracle is a hand-built screen on the same OS. The native arm is a one-to-three-hour agent build; a weak one makes Exact look fast and light. The original is measured in §4.0 and then left out of the bar.

Resolve: where the original is native, first frame, UI-built, hitch ratio, steady-state CPU and footprint are ratios to the original. Where it is Expo or React, use the better of the original and the native port for runtime. The native port remains the authoring control only. Pass a timing bar only when the medians clear it and the p10–p90 bands do not overlap; if they overlap, take more runs. n=20 interleaved cold launches is enough: in `RESULTS.md` those bands were narrow and disjoint. Give UI-built the same 1.1× slack as first frame.

**MAJOR — §2 fidelity.** “At least as close as the native port” is satisfied by a sloppy native arm. Differing-region counts will fire on Dynamic Type, dark mode and legal platform metrics. LLP 1115 §4 limits Chrome parity to layout given the same inputs, and the brief already forbids sub-point chasing. The release iOS dev menu (LLP 1115 D9) identifies Exact to a grader who has scored it for nineteen rounds.

Resolve: an Exact-caused tell is an absolute fail, on builds with `EXACT_DEV_MENU=0`, in dark mode and at the largest Dynamic Type. Count a closeness gap only when it exceeds that grader’s retest delta; keep §4.3’s double grade, and repeat it when the rubric changes. Keep `screenshot … compare` as a map of where to look, and drop the region count from the bar. Add numeric oracles the original already defines (clock, totals, rows after a kill). A grader and a screenshot miss a drifting tempo.

**MAJOR — §2 thresholds.** “Within one frame” is finer than simulator noise, and Maestro’s timestamps are coarser than the probe. Idle at 0.0% is below what `ps` can show. Memory ≤1.2× is already missed by the app this method copies (26.7 MB against UIKit’s 21.1, §10). A hitch bar with no floor fails against a quiet reference. Steady activity is named and then left unbarred.

Resolve: Maestro drives; the probe, `xctrace` or `FrameTimingMetric` times. Median of 10 journeys. Slack is one frame plus the probe quantum, or 1.1× where the reference’s own spread is wider. Idle CPU at or under the bare template, median of 10. CPU during the app’s steady activity ≤ the original. First-wave memory ≤1.3× the original, tightened to 1.2× after three apps have cleared it.

**MAJOR — §4 cost and overfitting.** The brief’s author is unnamed, so someone who knows Exact’s gaps can write the tiers around them. The brief is not frozen, and one arm can read the other. “Name another app” is satisfied by a sentence. Replay guards that port’s binary, and a later builder can still trip. Euxy ran 22 ports; §9 has no cap, and a fresh port is about 150 M tokens, most of them cache reads of a large context (Exact’s largest context in `RESULTS.md` was 735 k).

Resolve: the brief is written from the original only, by a person who will not build the Exact arm, then frozen. A scope change is a new id. Arms cannot read each other’s directories. A class fix names a second consumer that already exists — an `apps/` app, a bake-off spec, or another port in this corpus — and is probed there before the next new app. Otherwise it stays in the port or `QUEUE.md`. Cap an app at four fresh ports, then graduate or record a miss Charlie accepts. Before a fresh port aimed at authoring, run the extracted diary task first.

**MINOR — §3 and §8.** “A commit in the last year” excludes a stable app that still builds; the requirement is a current toolchain. A one-to-three-hour slice would have cut the tiers that produced LLP 1125; allow one deep app per wave. “The native builder is asked to do well” is a hint the Exact arm does not get. §8 should state LLP 1116 §1’s training-data confound beside any token ratio.
