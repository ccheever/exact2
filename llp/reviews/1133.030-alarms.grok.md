1. **MATERIAL, IN-DELTA.** The countdown adjustment uses an anchor that boot invalidates, and a clock jump can ring early. D9 (lines 593–603) stores a wall-clock instant and, on `TIME_SET` while the device stays up, moves it by the wall-clock change minus the `elapsedRealtime()` change since "its last anchor." The record is specified to keep the instant, not the anchor. `ACTION_TIME_CHANGED` carries no delta, and the receiver is often a cold process, so the anchor has to be in the device-protected record or the formula cannot be computed. Boot resets `elapsedRealtime()` to zero and the boot paragraph never replaces the anchor. A later `TIME_SET` — NTP after `LOCKED_BOOT_COMPLETED` is normal — then subtracts a pre-boot elapsed value and moves the instant by hours. Separately, `setAlarmClock` is an RTC alarm: a forward clock jump delivers a past trigger before the app's `TIME_SET` receiver runs, and nothing in D9 stops that ring when the adjustment puts the end back in the future.
