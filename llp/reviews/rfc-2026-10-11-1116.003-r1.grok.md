### D1 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE

Plan reload is the right fix for iOS wall clock. Builders ignored "edit on the web" once `agent ios` built itself: mood-journal went from 4 drives to 15 and from 7.9 minutes to 15.8. Launching the existing binary when only the plan is stale (seconds; rebuild for Rust, Swift, assets, `app.json` or host sources), plus a detached first build, matches that behaviour. The tap fixes belong here.

Speed does not fix GPT's token move, 1.06× to 1.34×. Those totals are cached context: mood-journal is 3.46M cached of 3.58M, 54 commands against SwiftUI's 38 and 1.44M. A two-second drive that returns a screenshot still rides every later step, and a cheaper drive will be taken more often. Round 6 should read GPT iOS tokens and drive count beside minutes and native rebuilds. The round notes already name the companion: a text layout check (boxes, contrast in both schemes, tap targets) so a check need not be an image.

### D2 — RIGHT

Scrolling the focused field and the next control above the keyboard, and one stated rule for when a header is a bar, are platform behaviour LLP 1115 already gives the host. Workout-log spent about ten minutes restructuring forms and about fifteen on a title that left the bar. That build was the outlier: 20.4M tokens, 161 commands.

### D3 — RIGHT

### D4 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE

"Chrome is the platform's; the screen's purpose is designed" fits LLP 1115. Bars, lists, forms and controls stay unsaid, native, and valid in both schemes. That does not reopen the hex regression if the round button and the answer tile are host-drawn `exact:content` parts, as `SummaryCard` already is.

The recipe must not teach `background-color`, `border` or `font-size` on a `button`. Start-here's first pitfall says that paint makes the control the author's box. Stopwatch followed it (small bordered capsules) and lost to Clock-style round buttons, 7 vs 8, dark mode included. Amend that pitfall in the same edit: content emphasis is a part. Judge the recipe on trivia (8 vs 6, "coloured text on grey buttons"), stopwatch, tip-splitter, and water-tracker's single column. The "7 of 10" list is wider than this decision. Unit-converter lost because its value fields set `inputmode="text"` and favorites could not be removed. Flashcards' first gap is no edit or delete, which this RFC already leaves to the builder. ## What is missing

The cost lever beside D1 and D5 is the context a drive adds. SwiftUI's token ratio went from 1.33× to 1.37× the same way: more commands, screenshots kept. The round notes already had Muse at about 20–30 KB more context per step than the comparison. D5 does not touch that. A text check in both schemes is to tokens what plan reload is to minutes. The ticking clock stays deferred. Round 5's time cost was the stale read, and water-tracker fell from 82 commands to 61.

D4, held to host-drawn parts and the colour warning, is the right answer to "plainer," and it keeps LLP 1115. D1 is the right wall-clock lever on iOS. D5 is the right lever on the React ratio. The token regression this round produced is the drive's cached context, which none of the three closes on its own.

## Risk

D1 can improve minutes and worsen tokens. That is how "all three" fell from 6 to 5 while preference moved to 13–9–2. Painted content buttons would spend the dark-mode wins, which are why Exact2 is now ahead, on a look graders liked in apps that ignore dark mode. Adding a design page to the default read would spend what D5 saves.
