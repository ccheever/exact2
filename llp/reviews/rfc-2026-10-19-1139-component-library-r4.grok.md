1. **BLOCKING** — D4 (slot chain and the rendered gate). The deny-list does not cover the ways a recipe on the chain removes the fill, and the pixel oracle does not prove the marker is visible. Groups bind only `class` rows on a `part=` node. The component’s own attributes are free except for that short list, and a node with no `part=` is free entirely. `transform`, `scale`, `font-size`, and `line-height` are absent from the list, and `text` explicitly allows `font-size` and `line-height`, so `font-size="0"`, `line-height="0"`, or `scale="0"` on the chain is legal. `opacity` is refused only at exactly `0`.
