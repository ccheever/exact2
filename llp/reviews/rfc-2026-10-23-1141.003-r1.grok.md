Verdict: READY WITH CHANGES — the mark, the title view, and the title menu are the right split and the right words, but several UIKit and tvOS presentation choices are locked ahead of the measurements they depend on.

## Strengths

- **D1 and the summary.** No new tag. `image`, `alt`, `width`, `height`, `object-fit`, and CSS `light-dark()` are the words a web author already has. Rejecting `<picture>` is right: `tags.rs` already points `picture` at `image`, and `media="(prefers-color-scheme)"` follows the page preference, not the element's used `color-scheme`.
- **D1's one-row rule and D4.** The bug today is a silent drop (`ChipFace` never builds a part for a non-symbol image; `Part::Pressable => {}` never looks inside a button). Warning, and sending stacked words to the title view, matches LLP 1115: a standard bar item is one row, and a hand-built mark in stacked type is a picture.
- **D2.** The heading stays the large title, the back title, the Mac window title, `document.title`, and the VoiceOver screen name. The principal is a separate live view. "Exact adds no collapsing of its own" is the right answer to LLP 1141.001 D9, which asked how a title view collapses with the large title.
- **`toolbarPlacement="principal"`.** The property already exists (LLP 1001: `window` on `role="toolbar"`, `navigation` on a window-toolbar action). `principal` is SwiftUI's name for this slot and later maps to the Mac's centred toolbar item. That is a better extension than a new attribute. HTML has no word for this slot.
- **D3.** A button around the heading is already the title's press (`bars.rs` only when it hears `press`). A menu button is already `MenuHost.pullDown`, which returns a `UIMenu` built with `UIDeferredMenuElement`. That is what `titleMenuProvider` (iOS 16, not tvOS) consumes. Putting `popovertarget` on the button, rather than on the heading text as D9's one-line brief said, is the right refinement. `MenuShape.swift` is the right definition of "menu".
- **Checked claims that hold.** A `MENU` wrapper becomes an item and the heading inside is not walked, so the header is no bar. `titleMenuProvider` is unset. `act` requires `press`. Shared glass is suppressed only for a badge (`hidesSharedBackground`); `sharesBackground = false` is the separate `ownGlass` path. The Mac keeps a pressable title in the page, whole or not at all (`HeaderMac.swift`). Desktop Linux with no bar shell paints the header (`host/linux/src/bars.rs`).

This RFC does not move tab bars onto a new API. A header `tablist` stays the segmented title unless a principal is also present, and that combination is `lower-header-unplaced`.

## Concerns

1. **MAJOR — §1 and §6.** Stage 0 is the compiler warning, not a measurement. The UIKit probe was a 90-line scratch app on iOS 27, was not kept, and did not tap. D2's "title view sits above the expanded large title and remains when it collapses" contradicts the documented contract that `titleView` is shown instead of the title, and that exception is version-sensitive. iOS 26 is unmeasured. **Resolve:** a kept probe on iOS 26 and iOS 27 — title view expanded and collapsed, back-title string, a real tap on the large title, chevron, custom-view glass, image rendering mode — before D1–D3 are treated as confirmed. The warning can still land first.

2. **MAJOR — D4.** The message tells the author to move the picture below the `header`. Agents will do that in the releases before D1, and remove the mark this RFC exists to restore. A computed source is not warned at all. **Resolve:** say the picture is not drawn in the iOS bar yet, name the node in the host log, and do not prescribe a move.

3. **MAJOR — D1, the UIKit table.** `UIBarButtonItem(image:)` is a template glyph. It will not keep a 72×20 multi-colour mark, honour `width`/`height`, or clip a round photo. A raster needs `customView`, `.alwaysOriginal`, and `hidesSharedBackground` when glass is unwanted. `sharesBackground = false` is a separate capsule, which is what `ownGlass` does today, and it is a different look. **Resolve:** `image:` only for a symbol-like button; custom view for every other picture. Leave the round-photo glass rule provisional on the iOS 26 probe.

4. **MAJOR — D1, VoiceOver.** The RFC says a mark's `alt` is read as an image. A `UIBarButtonItem(customView:)` is a button unless traits say otherwise. **Resolve:** non-interactive mark, image trait, `alt=""` omitted from the accessibility tree, covered by a test.

5. **MAJOR — D3, tvOS, and §3.** "A bar item showing the heading's words, beside a plain title" draws the title twice. tvOS has `titleView` and no `titleMenuProvider`. **Resolve:** the title menu on tvOS is the title view, a button that opens the menu and is in the focus order.

6. **MAJOR — D3, the kernel rule.** Promoting every popover around the heading turns a header that is not a bar today into a bar. `titleMenuProvider` can only return a `UIMenu`. **Resolve:** only a menu-shaped popover becomes the title menu. Any other popover around the heading keeps today's behaviour until it is specified.

7. **MAJOR — D3, the driver, and §4.** A bar pull-down does not open under `--chrome platform`. `NavigationBarIOS` reports it unsupported unless `--touch platform`, and `act` refuses a control that has no `press`. The planned `agent.mjs ios --chrome platform` tap will not open UIKit's menu. **Resolve:** name `--touch platform`, or the new bridge, in the drive, and extend `act` in the same stage.

8. **MAJOR — D3, the chevron.** Author-wins is the right precedence, and the docs say not to write a chevron. Hackers' original draws one, so the next port will write one and get UIKit's as well. **Resolve:** a compiler warning, in the same spirit as D4, when a title-menu button also contains a chevron.

9. **MINOR — D2, the property.** `toolbarPlacement` currently means which toolbar, not a slot inside a route header. **Resolve:** `principal` applies only to a non-heading child of a route `header`, including a custom button; it does not request a window toolbar; elsewhere it is ignored or warned.

10. **MINOR — D2, unsaid type.** Unsaid type "the body style, as a readout's" is 17-point body in the title slot. The drawn title uses headline and footnote. **Resolve:** unsaid principal text uses the bar's title styles.

11. **MINOR — D2, layout.** A popover top-layer root brings light-dismiss, inert, and a viewport containing block. The header is `display: none` on iOS, which skips descendants, so the principal is a new layout exception and must not also paint in the page. **Resolve:** a title-view layout root, relaid out when the width the bar offers changes.

12. **MINOR — §3, Android.** `presenter/bars.rs:228-245` is `host/linux/src/presenter/bars.rs` `bar_button` (title and symbol only), which supports "symbol or text". `:589-595` is `bar_box`, the anchor rect for a bar button, not a title drop-down. Android D1 and D3 are not marked stage 4, and stage 1 does not include Android. **Resolve:** mark both as stage 4 and drop the `bar_box` citation.

## Open questions

1. **Large-title tap.** Keep the `aria-level=2` advice either way. The probe already shows no chevron and no interaction on the expanded large title, so a menu there is undiscoverable at rest even if a tap happens to open it. Confidence that the advice stays: **80**. Confidence that the tap does not open the menu: **68**, until a kept tap on iOS 26 and 27.

2. **Layout root.** A title-view root: max-content, capped to the width the bar offers, hosted in the title view, no top-layer behaviour. Laying out the whole header would also retire `ChipFace`'s frame-less read, and it is a larger change that fights the one-row item rule. Confidence: **76**.

3. **Heading inside the button.** Keep button-around-heading as the canonical form, so title press and title menu stay one rule. Also accept the APG form, button inside the heading. On the web, expose the heading as the button's parent in the accessibility tree; `document.title` stays the heading's text. Confidence: **74**.

4. **`light-dark()` fallback.** Resolve it in the kernel from the used colour scheme, as a colour already resolves, and set that URL on the web, including when an authored `color-scheme` differs from the page preference. CSS's own `light-dark()` on images is an enhancement where the browser has it. Confidence: **86**.

5. **Mac title menu.** Use SwiftUI's `toolbarTitleMenu` (a menu on the toolbar title). AppKit's `representedURL` menu is the document proxy (path, rename, versions). A feed list there would be a tell. Stage 4 should screenshot against a `toolbarTitleMenu` sample. Confidence: **84**.

6. **Round photo and glass.** Use a custom view. Provisionally no shared glass (`hidesSharedBackground`), same as a badge, because the circle is the shape. Confirm on iOS 26 before the table becomes normative. Confidence: **62**.
