## 3. Directional risks

**D4 is wider than the grocery shape.** “Any `button` or `link` that holds text before any other text” also matches a row whose only content is a button. UIKit centers some of those (Delete Contact, a section’s destructive action) and tints a `Button` used as the row. This repo already treats a `button` row as an action cell (`grouped.rs` around line 768) and gives a destructive button the Mac’s push-button metrics (lines 778–781, LLP 1115.002). One lowering that left-aligns and grows every such button will surprise an author who centered it, and it will fight the Mac. Author > platform (`rules/RULES.md`, LLP 1115 D1): an authored `text-align` or flex wins. Apply the new rule when a later sibling is the value, which is the grocery case (name in the control, quantity after it).

**D7’s backdrop should amend LLP 1115 D2, not bypass it.** D2 says an unset sheet surface is the platform’s `systemBackground`. The iOS 27 grouped sheet is `systemGroupedBackground`, which is still the platform’s. Paint the route’s own background on the modal controller when the route has one; otherwise keep system. Walking ancestors will pull the presenting page’s grey or white into a transparent modal. This file is iOS-only. The web has no keyboard-accessory gap, and the Mac sheet should stay on its own rule.

**`SummaryCard` should warn, as `EmptyState` already does.** The card’s fill is what the author asked for. Stripping it inside a row is a silent restyle. A warning is the structural key and leaves the part’s identity intact (LLP 1116.007 D3.10 is why that part’s contrast on a grouped page was hard-won).

**“A session is a modal” will be copied too widely.** Book-club was already marked down for full sheets where a native app edits inline. Flashcards, trivia, and workout-log are three different shapes: two exits, a Back control that abandons a run, a pushed page that keeps running. The line that matches the tells is: a run has one exit, and that exit ends it or asks. “Session means sheet” is the next uncanny-valley guess.

**D6 is the right correction to round 18’s verify loop, and it can start the next one.** `1116.020` D4 told builders to act on large-text findings; five of them fetched `overflow-wrap` and broke words. Point at wrapping between words, and exempt platform controls and parts that are fixed on purpose, the same exemption `1116.020` D4 wrote for `font-size`. LLP 1115.000 D12.1 keeps an authored size as the author’s number, so this stays a finding, not a rewrite. `docs/recipes/symbols.md` line 19 (`width=28`) is the teaching to change.

**Cheaper path for most of the value.** One lane: recipe names in `contract vocab`, the grocery-shaped row rule, `list-detail`’s `delete=remove(it.id)`, the modal backdrop, and the `overflow-wrap` finding. That is the fidelity the reference screenshots can prove, plus the only token cut that does not depend on a new habit. The start-here trim is the next cut, and it is small. The persona label, server-search example, and session paragraph are real and secondary.

## 4. Measurement

Section 7 cannot separate success from noise.

Round 18 to round 19, same specs, Exact’s own tokens moved by a geometric 0.94×, and `1116.020` already calls that inside a round’s noise. Per idea the ratio runs from 0.50 to 2.20 (`r18/records` against `r19/records`). `1116.013`’s graded A/B could not see anything inside 0.96–1.11. A stop at GPT −5% and Muse −8% sits inside both bands. “Quality not down” is contaminated if the grader note lands in the same round that is compared to round 19.

What would tell:

- Regrade round 19’s screenshots with the note before any quality comparison. The pixels already exist.
- Judge D1 by lookup-only calls and names per call, which the change directly affects. Publish total tokens with a paired interval. Call the token goal met when that interval is clear of 1.0, or when the paired ratio is at or below about 0.90. A −5% mean will not do that at n=7 and n=5.
- Keep `1116.020`’s cause rule: a frozen cause counts as moved when it falls by more than the regrade noise (one per arm; six pairs graded twice moved tells by one or two and flipped one verdict of six), or when it falls to zero from five or more. D7’s 1- and 2-app rows cannot be read off the round. The reference screen is their judge (`1116.021` T2). The round can see the button-row cluster and, if counted together, swipe-confirm plus per-row destructive buttons.
- Keep reporting the unmoved classes from `1116.020`: hand-made cards, no `delete=`, fixed sizes, search still a field and a button. Otherwise a clean D4–D7 score reads as a fidelity win while the larger residue sits still.
