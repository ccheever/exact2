**4. Missing questions and understated risks**

- **My addition: distinguish absent data from “no override.”** The generated overlay API currently uses `undefined` to mean “show the original answer,” while an optional answer can be `null` ([typescript.rs:176–177](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/contract/cli/src/typescript.rs:176)). The web implementation tests this distinction at [ts-data.js:455–457](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/host/web-js/ts-data.js:455). Simply changing `T | null` to `T | undefined` loses an expressible result. Decide the boundary representation before approving absence migration.

- **My addition: specify continuation coalescing explicitly.** Two answers before an advance currently produce one continuation: [mutation.rs:1143–1149](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/contract/cli/tests/it/mutation.rs:1143). The semantics likewise stores an armed time per mutation, not a payload queue ([Runtime.lean:599–604](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/semantics/Contract/Runtime.lean:599)). Which payload survives must be stated.

- **My addition: ownership must account for acquisition cleanup and imported events.** Shelf explicitly stops a camera opened after leaving its screen ([app.contract:280–288](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/apps/shelf/app.contract:280)). Suppressing a destroyed owner’s continuation must preserve an equivalent cleanup responsibility. Meanwhile, imported events remain root-only: request ownership alone does not make that feature independently extractable.

- **My addition: measure decisions together after measuring them separately.** Payload continuations may remove many matches before absence migration; stricter snapshot diagnostics may make narrowing repairs more expensive. Do not add independently estimated savings.

- **Do not silently admit `find`.** 1150 D1 mentions it, but 1116.016 explicitly defers its early-exit search operation behind a trigger ([1116.016:242–244](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1116.016-contract-expressions-are-typescripts.rfc.md:242)). Keep that decision separate.

**1150 understates semantic relocation:** fewer grammar pages can mean more package policy, lifecycle machinery and boundary exceptions. Its TypeScript resemblance can also invite unsupported operations rather than reduce mistakes.

**Codex understates the cost of prohibitions and ownership choices:** correct snapshot programs gain captures; legitimate forgotten work gains declarations; component extraction forces authors to distinguish disposable work from durable writes. Those costs need measurement, not just acknowledgment.

**5. Strongest alternative**

Keep the present language and runtime model, and spend the same engineering budget on precise diagnostics, existing `members`/component extraction, and concrete library patterns for the difficult workflows. This preserves the proven core while testing whether the remaining difficulty is actually language structure. The historical record already shows that shorter Contract source can take longer to author ([1017:26](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1017-contract-restart.rfc.md:26)).

**One deciding measurement:** the paired proportion of held-out tasks completed correctly **including a maintenance change and adverse event ordering**, within the same fixed authoring budget, after equal engineering investment in each direction. If the combined proposal does not improve that outcome, retain the alternative; adopt any independently proven structural cleanup only under that narrower claim.
