Focus-timer: 78 KB of that, first write at command 8 of 29, on the order of 470k carried tokens of a 730k gap to React (1.17M vs 0.43M). Fresh tokens are already level (68k vs 71k), so this is re-send cost, and the 3 KB cap is the part that will move it. Two changes. Start-here is already 10.2 KB and is carried by every step, so "put what every build opens into start-here" has to replace bytes, not add a second copy of the recipe tables. The grade-bearing examples (stretched round-button row, pinned readout, radio persona, shuffle) stay inside those 3 KB cores. **D2. RIGHT.** Halving `AGENTS.md` (2.6 KB today) is about 10k tokens over a 30-step build. The step cuts are the decision. "Nothing needs a server" and "no `--help` tour" remove steps the doc shrink does not.

## 2. Diagnosis

Yes. Round 8 was right that counted tokens are context re-sent each step. Round 9 shows what that context is on small GPT apps: docs read up front. React's focus-timer read nothing and wrote at step 4 (15 commands, 0.43M). Cutting carried docs from ~64 KB to 25 KB is on the order of 300k tokens there and still leaves the build near 2×. D2 and D3 have to drop the extra commands or the 1.3–1.5×, and the 2.7× outliers, will not close. There is no larger lever in this round. Symbol misses are a minute each, and the Muse server pairs were the round-8 repair, already halved.

Cutting docs does not have to give back the quality wins. Those wins are the platform (bars, dark mode, grouped lists, `verify`) and short examples builders copy. This round's losses are a wrong example and a few missing lines, which is D6, not a reason to keep 11 KB of `navigation-stack` in every transcript. That bulk belongs on `pattern list-detail`, which a list app reads anyway.

## 3. Principles

Silent aliases in D3.1 weaken the one authoring vocabulary. A warning keeps the token win. D4's inference replaces a number field the author wrote with a different control, and only on some hosts. That fails both "what the author writes wins" and "would a platform person notice?" for any bounded field that is not a quantity. An explicit stepper does not. D5 is the principle applied: unsaid background, author wins, hosts agree.

## 4. Verdict

**READY WITH CHANGES.** (1) D1's cap is the measure; do not grow start-here past 10 KB, and keep D6's examples inside the 3 KB cores. (2) D3.1 warns with the canonical role. (3) D3.7 narrows split inference to a navigating row. (4) D4 is an authored stepper that `vocab stepper` returns, not a reinterpretation of `type="number"`.
