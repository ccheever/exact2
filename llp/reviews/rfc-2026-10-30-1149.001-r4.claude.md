## 3. New in r4, against the code

**A1, the parked drawable (L220–226, L346–350).** The mechanism is enforceable and keeps D7 and D11. Three contract lines are missing or wrong:

- *Where the check is, and what a skip keeps.* L222–223 has `begin()` take the parked drawable and "when none is parked the frame is skipped … the Windows carrier's rule". The Windows rule checks **before the walk**: `prepare_window_frame` runs before `frame_for(Window)`; on refusal no frame is painted, `present_due` is set and `p.repaint()` keeps the damage (`surfaces/window.rs` 484–502 at `77ab0960ee`; `window.rs` 466–470). A check inside `begin()` is after the painter has started the frame, and r4 says nothing about damage, queued uploads or the retry deadline on a skip. Sol's round-3 item 1 asked for exactly this ("preserve pending damage, ordered uploads and retirement when presentation is skipped"); r4 answered the mechanism and not this half. Say: the carrier checks the park before the walk, as `prepare_window_frame` does; a skipped frame keeps the presenter's damage and a retry at the next period.
- *Resize and scale.* L224–226: the live-resize sequence "runs as S1.6 made it, with the parked drawable". It cannot: `Resized` calls `layer.resize` (`setDrawableSize`, `apple.rs` 72–78) and then `draw()` synchronously (`window.rs` 414–421), and a drawable parked before that call has the old size. Today the worker's `call` acquires after the resize (`present.rs` 247–252, 285–296). A1 must say the park is discarded on resize and scale change and that the live-resize frame acquires synchronously after the resize, as now.
- *At rest.* A parked drawable is one of the layer's three held across idle (its IOSurface, ~10 MiB at 1600×1600, S1.4 L1441 shows two at 19 MiB). Either the park is released when the link stops and re-acquired at the first tick of a gesture (whose first frame is then skipped by the rule above, one period of latency), or it is held and the end-of-rest footprint row carries it. Pick one; it decides a scoreboard row.

Smaller, same amendment: the retained scene's pinning (L233–236, "pinned until the capture completes") can only work if **every** frame's pool targets and atlas entries are held until the next frame's encode replaces them, since a live `screenshot` arrives at an arbitrary turn and `begin()` resets the pool (`layer.rs` 28–44, `ink.rs` 376); say that, with its cost (one frame's pool targets held one frame longer). The gate (L241–243) compares a BGRA drawable copy with an RGBA re-render; Sol's round-3 note on channel normalization is unaddressed, one phrase. And "the present pass copies the presented drawable" (L241): under M3 a direct frame has no present pass; it is a blit after the scene pass on the worker's commit.

**A1's pre-flush count (L215–219).** "The kernel keeps a count of visible nodes that sample their destination" describes nothing that exists: `kernel/src` has no such counter (the only per-node knowledge is `paint_order.rs` 238–243 and the painter's walk, `paint.rs` 262, 1009). Write it convention-free: rows are recorded and replayed at the position a walk at offset `round(s)` would give them. Also `group_begin` receives points, not device pixels (L253–254).
