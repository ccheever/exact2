# GPT-6 Astra (xhigh) review of LLP 1116.011 r1

**Overall: READY WITH CHANGES.** D1 targets the remaining token gap credibly. D2 fixes real defects, but the quality plan needs a larger emphasis on screen composition. D3 is reasonable with explicit boundaries.

1. **D1 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** Working examples and file access address the observed lookup behavior. GPT web’s command gap is 8.5 commands per build; its documentation-lookup gap is 8.0. That makes this a strong React-token lever. Keep the command and file as alternative delivery of one card, and replace overlapping required reading rather than accumulating it. Judge the 12 KB expansion by total and fresh tokens, wall time, and subsequent lookups—not lookup reduction alone: extra context costs recur throughout a build. Include compact guidance on choosing compositions, since `content-design` was opened in nine builds. [Round-13 findings](/Users/ccheever/bakeoff/rounds/r13/findings.md)

2. **D2 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** Live-row layout and Dynamic Type are clear platform defects worth fixing. A matching launch background is worthwhile, but does not itself eliminate the reported second without usable content; distinguish those outcomes. For D2.1, teach a prominent button for “Start quiz.” Do not make word count determine control shape. A content-sized capsule is defensible as a documented component policy when geometry is unspecified; explicitly authored dimensions or circular geometry must still win. Judge fitting by actual text, including localization and accessibility sizes. These four fixes should ship, but they address only part of the quality deficit.

3. **D3 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** Document scrolling is a sound default for a website’s main content and consistent with LLP 1115. Restrict this to the framework’s page shell; viewport-filling geometry alone must not authorize overriding authored overflow or sizing. Preserve pinned chrome, push-at-top and Back restoration, independent split-pane offsets, and modal scroll isolation. Explicitly supersede LLP 1116.005’s choice of scroll container while retaining its navigation guarantees. Different web and iOS scroll implementations are justified by platform behavior; losing those guarantees is not. Full-page capture is supporting evidence, not sufficient justification by itself.
