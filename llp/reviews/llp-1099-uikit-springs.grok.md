SOUND WITH CHANGES

The v = 0 closed form, the bounce coefficients, the underdamped `settlingDuration`, the dimensionless reduction, and Core Animation's overdamped quirk all match `ios-27.0.txt`. The step-count claim is real too: on the W sweep, 11 / 12 / 13 Newton steps agree with UIKit on 6466 / 6960 / 6819 of 7014 rows, and 13 steps already equals a 40-step solve. The findings are where the text is tighter than that data, or too open to implement.

1. **BLOCKING** — A duration-form solve can return a value that is not a root, and the warning that is supposed to catch it does not cover the measured misses. At ζ = 0.2, u = 0.5, twelve steps return W = 2.86×10¹⁶ (`|g| = ε`, the exponential has underflowed) while UIKit's W is 2.480 and `|g| ≈ 10⁻¹¹`. At ζ = 0.05, u = 0.05 and ζ = 1, u = 5 the iterate never leaves W = 5. Four of the 54 W misses have `|g| > 10⁻⁶` at the reconstructed W. §4.3 calls every miss another root. The printed band table (`llp/1099-uikit-springs.rfc.md:369-376`) skips ζ = 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.7, 0.9, 0.95 and 0.99. It also cannot see Signal's call: ζ = 0.06 is not a W or A grid ratio (only the U row), and the A grid misses at ζ = 0.75, u = 3 and ζ = 0.85, u = 3.5 (curve gap 0.25 at ζ = 0.75). "Generated from the fixtures, with margins" does not define the margins, so the §8 test that the warning fires on `spring(200ms, 0.06, 0.8)` and not on the menu has no region to test. **Fix:** freeze a numeric (ζ, u) table, including 0.06 / 0.75 / 0.85 and a stated margin. After twelve steps, refuse the spelling when `|g(W)| > 10⁻⁶` or W is non-finite or above a stated bound. Warn only where the iterate converged to a different real root.

2. **BLOCKING** — The web lowering contradicts itself and LLP 1007. `keyframes` writes absolute values and the last frame is the target (`motion/src/spring.rs:202-232`). The glue cancels the previous animation and plays those values with the default replace composite (`host/web/motion-glue.js:581-586`). LLP 1007 §3 (`llp/1007-web-host-v1.spec.md:166-173`) says the same thing: absolute frames, replace, last frame is the target, and the style row is already the target. D6 (`llp/1099-uikit-springs.rfc.md:255-257`) keeps "the last frame is the target" and also says each component is its own animation with `composite: 'add'`. Adding an absolute frame to a style that is already the target doubles the property. 1099's amend list does not include 1007 §3. **Fix:** keep one replace-mode animation whose frames are the engine's sum, snap included. That stays inside the current glue and still shows a per-component snap at 240 Hz. Use `composite: 'add'` only for offset frames whose resting value is 0, which is what `presence-glue.js` already does for layout (`host/web/presence-glue.js:237`).

3. **MATERIAL** — Several headline tolerances are tighter than the fixture, and two identity claims are not.
   - v = 0, 273 A rows: worst `|W₀/W_UIKit − 1|` is 6.0×10⁻⁸ and worst `|k₀/k_UIKit − 1|` is 1.20×10⁻⁷ (ζ 0.6, d 1.5 s). The summary's 3×10⁻⁸ (`:35-36`) and §4.2's 2.7×10⁻⁸ (`:352`) are both low. All 273 do pass the 10⁻⁷ test `fit.mjs` actually runs. The 308 count is 273 + 14 W + 21 B/C, and those 14 W rows are the d = 1 slice of A.
   - "Renderer to 6×10⁻⁸" (`:45-46`) is the hand-built S rows (max 6.38×10⁻⁸). UIKit-made U rows, including the overdamped ones, max out at 4.01×10⁻⁵. §4.5 already splits them; the summary does not.
   - "What SwiftUI builds" (`:31`) does not hold for `response` at ζ > 1 or for `bounce` at b < 0. §4.4 is the accurate statement: those SwiftUI fields use the `(2ζ² − 1)` stiffness factor, and Contract is following UIKit / Signal's helper instead.
   - "Maps 1:1 onto `CASpringAnimation`" (`:253`) does not hold for a physical overdamped spring (D4 keeps the textbook root pairing in `spring.rs:126-134`) or for an in-band duration k.
   - "Off by 0.168" (`:68`, `:476`) is not recomputed by `fit.mjs`. Max `|response − UIKit|` on the menu call (0.4 s, ζ 0.8, v 1, k 497.536) over `[0, 0.4]` is 0.206. The 0.87 textbook figure does check: 0.874 on the S row with ζ = 2, `aod=true`.
   **Fix:** replace the tolerances with the recomputed worsts, attribute 6×10⁻⁸ to S only, and limit the SwiftUI and 1:1 sentences to the cases §4.4 and D4 actually measured.

4. **MATERIAL** — The 60 s cap is unspecified, and the reason given for it does not meet the current cap. `MAX_DURATION` is 10 s (`motion/src/spring.rs:56`, `llp/1003-motion-v1.spec.md:73`). Bounce b = 0.8, d = 1 s settles at 5.64 s, which already fits. Bounce b = 0.9, d = 1 s is 11.2 s; b = 0.95, d = 3 s is 66.4 s, past the proposed 60 s. Nothing says whether an overrun clamps and snaps, or is refused. The same hole is on the paint path: `linear()` allows 64 stops (`motion/src/easing.rs:61`, `llp/1003-motion-v1.spec.md:34`) at the sixty-per-second rate in `SpringConfig::easing`, so a paint spring longer than about 1 s cannot be encoded. `presence-glue.js:111` and `shared.js:76` also hard-stop at 10 s. **Fix:** state clamp-and-snap versus refuse, pick a bound that covers b = 0.99, d = 1 s (110 s) or refuse bounce/duration pairs whose settle exceeds it, and raise or explicitly subsample `MAX_LINEAR_STOPS` in the same LLP as an amend to 1003 §1.

5. **MATERIAL** — D1's spelling overloads the third argument and collides with LLP 1081. Today `spring(k, c, m)` and `spring()` are the only accepted forms (`motion/src/parse.rs:159-170`); a fourth number is a parse error. Under D1, `spring(400ms, 0.8, 1)` makes that third number a velocity, while `spring(400, 0.8, 1)` still makes it a mass. WebKit's `spring()` is four values in the other order, mass then stiffness then damping then velocity (`llp/1081-names-exact-invents.rfc.md:33`). A comma-separated fourth argument turns a mistyped WebKit spring into a successful, wrong spring. The normative table (`:81-85`) also drops zero-argument `spring()`, which `:112-113` and the §8 parser tests say must keep today's meaning. 1081, which Charlie had already asked to take (1081 header, 2026-10-03), removes bare `spring(` from the parser (`:204`). Q4 leaves that as a side question while D1 still spells `spring`. **Fix:** put the new forms on `-exact-spring` with labelled arguments (`duration`, `damping-ratio`, `bounce`, `response`, `velocity`), keep zero- and three-argument `-exact-spring(k, c, m)` as the physical form, and do not accept a fourth positional number.

6. **MATERIAL** — Retargeting is specified past what was measured, and it disagrees with the gesture path. Rows H and H2 are two `UIView.animate` calls in one turn, before either animation has a clock (`probe/main.swift:128-136`; `ios-27.0.txt:2615-2618`). Both are additive, from `{−100,0}` and `{−200,0}`. That supports same-turn stacking. It does not show a running animation keeping its `beginTime`, and `.beginFromCurrentState` is identical in that setup, so the option that changes a real retarget is unmeasured. D5 then says a spring without an end is identical to the additive sum (`:230-232`). That identity needs equal k and c, and it ends at the first snap. `engine.rs:450` supplies a gesture velocity in property units; D1's v is in moves per second. §3.2 sends a zero-distance interruption through today's restart (`:296-298`), which is the behavior `motion/tests/it/hold.rs:216` pins (a release of 100 pt/s at zero distance still moves), while D5 says the duration form never restarts. **Fix:** mark same-turn stacking as the measured structure; leave mid-flight curves, opacity, and `beginFromCurrentState` as stage 0 inputs that amend D5 before code. Define gesture release as property-unit velocity on one restarted component, and state whether that component still cuts at d. Keep the equal-k-c qualifier on the linearity sentence.

7. **MATERIAL** — `response` above critical damping has no curve, and the cited clamp evidence is a different API. D1 lowers `spring(response r, ζ)` to `k = (2π/r)²`, `c = 4πζ/r` for every ζ. SwiftUI's stored stiffness at ζ > 1 is `(2ζ² − 1)` times that (F and G rows; `fit.mjs` matches 3.5 at ζ 1.5 and 7 at ζ 2). D4's CA curve clamps ζ to 1 unless `allowsOverdamping`, and the physical form alone keeps the textbook overdamped solution. `response` at ζ 1.5 is none of those three. Rows D (`ios-27.0.txt:2507-2512`) are ζ 0.866, 0.50 and 0.224, all `aod=false`; they show that the animator's duration argument is ignored, not that UIKit clamps an overdamped `UISpringTimingParameters`. The clamp that is measured is a hand-built `CASpringAnimation` with `aod=false` (S, error 2.79×10⁻⁸). **Fix:** pick one evaluator for `response` at ζ > 1 — textbook `spring.rs`, CA critical clamp, or SwiftUI's stiffness factor — and add one overdamped `(mass, stiffness, damping)` probe row before citing a UIKit clamp.

8. **MATERIAL** — "One implementation" is not true of the hosts that already integrate springs, and the wire end time has two homes. `presence-glue.js:97-116` and `:225-228` integrate layout springs in JS at 60 Hz, ideal overdamped, capped at 10 s, and play offset frames with `composite: 'add'`. `shared.js:57-81` only matches `spring(number, number[, number])` and emits 49 `linear()` stops. D6's wasm-host row still says engine frames at 240 Hz. A presence test that only adds an underdamped duration form (`:541-542`) will not pin the quirk. Tag 7 is three f32s and tag 8 is `linear()` (`kernel/src/wire/codec.rs:289-303`); the new tag is unnumbered, and the row already has a duration f32 that today must be 0 (`motion/src/transition.rs:132-135`, `llp/1003-motion-v1.spec.md:33-37`). **Fix:** number the tag, say the row duration is the end and the tag does not carry a second one, amend 1003 §1, and make stage 4 delete the JS integrators or feed them engine frames. Add the negative-bounce case to the presence cross-host test. State that the twelve steps call `motion`'s pinned `libm` `exp` (`llp/1003-motion-v1.spec.md:226-232`).

9. **MINOR** — `allowsOverdamping` is set on all 56 `UIView.animate(springDuration:)` rows, and on none of the 4 E2 timing-parameter rows. It changes the curve only for b < 0. E2 never probes b < 0. **Fix:** say the animate API sets the flag for every bounce, and that the timing-parameter initializer is unmeasured below b 0.

10. **MINOR** — The paint end jump is described as a repeated 100% stop and written as `1.0006 99.99%, 1 100%` (`:257`). Those are different offsets. `piecewise` already returns the later output when two stops share an input (`motion/src/easing.rs:340-342`), so `linear(…, x 100%, 1 100%)` is the jump. `fit.mjs:150-151` also drops the sample at exactly `t = duration` from both "inside" and "after", so the tests do not pin the value at the cut. **Fix:** specify `t < d` is the curve and `t ≥ d` is the target, and use one repeated stop.

11. **NIT** — ζ = 0 is a legal UIKit damping ratio and the v = 0 formula has a limit; D1 refuses ζ ≤ 0. The component-collapse test, "within 10⁻⁶ of the move at rest" (`:225-227`), does not say whether rest is the absolute 10⁻³ rule. f32(0.4) moves k by 2.98×10⁻⁸ relative, which is the same size as the agreement claimed in the summary, so "below anything measured" (`:267-268`) only holds against the 10⁻⁵ W gate.

12. **Open questions.** Q2, Q3 and Q5 are the right calls. Q2 matches the S and U samples; ideal physics is off by 0.87 on the ζ = 2 spring. Q3 should stay on exact2's absolute rest so existing springs do not move; the visible gap is completion time, and on a long move Core Animation's relative end stops about 10⁻³ of the distance early. Q5 has to pin iOS 27.0: one Contract result cannot depend on the OS the user is running, and iOS 15–26 are simply unmeasured. Q1 should not ship the twelve-step result inside the band. A non-fatal warning still plays W = 10¹⁶. Refuse a non-root, as in finding 1; a warning is enough only for a converged wrong root. Reverse-engineering the binary is correctly left as a licence question, and it is not required once non-roots are refused. Q4's positional `spring(time, ζ, v)` is the spelling to drop, per finding 5. The list is missing the decisions stage 1 cannot invent: the 60 s overrun, the `response` ζ > 1 evaluator, gesture velocity versus authored v, `CGVector` with dx ≠ dy, and `.beginFromCurrentState`.
