**Clock and the 2s grain.** `Date.now()` and `stat` mtime are both `CLOCK_REALTIME`. On APFS the resolution is nanoseconds; on ext4 it is nanoseconds with 256-byte inodes, or 1 second with old 128-byte inodes. An edit during the compile is at or after `inputsAt`, so its mtime lands well inside a 2 second margin, including a same-second update that does not move a 1 second timestamp. The compare is `mtimeMs > since` (`scripts/agent-launch.mjs:645`), so an mtime exactly equal to `inputsAt - 2000` would be dropped; on these filesystems the error is much smaller than that. A step of the clock larger than 2 seconds (NTP) can still hide an edit. The wide side of the window only nominates files touched in those 2 seconds or during the compile; the digest then drops the ones whose bytes match, so the grain does not rebuild a quiet tree.

**Cost.** The walk already stats the tree. The new work is a sha1 of whatever that wider window nominates. After a quiet JS build that set is empty for a normal app, plus Expose’s `local.ts` when a key made the bake rewrite it. The `existsSync` per recorded key is the same scan as round 5.
