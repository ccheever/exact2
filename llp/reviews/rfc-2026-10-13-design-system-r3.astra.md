Consequently, tokenization still changes precision and transition interpolation: [motion.rs:267](/Users/admin/x2theme/exact2/kernel/src/motion.rs:267) treats `Wide` differently. **Fix:** follow the actual [CSS Color 5 serialization rules](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-5/#serial-color-mix), including sRGB mixes and relative RGB results; remove the “mix of those in `srgb`” exception.
