## 2. New fundamental issues, most serious first

### 1. `clock data` and `clock settle` as "empty events" is the wrong delivery, and the delivered counts rest on it

D4.1 delivers both as empty events because "under the synchronous oracle every answer has already landed" ([LLP 1151 r2, D4](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/llp/1151-properties-beside-the-app.rfc.md:295)). The answer has; its consequences have not. A send's answer lands in the mutation slot inside the sending commit ([Contract/Runtime.lean, `runAction`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/Contract/Runtime.lean:819)), but the mutation's `then` is only armed there ([Contract/Runtime.lean, `armThens`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/Contract/Runtime.lean:599)) and runs as its own commit at the next advance ([Contract/Runtime.lean, `advance`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/Contract/Runtime.lean:1116)). On a device, `clock data` is precisely the step that lands replies *and their `then`s* without moving the clock ([docs/contract-grammar.md, "Authored tests"](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/docs/contract-grammar.md:728)), and the runner already has that exact operation ([runner `land_then`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/runner/src/runner/advance.rs:201)). `clock settle` is worse: it fires timers it reaches while advancing ([docs/contract-grammar.md](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/docs/contract-grammar.md:813)). Delivering either as nothing judges the following `expect`s and every `always` line at a configuration neither side ever presents, manufactures property violations the device cannot exhibit, and starts walks from "test ends" that are not ends. Today's whole-block skip at these steps ([difftest `test_case`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/difftest/src/script.rs:153)) is wrong in the opposite direction, but it is honest.

**Resolution:** add a land event to the script vocabulary on both sides — due `then`s and queue `next`s, no timers, no clock movement (Lean needs the corresponding advance variant) — deliver `clock data` as it, deliver `clock settle` as land-plus-timers-due or keep it a stopping step, and recount §5. The "about twenty-six of 222" figure does not survive until that recount.

### 2. A record-field `path` cannot be matched from the observation text, so the "one matcher" claim is false for the grammar's own path form

D1 allows `expect state a.b.0 == …` and says a path reads a record field "as `expect state` does" ([LLP 1151 r2, D1](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/llp/1151-properties-beside-the-app.rfc.md:174)), matched "the same way" on runner, semantics and device "by one matcher" ([LLP 1151 r2, D1](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/llp/1151-properties-beside-the-app.rfc.md:203)). Both observations print a record positionally, field names discarded ([difftest `observe::value`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/difftest/src/observe.rs:54); [Contract/Observe.lean, `value`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/Contract/Observe.lean:60)). Today's differential `expect state` is an exact whole-line string compare that a dotted name can never satisfy ([difftest `expectations`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/difftest/src/lib.rs:345)); the dotted reading that works is the device's, over named JSON fields ([scripts/agent-test.mjs, `expect-state`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/scripts/agent-test.mjs:770)). So stage 1 needs three representation adapters plus shape-field resolution through nested records, lists and options from the plan's type tables — the per-representation work r2 cited as the reason to reject expression properties. The same split applies to `expect text`, whose device meaning joins descendants while the observation prints only the node's own text.

**Resolution:** either cut record-field paths from stage 1 (root slot and list index only, values compared whole), or specify the adapter: field resolution from the plan's shapes on both observation texts, named JSON on device, with a corpus case whose property reads two fields of one record-valued slot. Do not leave "as `expect state` does" standing; it conflates two different `expect`s.

### 3. Stage 1 item 5 and D9's exit criterion require restating a theorem the vocabulary cannot state

The plan gives four apps "test files stating the twelve theorems' shapes" ([LLP 1151 r2, §4](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/llp/1151-properties-beside-the-app.rfc.md:516)), and D9's exit criterion is "the twelve theorems restated as D1 blocks" ([LLP 1151 r2, D9](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/llp/1151-properties-beside-the-app.rfc.md:457)). One of the twelve is `ready_stays` — once `ready` is true, no event makes it otherwise ([semantics/README.md, proved properties](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/README.md:352); [PhotoEditor proofs](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/Apps/Proofs/PhotoEditor.lean:25)). No D1 form states it: `always` cannot condition on the previous step, and `after` on every action would need an "or unchanged" the grammar does not have, and still misses clock and multi-commit steps. r2's own Open Question 3 concedes the form is missing ([LLP 1151 r2, Open Questions](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/llp/1151-properties-beside-the-app.rfc.md:557)) while §4 spends it. Eleven of twelve is the honest count.

**Resolution:** decide Open Question 3 before item 5 freezes the grammar (my recommendation there: add the form), or rewrite item 5 and the exit criterion as eleven shapes with `ready_stays` named as the exception that motivates stage 2's first grammar addition.

### 4. On a device, `after` cannot know which action ran, and `command` lines cannot be matched at all

D8 checks "every `after` line whose step is one `tap` of that action, the same way," reading `state` and `tree` ([LLP 1151 r2, D8](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/llp/1151-properties-beside-the-app.rfc.md:428)). The driver knows a step's target testId, never its action: the tests JSON carries target and form only ([contract `tests_json`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/contract/cli/src/tests_json.rs:23)), the per-step hook receives `{ name, line, op }` ([scripts/agent-test.mjs, `runTests`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/scripts/agent-test.mjs:475); [the `atStep` call site](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/scripts/agent-test.mjs:843)), and neither `state` nor `tree` names the action a press committed or the host commands the step issued — so `after "done" … command focus "done"`, one of D1's own examples, is uncheckable on the only surface that reaches a real device. D8 already concedes `never refuses` for exactly this blindness; `after` and `command` have it too.

**Resolution:** scope D8 to `always` (plus `after` state lines only where the tap reply is extended to name the committed action), and say `command` and frame lines are harness-only in stage 1 — or specify the new driver surface that returns the action and commands per step and cost it in item 6.

### 5. The trace is scoped as an observation-format tweak; it is a new runner API plus an unproved twin of `advance`, and it silently replaces the differential target

§4 item 1 is "`ran` lines on the runner (`observe.rs`)" ([LLP 1151 r2, §4](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/llp/1151-properties-beside-the-app.rfc.md:505)). The runner's per-commit receipts carry no action, kind or outcome — a kernel transaction receipt ([kernel `CommitReceipt`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/kernel/src/txn.rs:53)) timestamped only ([runner `Timed`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/runner/src/runner.rs:138)) — and a refused commit produces no receipt at all, while today's observation discards even these ([difftest `observe::run`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/difftest/src/observe.rs:236)). Naming each commit requires instrumenting every commit site inside the runner's advance — transport answers, `then`s, queue `next`s, timers, and kinds Lean's advance does not have ([runner `advance_steps`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/runner/src/runner/advance.rs:378)) — and defining which of them print `ran` lines the Lean side must reproduce. On the Lean side, the traced variant duplicates the local recursion inside `advance` ([Contract/Runtime.lean, `advance`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/Contract/Runtime.lean:1103)); from item 1 on, the corpus no longer tests the `advance` the theorems quantify over ([Contract/Invariant.lean, `Event.step`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/Contract/Invariant.lean:642)), it tests the twin, with the receipt disclosing it. That disclosure is honest, but "held to the runner step for step" is a weaker claim than stage 1's framing, and the cost is a runner API design, not a format change.

**Resolution:** re-scope item 1 as: a traced-commit API in the runner (kind, action or mutation, outcome per commit, refusals included), the Lean traced variant, and an explicit list of runner commit kinds excluded from `ran` with reasons. Keep the proof deferred per Open Question 2 — but price the item as the largest in stage 1 when ordering, which §3 below does.

## 4. The open questions

1. **Multi-commit steps.** Recommendation: let the counts decide, with the trigger stated now — if counted-only `after` steps exceed checked ones on the ten apps, per-commit observation is stage 2's first item; otherwise proofs first, because the proof machinery already reasons through every intermediate commit of an advance ([Contract/Invariant.lean, `advance_go`](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2-wt-verify-review/semantics/Contract/Invariant.lean:250)) while per-commit observation only strengthens falsification. Confidence: medium.
2. **The trace's proof.** Recommendation: differential checking is enough for stage 1; prove traced-advance agreement as stage 2's first item only before a theorem is stated over `ran` lines — D9's first templates (`runAction_untouched`, `advance_untouched`) never mention the trace and do not need it. Confidence: high.
3. **"Once true, stays."** Recommendation: add the form in stage 1 — one hold line reusing the predecessor-step machinery `old` already needs (e.g. `expect state ready stays true` inside `always`, or a `stays` block). It is not expressible as an `after` on every action, and without it item 5 ships at eleven of twelve (New Issue 3). Confidence: high.
4. **The device claim.** Recommendation: a false line fails `bun exact.mjs verify` as a failed `expect` does. The author opted in by writing the property, the device run is the only stage-1 surface that exercises the real world, and a beside-the-tests report trains authors to ignore exactly the failures that matter. Keep it out of any blocking CI gate, as §3 already provides. Confidence: medium-high.
