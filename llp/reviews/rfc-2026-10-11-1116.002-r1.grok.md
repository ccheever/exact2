**READY WITH CHANGES.** The direction of all four decisions matches round 4. D1 and D4 need a tighter rule so they do not spend the dark-mode wins or leave the real cost in place.

## 1. Decisions

**D1 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** Literal hex predicted every one of those five losses, and four of them were dark-mode failures. Mood-journal won in round 3 and lost in round 4 on white text on hard-coded light cards. A `contract build` warning is the right channel: `no-tells` already lists literals, and builders shipped hex anyway. The repair and the core sentence should stay with LLP 1115: leave colour unsaid, and name a role when one is needed. The accent is the one authored colour, a system role or a single `light-dark()` pair on `accent-color`. A pair offered as the repair for text, page tints, and white cards will be over-applied the way "a hex is fine" was. Event-rsvp's dark tabs on a light page show a custom page still fighting system chrome. Keep the warning when the app forces one scheme. Locking light mode is the cheapest way to clear it, and light-only is how the Expo apps lost.

**D2 — RIGHT.** Countdowns was the more native app and lost because a just-added row stayed invisible until relaunch. Workout-log showed the same blank row. An XCTest on both apps is the right acceptance.

**D3 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** Keep the list, pushed detail, add sheet, delete alert, and wide split. That skeleton is why polls and the other structure wins flipped. Drop the sort menu and similar extras. A 120-line target works only if that skeleton survives; the wide branch is most of `list-detail`. Line count is the smaller cost. Polls went 7.4M to 12.5M tokens, and contacts went 2.9M to 4.8M and flipped from a round-3 win to a loss. An eighty-line cut cannot account for those millions. The extra steps of adapting the file and re-driving it can. Score those four pattern apps on step count and tokens against round 4. Pattern-users versus everyone else mixes different specs.

**D4 — RIGHT WITH CHANGE.** `test ios` and `agent ios` should build when stale, as the web verbs do. That removes a step `docs/recipes/ios-driving.md` currently requires. The cost in the evidence is Muse running `bun exact.mjs ios` 20–38 times, each followed by a drive. Folding the build into the drive leaves those iterations in place. The decision that moves tokens and minutes is to edit on the web host and drive iOS at milestones, one in dark mode and one on a mutation. That matches LLP 1115, where the web is where the author works. Auto-build stays, so a milestone drive is not refused. The recipe has to say this too.

## 2. What is missing

D1, with that change, is the right answer to the regression, and it keeps the brand that actually won: a root accent role plus native structure. The apps with no literal hex won most of their pairs. Painted hex did not buy the polish React and Expo still win on the web. Recipe-finder, water-tracker, and weather lost while looking plain or broken, not under-branded.

D3 and D4 point at the right pile, the Muse pattern apps and the iOS loop. As written they pull the small handles: a shorter file, and one fewer command per drive. The loop count is what can move a 12.5M-token, 22-minute polls build toward SwiftUI's 4.1M and 12 minutes. Nothing else in round 4 is a larger lever. GPT's tokens were already flat, and the hex losses are quality, which D1 covers. Recipe-finder and event-rsvp also lost on missing URL history. Routes already exist, and that gap is smaller than the loop.

## 3. Risks

D1 as written can make round 5 worse in two ways. Builders will paint `light-dark()` pairs across cards and text, repeating the design loop that took tip-splitter from 24 commands in round 3 to 40, and those pairs still miss Increased Contrast and fight platform chrome. Or they set a fixed scheme to silence the warning and ship light-only apps. D3 gets worse only if the cut removes the add sheet or the wide split. D4's auto-build, with the current recipe left as it is, leaves the 20–38 builds in place, and each drive then pays a compile. D2 has no goal or principle risk.

## 4. Verdict

**READY WITH CHANGES.**

1. D1's repair is a role or deletion. `light-dark()` is only the accent. The warning still fires when the scheme is forced.
2. D3 keeps the winning skeleton and is scored on steps.
3. D4's main clause is milestone iOS drives, with `ios-driving.md` brought into line, and auto-build so that drive works.
