**Blocking**

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:346](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:346): **CLI updates break subsequent automatic revocation on Linux.** `renameSync` at line 420 replaces the watched inode; the watcher is never rearmed. Later revocations leave credentials and streams active until SIGHUP. Watch the parent directory. This follows [Bun 1.4.2’s watcher implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oven-sh/bun/bun-v1.4.2/src/runtime/node/path_watcher.rs).

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:120](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:120): **Revoked labels can be reissued.** Remove a label from `revoked` and restore it under `accounts` with another hash: loading succeeds because `labels.seen` records no permanent revocation state. The CLI also permits this sequence.

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:332](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:332): **BYO ownership changes leave old routing authorized.** Moving a parent from account A to B without changing token hashes preserves A’s sessions and reconnect permission. Traffic for B’s newly assigned names still reaches A. Revalidate live sessions against changed parent permissions.

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:334](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:334): **Revocation leaves connections alive.** Waiting clients lose their timeout without being destroyed, potentially remaining open indefinitely. TXT connections and pending DNS operations are also absent from `conns` and survive revocation.

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:310](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:310): **Idle connectors are unlimited.** One authenticated run can accumulate arbitrary permanent sockets; neither session nor stream limits constrain this pool. Add an idle-connection cap.

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:209](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:209): **Queued clients bypass the stream cap.** The check happens before queuing; `waiter.give()` never repeats it. With a limit of one, two queued clients followed by two connectors produce two carried streams. Bound waiting clients and reserve/recheck capacity during handoff.

- [tamber-relay-server.mjs:235](scripts/tamber-relay-server.mjs:235): **Refused sockets have no remaining lifetime bound.** The greeting timer is cleared, then refusal calls only `end()`. A hostile peer withholding EOF can retain sockets indefinitely. Enforce destruction after flushing or a short deadline; [Bun’s implementation half-closes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oven-sh/bun/bun-v1.4.2/src/js/node/net.ts).
