Verdict: READY WITH CHANGES

1. The diagnosis is right, and early doc bytes are the lever ab1 did not pull. On the same specs, Exact2 minus the comparison fits about 0.27 tokens per re-sent doc byte for GPT, the same rate as other bytes, about four bytes a token. Docs are most of Exact2's extra re-send, and deleting a fifth of the steps left the card and start-here in the transcript, which is why tokens stayed put. Change the forecast. "1.14M and 2.83M, ahead of both comparisons" is the upper bound if those bytes disappear and nothing replaces them. About 0.3 MB-steps of the GPT doc total is app source batched into the same command, and Muse's 0.37 comparison "docs" are its own skills, present on both arms.

2. The card read will survive D1, so the token drop will not. In ab1 B, after instructions had already stopped ordering these reads, 16 of 16 builds still printed the card and 14 of 16 still read start-here in the first commands. D1 leaves `contract vocab` with no name as the 12 KB card, which is the command they issue, at the start, re-sent for the rest of the build (0.57 MB-steps on the round-14 GPT builds). Make that no-name output the short text, and keep the long entries behind `vocab <name>`. The identical 4 KB start-here is the right backstop for the file they will still open. The card needs the same backstop.

3. The 8 KB pattern cap cuts the worked example D2 is counting on to replace those docs. `rest`, `list-detail` and `dashboard` are 11–19 KB because the contract contains the detail route, the sheet, the confirmation and the kept value. Round 13's Muse losses were builds that read start-here, skipped the card, and trimmed those routes. This RFC makes that the normal path. Muse also reads `docs/patterns/` itself, 9.7 KB and 0.49 MB-steps, and then reads the written app. Cut that second copy. Keep the routes. Cut repeated prose.

4. A larger `AGENTS.md` is re-sent on every model call, one more time than a read at step 1. It saves a step. It does not save a byte that also stays in the transcript. Four KB in the prefix plus the old card and start-here reads is strictly more tokens. Revert unless the comparison shows total tokens down. Keeping a null, as 1116.012 kept D1, is the wrong precedent once the always-loaded file has grown.

5. Tests and `verify` do not see the failure this change invites. Those round-13 apps still built. Make the graded round the keep decision, on the same comparison, and read minutes and fresh tokens beside total tokens. On the Muse paired fit the step term is about 46k tokens, so on the order of 15 extra steps offset deleting the docs. GPT's step term is about 10–14k, so the same flail does not eat GPT's savings. A drop in doc-class MB-steps is a classifier result: one command that cats the card together with the app's files is counted entirely as docs.

6. D3 is a small, later lever. Named vocab is about 0.4 MB-steps, already around a third of the way through the build, and a 6 KB answer cut to 1 KB is on the order of 0.1M tokens if the lookup count stays put. `screen` (2.9 KB) and `dialog` (2.2 KB) cut to 1 KB will earn a second lookup or a failed build, and that output is large and then re-sent. Keep an entry as long as the one example that prevents the next command. The diagnostic that names its fix is the part of D3 that removes a round trip.
