## MATERIAL

### D8. macOS has no dlopen

**Problem.** The only loader D8 names is compiled out of the Mac kit. Stage 3's exit is drums heard on macOS. Following the text, the dylib is copied beside the Mac executable and nothing opens it.

**Evidence.** D8 loads through `embeddedModule(framework: "ExactSound", dylib: "libexact_sound.dylib")` on iOS and tvOS, citing `VideoModule.swift:29` and `:308–312`. Line 29 is the `#else` of `#if os(macOS)`. `embeddedModule` itself is `#if !os(macOS)` (`VideoModule.swift:303–312`), so a call to it does not compile into the Mac kit. The Mac path the video arm actually uses is the uncited `VideoModule.swift:26–27`: `Bundle.main.executableURL`'s directory plus `/libexact_video.dylib`. `WebModule.swift:150–151` is the same split. The Mac copy site D8 does name, `build.mjs:1213–1218`, is inside `if (!ios)` and places a loose dylib next to the executable. §6 stage 3 requires drums heard on macOS.

**Fix.** On macOS, dlopen the executable directory plus `/libexact_sound.dylib`, the `#if os(macOS)` arm beside `VideoModule.swift:26–27`. Keep `embeddedModule` in the `#else`. Add the dylib to `loaded` at `build.mjs:1213` so the bundle copy (`:1268`) and codesign (`:1278`) include it.

### D6, §5. The device check cannot pass, and the keep/drop rule has the wrong sign

**Problem.** The stage-3 check compares a tap buffer's host time with a 1 ms window, and then treats a late result as proof that subtracting `outputPresentationLatency` is right. A correct onset fails that check, and the failing reading tells the implementer to keep the term.

**Evidence.** `installTap`'s `when` is the time the buffer was captured (`AVAudioNode.h:23–32`). The supported `bufferSize` is 100–400 ms (`AVAudioNode.h:86`). §5 defines speaker time as that `hostTime`, converted as D6, plus the output node's `presentationLatency`, and requires it within 1 ms of `t0 + at/1000`. The sample's own time is `hostTime + frameIndex/sampleRate` plus the latency still downstream of the tap. A hit after the first millisecond of the buffer misses the gate. The same header advises tapping the node connected to `AVAudioOutputNode`, because a tap on the output node can disagree with the device format (`AVAudioNode.h:100–103`).

`AVAudioSourceNode.h:26` calls `mHostTime` "the HAL time at which the audio data will be rendered." `outputPresentationLatency` is the maximum delay downstream of the node (`AVAudioNode.h:218–228`): for the source node, the mixer, the output's processing, and the device. Subtracting it aims the speaker at `t0 + at/1000`. The signature of that subtraction is the 1 ms sentence. The late bullet says a speaker time late by the source node's `outputPresentationLatency` means the subtraction is right. That is the reading you get when the term was left out, and it contradicts the 1 ms sentence. The early bullet, drop the term when the onset is early by that amount, is the double-count case and matches the header.

**Fix.** Speaker time is the tap's `hostTime` plus `frameIndex/sampleRate` plus the presentation latency still downstream of the tap. Tap `mainMixerNode`. Pass only when that time is within 1 ms of `t0 + at/1000`. Drop the source term only when the measured onset is early by that term. A late onset fails the check.
