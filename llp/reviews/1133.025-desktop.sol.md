1. **MATERIAL — Failed shortcut replacement conflicts with the promised lifetime.** [D3, lines 292–310](llp/1133.025-desktop.rfc.md:292) promises uninterrupted registration, but an unavailable replacement returns a successful answer containing `registered:false`. The runner subsequently releases the previous argument group once the request settles ([bound.rs, lines 332–345](runner/src/runner/modules/bound.rs:332)). Changing working chord A to unavailable B therefore leaves neither registered.

   Keeping A implicitly would also contradict the stated reader lifetime: no reader now requests A, and the answer describes B. The existing consumer avoids this: [T3SnapShot.swift, lines 332–338](examples/t3-code/modules/apple/T3SnapShot.swift:332) retains the previous choice on failure, and [T3SnapshotShortcut.swift, lines 100–106](examples/t3-code/modules/apple/T3SnapshotShortcut.swift:100) releases its registration only after replacement succeeds. [§12.1, lines 802–816](llp/1133.025-desktop.rfc.md:802) removes that distinction while repeating the guarantee.

   **Decision:** separate candidate validation from the committed registration, or explicitly retain and report the effective registration after failed replacement. Test A → unavailable B, including which chord still fires and what settings display.

2. **MATERIAL — An accessory app’s status owner can disappear with its last document session.** [D4, lines 356–360](llp/1133.025-desktop.rfc.md:356) assigns status items to the launch session, falling back to the oldest surviving session. This explicitly accommodates `navigate-new`, but supplies no owner when none survives. The parent [LLP 1069.010 D8, lines 320–321](llp/1069.010-the-mac-as-a-document-platform.rfc.md:320) preserves normal document-window closing; [main.swift, lines 456–478](host/apple/Sources/ExactMac/main.swift:456) destroys the corresponding session.

   Consequently, an accessory `navigate-new` app can show its window, close its final document, and lose its status item, timers and shortcut readers while its process remains running. [D6’s promise of continued execution, lines 452–456](llp/1133.025-desktop.rfc.md:452), does not settle which session survives. Its reachability fallback checks only the initial commit. [§12.4, lines 865–870](llp/1133.025-desktop.rfc.md:865) tests showing Settings but never closing and reopening it.

   **Decision:** retain a dedicated application session, reserve the launch session independently of document windows, or reject this manifest combination. Extend the fixture through closing the last window and reopening it, verifying continued status updates and registrations.

3. **MINOR — The first status-item hatch cannot run at its promised moment.** [D9, line 552](llp/1133.025-desktop.rfc.md:552) promises the hatch before the item is first shown. [D6, lines 446–450](llp/1133.025-desktop.rfc.md:446) first places the item, then loads the app module. Existing activation follows that ordering ([SessionActivation.swift, lines 8–19](host/apple/Sources/ExactKit/SessionActivation.swift:8)); hatches connect after the gate.

   This affects initial configuration, including visibility restored through `autosaveName`, as [Apple documents](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appkit/nsstatusitem/isvisible).

   **Decision:** explicitly permit an initial presentation with host defaults followed by the hatch, and declare any settings that must precede placement. The unpublished-frame boot approach itself is coherent; this contradiction does not independently block it.

**BEYOND — Data-code live reads lack the argument-specific ownership this design assumes.** [D12, lines 694–696](llp/1133.025-desktop.rfc.md:694) promises registration only while its owning answer is kept. However, [modules.rs, lines 994–1001](runner/src/runner/modules.rs:994) forwards data reads without their resource owner, and [bound.rs, lines 305–311](runner/src/runner/modules/bound.rs:305) explicitly releases their argument groups only with the whole source name. If data resources hold A and C, replacing A with B can leave A registered while C remains. Track argument groups by owning answer across Contract, TypeScript and Rust, and verify independent replacement and removal. Other argument-keyed modules face the same problem.

**BEYOND — Windows currently couples application progress to painting.** [D6, lines 452–455](llp/1133.025-desktop.rfc.md:452) requires hidden execution, but [window.rs, lines 235–267](host/windows/src/window.rs:235) pumps work and timers inside drawing; [lines 563–567](host/windows/src/window.rs:563) stop scheduling while minimized. Separating session execution from presentation is necessary across background uses. For this RFC, that is **IMPLEMENTATION-PHASE**: the intended behavior is already decided. Excord’s heartbeat must be verified while its window receives no paints.

VERDICT: NOT READY
