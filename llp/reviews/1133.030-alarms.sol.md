1. **IN-DELTA · MATERIAL — Resume bypasses the new person-only scheduling boundary.**

   [D7:467–480](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ccheever-projects-exact2/f4b5c629-4ed6-4746-9045-74f6106a629d/scratchpad/alarms/r2/capsule/llp/1133.030-alarms.rfc.md:467) refuses tool/wake scheduling because it commits the device to sound through silence, but expressly permits those origins to resume. [D9:639–641](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ccheever-projects-exact2/f4b5c629-4ed6-4746-9045-74f6106a629d/scratchpad/alarms/r2/capsule/llp/1133.030-alarms.rfc.md:639) says pause removes the system alarm and resume schedules it at `now + remaining`.

   A tool can therefore reactivate a timer the person deliberately paused, or pause a running timer and resume it arbitrarily later. That creates the future sound commitment the new rationale reserves to the person. Whether original scheduling authorizes such later reactivation is an authorization decision.

   **Decision:** apply scheduling’s origin restriction to resume; leave cancellation and pause unrestricted. Alternatively, explicitly authorize tool/wake resumption under prior consent and narrow the person-only promise. Recording already makes the analogous decision: [D9:749–757](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ccheever-projects-exact2/f4b5c629-4ed6-4746-9045-74f6106a629d/scratchpad/alarms/r2/capsule/llp/1133.009-recording.rfc.md:749) treats resume as a start. The runner’s [per-name origin admission](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ccheever-projects-exact2/f4b5c629-4ed6-4746-9045-74f6106a629d/scratchpad/alarms/r2/capsule/runner/src/runner/modules/admit.rs:9) can enforce the chosen rule.
