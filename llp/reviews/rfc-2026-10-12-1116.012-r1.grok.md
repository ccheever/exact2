# Grok 4.7 (xhigh) review of LLP 1116.012 r1

## 1. Decisions

**D1 is the wrong direction.** The total-token gap is the prefix re-sent on every extra step, and fresh input plus output is already about level in the published round ratios (React 1.03×, SwiftUI 1.12× this round). D1 adds start-here and the card, about 22 KB, to that prefix on every step in order to delete two early reads. Once those files have been read they are already in the transcript, so later steps pay them either way. The steps this can remove are the cheap early ones. On focus-timer the first write came after `date`, `cat AGENTS.md`, `feedback status`, start-here, two recipes, the card, the scaffold, `help`, two more recipes, `agent --help`, and `contract vocab`. Diaries for both GPT and Muse show that same opening, then more recipes, pattern files, and named `contract vocab` calls. That tail is the +8.3 doc lookups in the round-14 mix. Round 13 already put the how-tos on the card and lookups fell. Round 14's tokens came back to round 12. A first line that says the guide is already loaded will not stop the read: the bake-off stack prompt says "Read `AGENTS.md` here first," and today's `AGENTS.md` orders `feedback status`, start-here, the card, and `help`. Inlining turns that ordered `cat` into a 24 KB tool result on top of the same bytes in the prefix. Leave `AGENTS.md` a short pointer. Cut the instructions that create late steps, when the cache is large: "Run `verify` after each edit," the opening `feedback status` / `help` / full-card preface, and the recipe tour. Point the builder at `pattern <name>` and one verify when the features work.

**D2 is right with one change.** Each spec against its comparison-arm median, fresh input plus output beside total, and `stepmix.py` is how this plateau becomes readable. Exact2's own arm was 1.20× round 13 and 1.00× round 12, inside the ±15–20% noise. Keep round 15 as the clean Expo regression LLP 1116.010 D4 already set. Do not ship a doc-prefix change into that round. A 24-app geomean cannot see two fewer early commands. Probe step-class counts on a few specs before a full round is asked to judge a doc edit.

## 2. What the evidence supports

The plateau is real, and the fresh-versus-total split holds on the pairs opened here. Focus-timer was 67 commands and 982k total against React's 20 and 690k, with fresh lower on the Exact2 side (64k versus 74k). Recipe-finder and event-rsvp were essentially equal on fresh while totals stayed higher. Some long builds still spend more fresh tokens too (trivia, book-club, notes). The gap is mostly cached re-sends. It is not the two opener files.

Builders re-read because they are told to. The stack prompt orders `cat AGENTS.md`. That file orders the guide, the card, `feedback status`, and `help`. Start-here then names a recipe per feature and says to verify after each edit. Those late verify, drive, and build steps (+4 drives, +2 verify, +1.5 builds in the GPT-web mix) are the expensive multiplier. Bare `date` is the shared diary rule. Fewer real events take fewer of them.

Wall clock is already ahead (0.86× React, 0.85× SwiftUI) and aggregate fresh tokens are about level, so another doc-placement round cannot serve the goal. Keep a round for a platform defect a grader can see, and for the unconfounded Expo check. Judge a workflow change by its step class on a handful of repeated specs. The plain web look is a quality gap the findings already assign to LLP 1095 D4's theme.

## 3. Principles

D1 strains LLP 1115 D7. That decision made one short starter the only required reading after an app `AGENTS.md` had grown into a large required context. Injecting both full texts into the file every agent loads, on every step, is that required context again. Author, then platform, then CSS default is untouched. The token goal is not: a larger always-on prefix is the quantity this round says is too high. Concatenating both documents also duplicates the groceries lesson and the how-tos inside the same prefix.

## 4. Verdict

**Rethink.** Land D2's reporting. Do not land D1. The change that can move total tokens is fewer late steps: one pattern, one verify, a lookup only when a compile fails. Measure that on the step mix, and leave round 15 a clean Expo check.
