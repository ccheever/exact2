SOUND WITH CHANGES

1. **Major — D1 omits the write-only case.** If the reported App Store requirement holds, `device.health-write` alone also needs both purpose keys. The draft supplies the missing key only for read-only apps; the existing derivation emits only each row’s listed keys (`llp/1069.008.000-healthkit-grants.rfc.md:75`, `:83`; `bake/src/reach.rs:129`). Define the symmetric fallback or explicitly refuse write-only declarations.

2. **Major — D2’s platform test contradicts D5 for tvOS.** `--tvos` sets `ios = true`, and the build passes `"ios"` to the bake (`host/apple/build.mjs:630`, `:853`). Consequently, collecting HealthKit entitlements whenever `platform == "ios"` also gives tvOS the entitlement and usage keys, contrary to D5. The derivation needs the actual destination or target to distinguish tvOS.

3. **Major — D7 needs explicit removal of existing kept answers.** Disabling `keeps_answers` only stops writes (`runner/src/runner/kept.rs:137`). Boot independently loads existing answers (`runner/src/runner.rs:944`), and `obsolete()` retains entries for still-declared readers (`runner/src/runner/kept.rs:26`). Require Health grants to suppress seeding and purge existing `exact.kept.*` entries, including across reloads; otherwise previously cached Health data can remain on disk or reappear despite D7.
