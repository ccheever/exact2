Two fundamental issues remain.

1. **A capped waiting timer can lose its wakeup.** [D6](/Users/admin/Developer/exact2-timer-wait-points/llp/1147-timers-that-may-wait.rfc.md:232) exposes caps only for held timers, makes an advance establish that state, and permits skipping the deadline wake during scrolling. Meanwhile, `waits()` reports only the earliest deadline of each kind, including already-held timers.

   Consider two `scrollend` timers: uncapped A due at 100 ms, and B due at 200 ms with `max=100`. A becomes held during a long drag. Its 100 ms deadline remains the earliest reported wait; B is still waiting, so its cap is not reported. With no frame tasks or other advances, nothing schedules B’s required 300 ms wake. Both can remain pending until the drag ends.

   [D10’s cap case](/Users/admin/Developer/exact2-timer-wait-points/llp/1147-timers-that-may-wait.rfc.md:442) exercises runner advancement, which can pass while this host scheduling failure remains.

   **Instead:** Make every capped waiting timer contribute an unconditional wake from arming, regardless of whether it has become held. Ensure an older held timer cannot hide later required wakes. Add a host case with the two timers above and no Exact frame loop.

2. **D5 gives conflicting orders that change whether a gated action fires.** [D5](/Users/admin/Developer/exact2-timer-wait-points/llp/1147-timers-that-may-wait.rfc.md:217) puts idle timers after every other timer due then. Its following paragraph orders all simultaneous timers, precise or waiting, by deadline and plan row.

   Declare an idle `after(100, save)` gated on `enabled` before a precise `after(100, cancel)` whose action clears `enabled`. Idle-last cancels the save before it fires. Deadline-then-row runs the save first. These are observably different seek semantics.

   [D10’s separate ordering fixtures](/Users/admin/Developer/exact2-timer-wait-points/llp/1147-timers-that-may-wait.rfc.md:432) do not specify which rule wins in this collision.

   **Instead:** Make idle-last an explicit exception to the simultaneous-timer comparator, and pin the gated cancellation example in one differential fixture shared by Lean, the runner and JS.
