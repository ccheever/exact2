Read-only probes show the parent aborting and this commit staying open in that scenario. **Proposed edit:** `ownSignal` uses a bound native `any` captured before app initialization. Probes record three additions and zero removals after three completed opens. The parent retained direct listeners too. **Proposed edit:** `open` saves the dependent and callback, then removes the listener when its returned promise settles. [DOM garbage-collection rules](https://dom.spec.whatwg.org/#abortsignal-garbage-collection) preserve these dependents while the live source and listener remain.

The ordinary native-composition path works: already-aborted signals propagate immediately; a `Request` supplies its signal when `init.signal` is absent or `undefined`; `null` clears that selection. Replacing the global `AbortSignal` after initialization leaves the captured constructor usable. Native dependent abort events operate independently of the caller’s listeners, so listener ordering and early garbage collection do not defeat this path. [DOM abort algorithm](https://dom.spec.whatwg.org/#abortsignal-signal-abort)
