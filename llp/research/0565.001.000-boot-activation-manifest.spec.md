## Summary

The Boot Activation Manifest makes boot-time JS eagerness **decided by
declaration, not by reachability** — with every load-bearing check one
of exactly three honest kinds: a **recomputation from committed graph
facts** (membership, units, groups, order, totality, the needs-walk
arithmetic), a **byte comparison against committed encodings** (the
needs list, the seam→group mapping, the pinned array orders), or a
**compiler-attested fact with a named conformance obligation** (the
first-frame entity marks; the effect-safety proofs' analyzer
identities). The manifest commits seam policies, derived membership
over an SCC condensation DAG whose **units evaluate independently**,
the entity table with first-frame marks, the required `needs` list,
and typed effect-safety proofs. The boot-activation stage is a
**non-failing step-9 pre-evaluation substage** inside ibex (by
lockstep amendment); structural failures stay ordinary admission
refusals; well-formed semantic invalidity degrades whole-manifest to
reachability-eager, loudly. **Classification can cost speed, never
boot; applied-path first-frame completeness holds modulo the
committed first-frame marks, which carry conformance evidence —
everything downstream of the marks is independently recomputed.**
Magnitude claims wait for M0; the `evaluation` slice binds at the
grandparent §5's number.

## 1. Scope and non-goals

**In scope:** the manifest schema, digest binding, and bounds; the
seam inventory and follow policies; effect safety; membership,
units, and groups; the needs walk and the two safety directions; the
boot-activation stage; the lockstep amendment shape; report fields;
budget joins; fixtures; obligations.

**Non-goals:** sync→async conversion (v2, gated per the parent's
ruling — the schema reserves nothing for it); any cap value
(grandparent §5); the plan-pure certificate's own definition
(0565.002 owns it); the agent package (governed by `boot-core-v1`,
0413.001 §2.1 — the manifest's module domain is the **app package**
only in v1); **React-tier routes carry no manifest — reachability-
eager with the existing L-D budget (the parent OQ2's v1 answer).**

## 2. The four classes (bound to domains)

| Class | Domain | Membership authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `runtime-nucleus` | runtime/ibex substrate modules | fixed by the runtime distribution — a committed nucleus inventory digest, never compiler-extracted from a route Plan |
| `plan-pure` | Plan **entities** (handlers, tasks, cells, providers) | a 0565.002 certificate row per entity; no certificate ⇒ `js-requiring` (fail-closed) |
| `eager-js` | app-package modules | derived: §4 |
| `deferred-js` | app-package modules | derived: everything else; evaluable-group membership is **per-unit and may be shared** (§4 — a unit belongs to every group whose root reaches it); the committed `modules[].groupId` is §4's home-group annotation, not an exclusive partition |

Totality: every app-package `SourceId` is `eager-js` or `deferred-js`;
every Plan entity of the route is classified; the nucleus is disjoint
from the app package (`nucleus-overlap` otherwise). Entity rows
commit `{entityId, kind, definingSourceId, class, certificateRef?,
firstFrame?: "render-producer" | "first-screen-handler" |
"mount-eager"}` — the **first-frame marks** are committed facts,
produced by the compiler from the route Plan; they are the §4 walk's
mark set.

## 3. The seam inventory, follow policies, and effect safety

A **seam** is an already-asynchronous evaluation boundary in the
committed graph facts: (1) a literal dynamic-import edge
(declared-and-bound under the served-subset rule, or host-bridged),
or (2) a registered deferred-delivery/suspension point (the 0535
`staticDependencies` lineage). The inventory is recomputable from the
composition; the manifest never invents a seam.

**Alias collapse precedes everything:** seam and closure construction
run over representative `SourceId`s (0413.001 §2.2). `seamId` is a
deterministic function of **post-collapse** facts — v1: the bound
edge's representative importer `SourceId` + the literal specifier
(deferred-delivery points: their registered identity) — frozen by the
lockstep amendment. Each bound seam carries a committed policy:
`boot-eager` or `deferred`. Host-bridged seams are outside the policy
table. A policy row naming no committed seam (`seam-unknown`) or a
bound seam with no row (`seam-unpolicied`) is semantic invalidity
(§7).

**Effect safety for `boot-eager` (a typed proof union, one required
per row):**

- `static-effect-free {analyzerId, closureDigest, resultDigest}` — a
  named analysis of the target closure; **a bare "effect-free" tag
  with no certificate is refused**;
- `plan-boot-observed {planCertificateRef, closureDigest}` — the Plan
  marks the closure's effects boot-observed;
- `idempotent {certificateRef, orderingDomain, closureDigest}` — an
  explicit idempotence/ordering certificate.

"Semantics-preserving" covers top-level throws, top-level await,
ambient reads, observable ordering, and error timing — not merely
side effects. An unknown `analyzerId`, an unverifiable certificate,
or a closure-digest mismatch is semantic invalidity ⇒ whole-manifest
fallback. Deferral never needs proof (the seam is already
`import()`-timed); pulling forward always does. Boot-eager
evaluation order is committed (§4's engine-valid order).

## 4. Membership, units, groups, and the needs walk

**Membership derivation** (recomputed, byte-compared —
`membership-mismatch`): alias collapse first; `E₀` = the app entry's
static closure (ESM/CJS order preserved — v1 never skips a
statically-imported module); add `boot-eager` seam-target closures to
the fixed point in committed order; `eager-js` = the result.

**Units and groups (per-unit execution — the r4 correction):**

1. Over the post-collapse static-import graph of the non-eager
   remainder, compute strongly-connected components; build the
   condensation DAG. **The unit of evaluation is the SCC
   (condensation node).**
2. Each `deferred` seam names its **activation root** (the unit of
   its target). A **group** is the set of units reachable from its
   root in the condensation DAG. **Units are shared between groups**
   — sharing is the normal case, not an ownership problem: every
   unit evaluates at most once, process-wide (single-flight,
   once-only, sticky-error **per unit**).
3. **First touch of a seam evaluates exactly its group's
   not-yet-evaluated units, in condensation dependency order**
   (engine-valid; ESM/CJS never overridden; lexicographic
   representative-`SourceId` order only as the tie-break among
   independent ready units). Touching one seam can therefore
   evaluate shared dependency units — including another seam's
   activation root **when that root is reachable from the touched
   group's own root**, in which case it is a genuine dependency and
   evaluates as one — but **can never execute an unrelated root**
   (one outside the touched group's reachable set).
4. Two seams with the same target share one root and one group; the
   committed **seam→group mapping** says so explicitly.
5. `groupId` = a domain-separated digest with the pinned preimage
   field order: (activation root — or the distinguished
   `never-activated` sentinel for the rootless group; ordered unit
   membership — each unit as its ordered representative `SourceId`
   list; dependency edges in canonical order, ∅ for
   `never-activated`). The sentinel's concrete preimage token is
   assigned by the §8 item 3 vector-corpus rows. Records reachable
   from no seam and not eager form the canonical **`never-activated`**
   group (admitted, linked, never evaluated at boot, reported).
6. **The `modules[].groupId` home-group annotation (the committed
   encoding under sharing):** present and equal to the unique
   reaching group's id when the module's unit is reachable from
   exactly one activation root; the `never-activated` group's id on
   never-activated modules; **omitted when the unit is reachable
   from more than one root** — shared membership is fully derivable
   (and byte-compared) from `seamGroups[]` + the recomputed
   condensation, so the annotation never lies about exclusivity —
   and **omitted on every `eager-js` row** (groups exist only on the
   non-eager remainder). The verifier recomputes exactly this rule
   (`membership-mismatch` on disagreement).

Two conforming producers on one composition MUST derive
byte-identical tables; the verifier recomputes and byte-compares.

**The needs walk (the verifier's, not the producer's):** needs = the
static closure (post-collapse, committed order) of
`{definingSourceId : entity has a firstFrame mark AND class ==
"js-requiring"}` — **plan-pure entities are excluded**: a certified
plan-pure handler needs no JS ever (the parent's rule), and its mark
remains only for O-A1 conformance. The walk is recomputed by the
verifier from the committed entity table + graph facts, then
compared byte-for-byte with the **required committed `needs` list**
(`needs-mismatch` on disagreement; a digest alone has no elements to
subset-test — `needsDigest` is optional, over the digest-omitted
preimage). Then the safety test: `needs ⊆ eager-js`
(`needs-violation`). The check never joins through 0485 dependency
rows.

**The trust boundary, stated:** the firstFrame marks are
compiler-attested (which entities produce the initial tree, which
handlers are first-screen, which tasks are mount-eager). They carry
**named conformance obligations** — reference-extraction fixtures on
the reference routes asserting the marks against the rendered first
frame (O-A1). Everything downstream of the marks (the walk,
closures, subset, membership, units, groups, order) is independently
recomputable by both halves.

## 5. Manifest schema and digest binding

`exact/boot-activation-manifest/1`, canonical JCS, one per
route/composition, committed composition content:

```
{ schemaVersion: "exact/boot-activation-manifest/1",
  routeId,
  nucleusInventoryDigest,
  seams: [{ seamId, kind: "dynamic-import" | "deferred-delivery",
            policy: "boot-eager" | "deferred",
            effectSafety? }],            // §3 proof union; REQUIRED iff boot-eager
  seamGroups: [{ seamId, groupId }],     // the committed seam→group mapping
  modules: [{ sourceId, class: "eager-js" | "deferred-js",
              groupId? }],               // §4 item 6: the home-group annotation —
                                         // unique reaching group, never-activated id,
                                         // or OMITTED on shared units
  entities: [{ entityId, kind, definingSourceId,
               class: "plan-pure" | "js-requiring",
               certificateRef?, firstFrame? }],
  needs: [sourceId, ...],                // REQUIRED list; §4's walk encoding
  needsDigest?,                          // optional, digest-omitted preimage
  eagerCount,                            // committed; must equal recomputation
  manifestDigest }
```

**Pinned array orders** (JCS makes order significant; two conforming
producers emit identical bytes): `seams[]` by `seamId`; `seamGroups[]`
by `seamId`; `modules[]` by `sourceId`; `entities[]` by `entityId`;
`needs` lex; group membership per §4 item 5's preimage order.
`eagerCount` is **verifier-derived** (the committed value must equal
recomputation — the same polarity as 0565.006 summaries).
**`manifestDigest` preimage:** the domain-separated digest of the
manifest's canonical JCS bytes **with the `manifestDigest` field
omitted**; the shared exact/ibex vector corpus covers this rule.
Domain constant `ibex:boot-activation-manifest:1` — assigned by the
lockstep amendment, never unilaterally. Bounds (validated at
**admission**, §7): seam rows ≤ 4 096; entity rows ≤ 65 536; module
rows ≤ the package record cap; limit/limit+1 fixtures.

## 6. The boot-activation stage (a non-failing step-9 pre-evaluation substage)

Verified at the pin: ibex 0056 §3.4's C-ABI runs steps 0–9 in one
embedder call, and §7.5 leaves composition records out of the
deferred-dynamic machinery. There is no exact-side "after step 8"
interception point — the stage lands **inside ibex**, as the lockstep
amendment's behavioral half, under six invariants (normative):

1. Structural/canonicality/bounds/commitment defects remain
   step-1/2a refusals (existing rows).
2. Manifest semantics cannot influence steps 1–8 authorization or
   linking.
3. Every composition record links regardless of activation mode.
4. Any semantic defect ignores the whole manifest
   (`activationMode: "reachability-eager"`).
5. Classification cannot create a step-9 startup error (return codes
   unchanged: absent/invalid ⇒ admitted, code 0, loud report; a
   step-9 throw on the eager path stays code 2 as today).
6. The invalidity report is loud (`activationManifest: "invalid"` +
   reason).

The stage runs **after atomic link, as the first act of step 9**
(never "during admission"): the verifier's semantic checks (§7)
select the mode; `applied` evaluates the app entry with `deferred`
groups unevaluated until first touch through the
CompositionSessionV1 **unit executor** (per-unit single-flight,
once-only, sticky-error; promise and error semantics are the seam's
`import()` contract; dependency order per §4; the monotonic
descriptor executor understands partially-evaluated groups). The
first-touch receipt `{groupId, seamId, evaluatedUnits[], evalMs,
touchedAtMonotonicMs, epochAnchor, compositionGeneration,
manifestDigest, outcome, trigger}` **proves no link occurred** (link
happened at admission; the receipt binds the admission-time link
evidence, not merely omits a link duration). Diagnostic first,
budget later.

## 7. Invalidity: the structural/semantic split

- **Structural (existing admission rows; refusals as today):**
  non-canonical encoding, type violations, bounds violations →
  step 1 `envelope-malformed`; manifest bytes inconsistent with the
  envelope commitment, `manifestDigest` mismatch → step 2a
  `composition-commitment-mismatch`. **No new registry rows.**
- **Semantic (well-formed, authenticated, wrong; degrade
  whole-manifest to reachability-eager, loudly):** `seam-unknown`,
  `seam-unpolicied`, `membership-mismatch`, `needs-mismatch`,
  `needs-violation`, `effect-rule-violation` (proof-union failures),
  `certificate-missing`, `nucleus-overlap`. Closed set; each a
  fixture; each reported as `activationManifest: "invalid"` +
  reason with the composition admitted and booted eager. A manifest
  is applied whole or not at all.
- **The conformance statement (inline, normative):** every
  **admission failure** has a registry row; semantic invalidity is
  not an admission failure; ignoring an invalid manifest is
  **reachability-eager — the complete evaluation**, never a partial
  manifest. Tamper still dies at 2a; classification cannot turn a
  boot into a refusal or a step-9 error.

## 8. The lockstep amendment (complete shape)

One 0413.001 + ibex 0056 amendment pair (both documents, dated,
parity-gated) carrying ALL of:

1. **§3.1 item 9 (append-only)** — the manifest under the envelope
   commitment; never inserted mid-order.
2. The `ibex:boot-activation-manifest:1` domain constant and the §5
   bounds rows.
3. The **ibex recomputation half**: membership/unit/group
   derivation, the needs walk and subset test, effect-safety and
   totality checks — graph-fact arithmetic both halves run; shared
   vectors join the O-1 corpus (manifest rows, digest-preimage rows,
   limit rows, groupId-preimage rows).
4. The **activation mode** carried to step 9 (expectations or
   session field — the amendment picks the carrier), plus the §6
   unit executor and partial-group semantics for
   CompositionSessionV1 — the §7.5 non-enrollment lifted **for
   manifest deferred groups only**, the named amendment that section
   anticipates.
5. **Report fields under the 0056 §8 subset rule:** the ibex report
   gains `bootActivation: {mode, invalidReason?, eagerCount}`;
   `__exactStartupReport.activationManifest` mirrors it as a defined
   subset. The amendment pair also **owns the report/schema-version
   transition** (whether `compositionSchemaVersion` bumps and how
   the fail-loud arms extend — 0413.001 §5's one-way discipline, one
   pair).
6. **No refusal-registry rows** (§7's split, stated explicitly so
   the registry's totality story stays closed).

Absent the amendment, the manifest may land dark exact-side
(produced, committed, never consumed) exactly as the composition
itself did — but the **boot-activation stage does not exist until
the amendment lands**; this spec never pretends otherwise. Naming:
the stage is "boot-activation" in all runner/ABI names (the ibex
package `activation/` inventory carve-out is an unrelated
namespace).

## 10. Fixtures

(1) derivation determinism — two producers, byte-identical
membership, units, AND needs bytes; (2) applied manifest defers a
seam; first-touch receipt fires with the evaluated-unit list, no-link
proof green, dependency order asserted; (3) `needs-violation` →
fallback + loud report, boot correct and complete; (4) absent
manifest → reachability-eager, report `absent`; (5) each §7 semantic
reason reachable; (6) tampered manifest bytes → step 2a commitment
refusal (never the activation stage); (7) shared unit evaluates once
from either of two seams (per-unit single-flight) in the
**non-reaching-roots sharing graph** (neither root reaches the
other); touching seam B never executes an **unrelated** root — one
outside B's reachable set (a reachable root evaluates as a
dependency; fixture (2)'s order assertion covers that case); (8)
sticky-error: a failed unit
evaluation rethrows identically on re-touch, per unit; (9) plan-pure
entity without certificate → `certificate-missing`; (10) bounds
limit/limit+1 → step 1; (11) `never-activated` group admitted,
linked, never evaluated, reported; (12) proof-union refusals (bare
tag; unknown analyzer; closure-digest mismatch); (13) the L-D join
binds `activationEagerCount` on an applied session; (14) committed
boot-eager order is the observed evaluation order; (15)
`needs-mismatch` — a committed list disagreeing with the verifier
walk falls back loudly; (16) first-frame-mark conformance (O-A1's
reference-route fixtures); (17) array-order permutation ⇒ byte
mismatch caught as `membership-mismatch`; (18) a plan-pure
first-screen handler contributes nothing to needs (the r4 exclusion);
(19) the home-group annotation under sharing — a unit reachable from
two roots has `groupId` omitted on its module rows and the verifier
recomputes exactly that; (20) the `never-activated` group's sentinel
preimage digests identically across producers.
