## 0. Read this first: what ratifying this document does

**A freeze statement is ratified once, and an error in it cannot be corrected
without opening another breaking window.**

That is the whole reason this document is written the way it is. Ratification is
not an approval of the surfaces below — it does not say they are good, complete,
or well-designed. It says something narrower and much more expensive:

> From the moment this is ratified, changing anything listed as **frozen** costs
> a breaking window. Not a PR, not a migration, not a deprecation cycle — a
> window, with everything LLP 0506 D1 requires to open and close one.

So the cost of a wrong row is asymmetric, and asymmetric in an unusual
direction:

- **Freezing something that should have stayed negotiated** is the expensive
  error. It is discovered later, by someone who needs to change it, and the
  answer is "not until the next window."
- **Leaving negotiated something that should have been frozen** is the cheap
  error. It can be frozen later at any time; freezing is always available.

**This asymmetry is the argument for reading §3–§5 line by line rather than
approving the shape.** It is not paranoia; it is that one direction of error is
recoverable and the other is not, and the two look identical on the page.

There is a third error, worse than either: **freezing something whose current
contents nobody has measured.** A frozen surface with an unmeasured membership
is a promise about bytes nobody has counted. §2 explains what this document did
to avoid that, and §6 is honest about where it could not.

## 1. What this document is, and what it is not

RFC 0491's OQ7 asks: *what does "1.0 ABI freeze" mean afterward?* Its own answer
names the deliverable:

> the exit criterion for the program is a written statement of which surfaces
> are then frozen (the EXWF frame rev, the generated C ABI, the rlib API, the
> EXNODE/feed schema) and which stay negotiated.
> — `llp/0491-kernel-refresh-program.rfc.md` OQ7

This is that statement, in draft.

**It is not:**

- a decision about whether the surfaces are ready to freeze;
- a schedule for the freeze;
- a claim that the four registered Phase 5 exit checks are green (two are not —
  `docs/kernel-refresh/p5/README.md` is the live surface, and §7 summarizes);
- an authored proposal. Where the tree settles a classification, it is read out
  and cited. Where it does not, §6 asks rather than answers.

**The governing rule this document was written under: derived or abandoned,
never authored.** A row with no citation is a row that should not exist.

## 2. Method, and its limits

Each surface in §3–§5 is reported in three parts:

1. **What it concretely is today** — measured from the tree, with file:line
   citations. Not what an RFC says it will be; what a reader can open.
2. **What the tree already says about its stability** — read from an authority
   file (`exact-contracts.json` is the LLP 0150 authority map), never inferred
   from tone or from RFC prose.
3. **The classification**, which is either *derived* (the tree already settles
   it, cited) or *deferred to §6* (a genuine choice, left open).

**The distinction that matters most in this document is between a surface that
EXISTS and a surface that is SPECIFIED.** RFC 0491's Phase 2 prose describes
EXWF carriers, digest preimages, and epoch dimensions in considerable detail.
Detail is not existence. Where a surface is prose-only, this document says so in
those words, because **you cannot freeze a specification — only an artifact.**
Ratifying a freeze over a surface that does not exist yet freezes nothing and
creates a false record that something was settled.

**Limit of this method, stated plainly:** this document enumerates surfaces and
their stability posture. It does **not** certify that the contents of each
surface are correct, complete, or free of the kind of defect a freeze would make
permanent. That is a different and much larger review, and nothing here should
be read as having done it.

### 3.2 The "generated C ABI" — hand-written, unversioned, and ungated

**The name in OQ7 is wrong, and the error is load-bearing.** There is no
cbindgen in the tree — no `cbindgen.toml`, no `kernel/build.rs` (the file does
not exist), and `cbindgen` appears only in RFC prose
(`llp/0491-kernel-refresh-program.rfc.md:623`, `:1804-1805`, as a *proposed
interim fallback*). The C ABI is **a hand-authored 3,031-line header**,
`kernel/include/exact_kernel.h` (guard `EXACT_KERNEL_H`, `:8-9`), paired with
hand-written `#[no_mangle]` Rust. The tree's own W0-A census says so:
`docs/kernel-refresh/w0a/ffi-surface-census.md:58` — *"The **hand-written
header**…"*.

This matters for a freeze because a generated surface is frozen by freezing its
generator and inputs; **a hand-maintained one can only be frozen by pinning its
bytes, and nothing pins them today.**

**Measured size: 222 exported symbols.** `pub extern "C" fn` and `#[no_mangle]`
counts agree exactly at 222 across `kernel/src`: `ffi.rs` 188,
`motion_gesture_ffi.rs` 16, `motion_outcome_ffi.rs` 12, `crash_capsule.rs` 4,
`crash_retention.rs` 2. Header shape: 10 opaque handle typedefs, 56
`typedef struct`, 3 `typedef enum`, 12 callback function-pointer typedefs.
**94 of the 222 (42%) are Motion-domain** facades. `motion/` itself exports
**zero** C symbols (`motion/Cargo.toml:18` is `crate-type = ["rlib"]`; the FFI
adapters deliberately stay in `exact-kernel` — `motion/src/lib.rs:27`).

**There is no version carrier.** The header guard is unversioned; there is no
`EXACT_KERNEL_ABI_VERSION`, no `exact_kernel_abi_version()`, no versioned symbol.
The only version macros are per-sub-ABI: `EXACT_LAYOUT_TREE_V1_SCHEMA_MAJOR/MINOR`
(`kernel/include/exact_kernel.h:1146-1147`, EXLT only),
`GRAPHICS_SCHEMA_VERSION` (`kernel/src/graphics_transport.rs:17`),
`PARAGRAPH_BOUNDARY_MAP_SCHEMA_VERSION` (`kernel/src/selection.rs:476`).
**The C ABI as a whole has no version. Current value: N/A.**

**There is no gate on it, and the absence has already cost accuracy.** No
`cargo-semver-checks`, no `cargo public-api`, no symbol golden, no `.def`/`.sym`
file, and no check in `exact-verify.json` (655 checks at `d2f3b0157`; 660 at
`adf44906c`) whose id or title covers
`ffi`, `header`, `symbol`, or `c-abi` for this surface. Only two narrow slices
are pinned: the EXLT macros (`kernel/src/layout_export.rs:331-341`) and the event
enum (`kernel/src/tree.rs:544-550`). Consequences measured today:

* **The registered census has drifted and nothing reported it.**
  `docs/kernel-refresh/w0a/ffi-surface-census.md:10` states **212** exports and
  `:58` states **2,790** header lines. The tree today holds **222** and
  **3,031**. `kernel-refresh-w0a-pack` (`exact-verify.json:4872-4894`) validates
  **file membership, provenance headers, JSON parse, and section presence** — its
  `expectedMembers` is a hardcoded filename list and **it never re-runs the census
  commands.** A stale number in a registered pack, green.
* **Two symbols are exported but never declared in the header** —
  `exact_module_check_capability` (`kernel/src/ffi.rs:5769`) and
  `exact_module_get_capabilities` (`:5796`). 220 of 222 appear in the header.
  Nothing catches the gap.

**Consumers: effectively one.** The Apple host imports the header directly
(`ios/ExactApp/ExactApp-Bridging-Header.h:14`, search path
`ios/ExactApp/ExactApp.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj:4554`); `ios/ContractNativeLab/bridging.h:3,21,27`
is a **hand-copied subset re-declaration** — a second parallel declaration of the
same prototypes. Windows, TUI, the generated app archive, and the corpus/gates
crates all link the **rlib**, not the header. **Android is not a consumer at
all**: `grep -rn 'exact_kernel_\|libexact_kernel' android/` returns zero hits.

> **Correction owed to a doc:** `docs/introduction-to-exact.md:243` claims
> *"Android reaches it through a thin JNI shim over the same C header."* That is
> **not true in this tree** — Android's JNI is a Hermes bridge and touches no
> kernel symbol. Do not cite that document as a consumer inventory.

**Derived classification: NONE IS POSSIBLE, and the reason is stronger than for
the other surfaces.** `exact-contracts.json` **does not register the C ABI as a

`layout-export-abi` (`:433`, `:443`), i.e. **1 of 222 symbols is registered.**
See §6 Q3.

This document set out to be an enumeration for you to read and ratify. **The

| surface | measured state | can it be frozen today? |

## 8. What ratification would make unchangeable

**Under §0.5 as it stands: nothing.** No surface is classified frozen, so
ratifying this statement creates no unchangeable commitment. The C ABI and the
rlib become explicitly negotiated under a directive that already exists and
already expires at first external release; EXWF and EXNODE are recorded as
unclassifiable-today with the measured reason for each.

**And this must not be read as "the question was skipped."** That distinction is
load-bearing enough to state twice:

* A record that says *"OQ7 freeze statement: ratified"* over four surfaces
  nobody classified would be the failure §5 names — authoritative-looking and
  referring to nothing.
* A record that says *"measured all four; two have no artifact to freeze; two
  are negotiated under the standing directive; here is each measurement"* is a
  classification of all four. §0.5 is the second thing.

**A structural option the earlier draft never offered, and should have** *(raised
independently by a 2026-08-26 review; recorded because it may still be the
cleaner instrument even though §0.5 makes it unnecessary)*: rather than
satisfying the conjunct, **amend it.** RFC 0491's Phase 5 row and LLP 0506 D1(e)
could read *"a ratified freeze statement **or** a recorded measured non-freeze
with a named re-entry trigger."* Every option in §6 assumed the conjunct had to
be satisfied as worded; on this measurement the honest deliverable is a
non-freeze, and a conjunct that can only be discharged by a freeze quietly
pressures the next lane toward freezing something. §0.5 discharges it the other
way — by classifying all four — so this is offered as a durable improvement to
the conjunct's wording, not as a way out.

**If a freeze is ever chosen later**, these remain the irreversibles, in order of
consequence:

1. **EXWF "frozen as of first published tuple"** (Q2 option ii, NOT taken) would
   freeze the two currently-gapped classes sight-unseen, including **23 event
   rows with no payload-family schema**. The largest unchangeable available here.
2. **Freezing the C ABI** (Q3 option ii, NOT taken) would freeze 222
   hand-maintained symbols with no version carrier and no gate, at a count the
   registered census currently gets wrong — and, per §6.6 item 3, must be done
   over a normalized ABI manifest rather than the header's bytes.
3. **Freezing EXLT** (Q4 option ii, NOT taken) would freeze the `{1,0}` wire
   layout across its Rust, Swift and TypeScript decoders. The safest freeze
   available, because EXLT already has a version, golden bytes and real
   consumers — but it belongs in its own row, not as a substitute for EXNODE.
4. **A `stability` dimension on all 135 `exact-contracts.json` rows** (Q5 option
   i) is not structurally irreversible, but an axis carried by 135 rows is very
   hard to move afterwards. Q5(iv) is far cheaper.
