## D1. A relaunch keeps the clock

A test's `reload` relaunches at the virtual time the test had reached, not at its launch date: a phone
restarted at 9:05 does not wake at 9:00. The zone, locale and seed carry as they do now. (habit-tracker:
three steps grepping the docs, in two rounds.)

## D2. An unknown location says so

With no `notfound` route, a location no route matches keeps its URL, and the app boots showing a not-found
state in its shell with a way to the root, instead of refusing to boot. A `notfound` route, when written,
gets it as now. A detail route whose id names nothing is the app's: `pattern list-detail` writes that state
as the detail's body ("No such recipe" and a way back), and the navigation recipe says both in a line.
(recipe-finder, event-rsvp; flashcards and contacts in R10.)

## D3. A sheet is never transparent

A modal route's unsaid background is the platform's sheet surface (UIKit's sheet; on the web an opaque
surface), so a form is never see-through; an authored background wins. The card over a scrim on a wide web
window is LLP 1115.000 D4.8, built behind `EXACT_WEB_LOOK=neutral`. The neutral look is ruled the default
(`c9d41b52d`), but its switch (`DEFAULT_LOOK` in `host/web/look.mjs`) waits for a second pass after Expo's
styleguide, so the card is not this round's. (habit-tracker, R10 and R11.)

## D4. A sheet's title is centred; a page's stays leading

The web centres the inline title of a sheet's or dialog's bar whatever flanks it, unless the author placed
it; a page's header keeps its title leading. The recipes and patterns stop writing
`justify-content="space-between"` on a two-item header (event-rsvp copied it from a three-item sheet bar).

## D5. A box wider than its parent, caught before `check`

A percentage `width` with horizontal padding under the default `box-sizing: content-box` is wider than its
parent by the padding, as in CSS: the compiler warns (drop the width, or say `box-sizing="border-box"`), and
start-here's pitfall for it returns. `check` finds the same box past a phone's viewport or its clipping
parent, and never a platform bar, a nowrap readout or a row that scrolls on purpose. (focus-timer,
recipe-finder: "Verify reported clean while padded phone containers visibly overflowed".)

## D6. `help` is the one read for the verbs

`bun exact.mjs help` names each verb with the flags builders use (verify's platform and `--shots`, agent's
`--storage` `--size` and its operations, test's platform), in one screen, so a build reads it once instead of
four or five `--help`s; `AGENTS.md` says so. `contract vocab background-color` lists the colour roles it
takes (tip-splitter guessed one).

## D7. Composition

- The content-design and web-look recipes say when content wants a focused, constrained column (one number
  and its actions: a tracker, a timer) and when two columns (a list and its detail, a dashboard), replacing
  web-look's blanket advice against a centred column. The round buttons' column takes a `max-width`
  (water-tracker: "at opposite edges of the column with a large empty gap").
- One value shown once: a second, larger copy of the SummaryCard's number is a slip (water-tracker).

## D8. The patterns write the ordinary app

A build that starts from a pattern opens one recipe or none. `pattern list-detail`, `one-screen` and
`dashboard` write, already working and tested, what builders open recipes for: storage that persists, a
dated record with its day and its test, the sheet with validation, the wide layout, the missing record
(D2). start-here's lookup says what each pattern already does, so a recipe is for what it doesn't.
Measured: docs, help and pattern bytes read per build (R11 GPT median about 45 KB), and recipes opened.

## As built (2026-10-12)

Six lanes, merged on `bake/1116.009-integration` with origin/main; lane log `~/bakeoff/lanes-1116.009.md`.
The lanes' base still held #259 (`f9d8cf637`), which crashed every iOS app with a navigation stack at
launch; origin/main had reverted it (`0d9f1140f`), the merge brought the revert, and QUEUE holds a lead on
the crash (weak captures in two closures #259 runs at once).

- **D1.** A test's `reload` relaunches at the launch date plus the virtual time the test reached (the
  runner's own clock; the web page's `epoch`, `EXACT_AGENT_EPOCH` natively), on every host; `verify`'s
  relaunch uses the reached time too. The tests recipe, the timer recipe and the grammar say so.
- **D2.** With no `notfound` route, the compiler writes one (`contract/syntax/src/notfound.rs`) for an app
  whose root view draws its navigation stack: a "Not Found" route ("Page not found", the location, "Go Home"
  to the first root) as ordinary Contract, so every host shows it with no host code; the URL stays. An
  authored `notfound` is unchanged. The JS target's launch with no navigation root falls back to `/` as the
  runner's does. `pattern list-detail` shows "No such item" with a way back; `contract vocab notfound`.
  QUEUE: a route stack reached through a `use`d component gets no screen; the words are English literals.
- **D3.** The lowering supplies `-exact-background` to a modal route (`sheet_page`), so a sheet is opaque
  on the web's current look and on the Mac (where it showed through); iOS was already opaque. The card over
  a scrim on a wide window stays LLP 1115.000 D4.8's, behind the neutral look's switch.
- **D4.** In the neutral look (`host/web/neutral.css`), a sheet's header centres its heading unless the
  header's `justify-content` was authored (marked `data-exact-said`, which the JS target now emits); a page's
  title stays leading. The recipes and patterns no longer write `space-between` on a two-item header.
- **D5.** The warning `lower-width-padding` (a container with `width="100%"` and horizontal padding or a
  border, in a block or column parent, content-box); the web and iOS `check` find the same box past its
  parent, never border-box, a row's or grid's items, an authored `overflow-x` or a nowrap readout.
  start-here's pitfall is back. Separately, a false "covered" on iOS: a grouped row sends a touch nothing
  claims to its field, and `check`'s probe read that as the field covering the text under it; the probe
  now turns that fallback off (`touchFallsToCellField`).
- **D6.** `bun exact.mjs help` prints the forms a build uses (30 lines, 2.3 KB); `AGENTS.md` says a verb's
  `--help` is for the rest; `contract vocab` lists the colour roles on every colour property.
- **D7.** content-design and web-look say when content wants a centred, `max-width` column and when two
  columns; the Stopwatch column is 360 wide at most; a value is shown once.
- **D8.** `pattern one-screen` is a tracker (a file log of dated entries, a midnight tick, a centred
  column; 6.9 KB) and `pattern dashboard` an expense dashboard (cents and days in a file, a sheet that
  validates after Save, two columns when wide; 11.2 KB), each tested; start-here's lookup names what each
  pattern already has, and a recipe is for what it lacks.
