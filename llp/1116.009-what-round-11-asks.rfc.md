## D1. A relaunch keeps the clock

A test's `reload` relaunches at the virtual time the test had reached, not at its launch date: a phone
restarted at 9:05 does not wake at 9:00. The zone, locale and seed carry as they do now. (habit-tracker:
three steps grepping the docs, in two rounds.)

## D2. An unknown location lands somewhere

With no `notfound` route, a location no route matches lands on the router's root and replaces the URL; the
app boots instead of refusing. A `notfound` route, when written, gets it as now. The docs show both: the
router's `notfound`, and a detail route whose id names nothing ("No such recipe" and a way back), which
`pattern list-detail` shows. (recipe-finder, event-rsvp; flashcards and contacts in R10.)

## D3. A sheet is a sheet on every window

A modal route's unsaid background is the platform's sheet surface (UIKit's sheet; on the web an opaque
dialog surface), so a form is never transparent; an authored background wins. On a wide web window (900 px
and up, the split's threshold) a `navigationPresentation="modal"` route is a centered dialog over the page,
a web app's modal, at a form's width; narrower, it stays the full-height sheet it is. (habit-tracker.)

## D4. The web's header centres its title

A route's header on the web places its title in the bar's centre whatever flanks it, as the iOS bar does,
when the title is inline; a large title stays leading. (event-rsvp.)

## D5. `check` finds a container wider than its screen

At phone widths, a box whose padding or content runs past the viewport or past its clipping parent is a
finding on the web and on iOS, not only a flex item past its row (LLP 1116.007 D3.11). (focus-timer,
recipe-finder: "Verify reported clean while padded phone containers visibly overflowed".)

## D6. `help` is the one read for the verbs

`bun exact.mjs help` names each verb with the flags builders use (verify's platform and `--shots`, agent's
`--storage` `--size` and its operations, test's platform), in one screen, so a build reads it once instead of
four or five `--help`s; `AGENTS.md` says so. `contract vocab background-color` lists the colour roles it
takes (tip-splitter guessed one).

## D7. Docs

- Round buttons on a wide window stay a phone's width apart: the content-design example's column takes a
  `max-width` (water-tracker: "at opposite edges of the column with a large empty gap").
- One value shown once: the SummaryCard is the screen's number; a second, larger copy of it is a slip
  (water-tracker).
