## 0. Summary

Round 14 (8 React, 16 SwiftUI): React 6-2, minutes 0.86×, tokens 1.33×; SwiftUI 7-8-1, minutes 0.85×, tokens 1.42×.
Exact2's own arm: 1.20× round 13's tokens, 1.00× round 12's. Round 13's dip was noise.

Measured against something steadier than one build, the program has plateaued on total tokens. Each spec's
comparison arm has been built six to eight times since round 7; its median is a steadier denominator:

| Round | R7 | R8 | R9 | R10 | R11 | R12 | R13 | R14 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exact2 tokens / the React arm's median | 1.54× | 1.39× | 1.40× | 1.54× | 1.43× | 1.50× | 1.52× | 1.59× |
| Exact2 tokens per app (geometric mean) | 2.37M | 2.45M | 2.12M | 2.01M | 1.98M | 2.33M | 1.94M | 2.34M |

Eight rounds of doc and tooling changes cut the bytes builders read by a third and the framework complaints in
their diaries by half, and moved neither line. Two things explain it.

**Fresh tokens are already level.** Fresh input plus output, against the same round's comparison: React 1.12×,
0.97×, 1.12×, 0.99×, 1.03× (rounds 10–14); SwiftUI 1.30×, 0.70×, 1.10×, 0.98×, 1.12×. The whole total-token
gap is cached context re-sent once per step, and Exact2 takes more steps: a GPT web build 34.5 commands to
React's 21.5 (doc lookups +8.3, drives +4, `verify` +2, builds +1.5, bare `date` +2.2, tests −3).

**Builders pay to read what is already in their context.** Codex and Muse both load the app directory's
`AGENTS.md` into the model's instructions before the first step (checked: each answered a question whose
answer was only in `AGENTS.md`, without running anything; Muse does so under the harness's `--yolo`, which
trusts the workspace). Yet every Exact2 build spends its first steps reading: `cat AGENTS.md` (already
loaded), start-here (10 KB), the card (12 KB), the app's files. Each of those is a step that re-sends
everything before it, and the first line of code comes at step 5 to 8.

## D1. Fewer instructions that create steps

The instructions builders follow into extra steps go. The harness's Exact2 stack prompt says `AGENTS.md` is
already in the builder's instructions (it said "Read `AGENTS.md` here first", and every build `cat`s it); a
harness change, logged, and neutral between arms. `AGENTS.md` says what to start from (`bun exact.mjs pattern
<name>`, then the card when a name is in question) instead of an ordered preface of `feedback status`, `help`
and the card. start-here's "Run `verify` after each edit" becomes: `verify` when the features work, and once
more on the app's platform at the end; a lookup is for a name the card lacks or a compile error. Nothing is
added to what is always loaded.

Measure: by D2's comparison, verify and drive steps, doc lookups, steps before the first write, total and
fresh tokens.

## D2. Measure a change against the unchanged Exact2

A workflow or doc change is judged by a controlled comparison, `~/bakeoff/ab.py`: the same specs built with
the unchanged Exact2 (A) and with the change (B), same model, same harness, interleaved, each spec twice per
arm, reported by step class (`stepmix.py`), commands, fresh and total tokens, minutes, and whether the build's
own tests and `verify` pass, with the spread beside each mean. Full rounds report each spec against its median
comparison arm and fresh tokens beside total. Round 15 is the Expo check (LLP 1116.010 D4), run after D1's
comparison.

## D3. Verification that shows the delivered app

- An iOS screenshot shows what the person sees, a native alert or sheet included, without `window`
  (grocery-list's first two captures missed the alert).
- The app a person launches draws what the agent's launch draws (notes: a standalone launch omitted the
  navigation title the agent's launch showed; find why the two launches differ and make them the same).
- The web `check` does not report a list's last row as under an in-flow tab bar when every row scrolls into
  reach (contacts: a fold-row finding that padding did not move).
