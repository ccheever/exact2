## 0. Summary

Round 19 beat SwiftUI 10-2 and UIKit 10-2, with a platform score of 7.4 and 7.3 against 5.7 and 5.2 and fewer bugs
an app (3.5 and 3.2 against 4.7 and 4.2), in less wall clock (0.45x, 0.66x). Two things are not yet sound. Tokens:
Exact's own arm did not move against round 18 (0.94x), and GPT's Exact builds cost 1.11x their UIKit pairs. And a
residual of tells and bugs, a third of them caused by Exact's own defaults, tools or patterns.

Two investigations measured both (`scratchpad/inv21-{tok,fid}`). The token one counted every model call's input:
docs and lookups are 24% of a GPT build and 22% of a Muse build, and what moves is calls, not knowledge, as LLP
1116.013 found. A Muse build asks for one name a call, eleven calls that only look something up, each re-sending a
53k context: 15% of the build.

| | What changes | Saves or answers | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|

| D2 | start-here sheds what no builder of these apps uses (Snapback, ports, release), behind the lookup table | GPT −3%, Muse −2% (bound) | docs |

**What it buys.** Tokens: bounds of about 0.2M a GPT build and 0.4M a Muse build if builders batch; expect about half.
Fidelity: about 30 tells and bugs Exact causes.

## 2. The tests every change must pass

- **T1, knowledge kept.** A token change removes calls or bytes that carry nothing a builder needed; it never hides
  what they look up (LLP 1116.013).
- **T2, the reference.** A fidelity change matches a hand-built UIKit screen on the iOS 27 simulator.
- **T3, by structure.** A default or warning keys on structure, never on a word or a symbol.

## D1. One call, several names

`contract vocab` already takes several names and prints each answer in order, but not a recipe's: it answers
`grouped-form` with "not a built-in tag". It takes recipe names too (`contract vocab swipe grouped-form persist`
prints the recipe in its place), and start-here's lookup table shows that call as a line to copy for each kind of
screen, which models follow where a sentence asking them to batch they do not. Round 19's Muse builds asked one name
a call.

## D2. What the builder is handed

start-here keeps what these builders use. Snapback, the ports, Bun and Chrome, porting and release move to recipes
the lookup table names, so a builder who needs them is one name away. Pattern sources stay where they are.

## D3. Fewer checks, cleaner output

- The docs and `exact.mjs help` say `test` compiles, so no `contract build` before it.
- The iOS build's output leaves out warnings from Exact's own sources; a symbol error points to `contract vocab
  symbol` instead of repeating the roles.
- `verify` prints the time a run takes, so a builder waits once rather than polling.

## D4. A row whose tap area is a button

In a grouped section, a `button` or `link` that holds the row's title, with a later text beside it as the value, is
the row's title: it grows, its face is leading and vertically centred, and the later text is the trailing value, as
LLP 1116.020 D5 made a stack of lines the title. A lone button row stays an action cell; an authored `text-align` or
flex wins; the Mac keeps its push-button metrics.

## D5. Swipe deletes as iOS does

- `list-detail` writes `delete=remove(it.id)`; `list-rows.md` says iOS deletes on swipe without asking where the
  deletion is routine or recoverable (Mail, Notes, Reminders), and that an irreversible loss confirms, as a detail
  screen's Delete or a bulk clear does.
- A `button destructive=true` in the row a `for` repeats, under a grouped section or a list, with no `delete=` on the
  row, is advice: "if this button deletes the row, write `delete=` on the row and iOS offers it as a swipe".
  Structural only; a detail screen's section is not a repeated row.

## D6. `verify` without new tells

- `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on prose, a number or a date is a `no-tells` finding (it breaks inside words, "Octo/ber",
  "$15.2/1"); on a URL or a long token it is fine. `contract vocab overflow-wrap`, `verify`'s wording and the chat
  recipe point to wrapping between words.
- A symbol image's `width` or `height` in points beside text that scales is named, as a fixed `font-size` is;
  platform controls and parts fixed on purpose are exempt; `symbols.md` stops teaching `width=28`.

## D7. Smaller defaults and docs

- On iOS a modal route's controller paints the route's own background when the route has one (a grouped list's
  grey), so no white band shows between a sheet and the number pad's bar; a route with none keeps the system
  background. This amends LLP 1115 D2: a grouped sheet's surface is the platform's grouped background.
- The `rest` pattern's bar button shows the signed-in person's name.
- `SummaryCard` in a grouped row is named by a warning, as `EmptyState` in a section is.
- `navigation-stack.md`: a run (a study session, a workout) has one exit, which ends it or asks.
- `contract vocab search` and `list-detail` show a header search querying a server.
- The grader's prompt excuses UIKit's own syllable hyphenation in list cells and a truncated large title at the
  largest size; a break inside a word or number stays a tell. Round 19 is regraded under it before round 20 is
  compared.

## 7. How it is judged

| Measure | What it is | Stop rule |
|---|---|---|
| Lookups | round 20 against round 19, per model | lookup-only calls a build and names a call move (Muse's eleven lookup-only calls fall) |
| Tokens | Exact's arm against round 19, spec for spec, per model, with a paired interval; if the interval cannot clear 1.0, an A/B of the old and new main on LLP 1116.013's design | the interval clears 1.0, or the paired ratio is 0.90 or below |
| Fidelity | round 20 (round 17's pairs, prompts with D7's note, grader) against round 19 regraded under the same note | causes frozen against round 19 before the round, read as moved when a count falls by more than one an arm or to zero from five; the unmoved classes reported; quality, platform and bugs not down |
| The reference | each fidelity change beside a hand-built UIKit screen | none lands that looks less like it |
