   own NSTrackingArea: A the host's options [.mouseEnteredAndExited, .mouseMoved, .activeAlways, .inVisibleRect];
   B those plus .enabledDuringMouseDrag; C as B and D as A with the press passed to super. The same cliclick drag
   (31 leftMouseDragged events reach the window, an NSEvent local monitor shows them) over each column:
     A: A1+0 A1v A1^(drags 31)   then on the free move A1-0 A4+0
     B: B1+0 B1v B1^(drags 31)   then on the free move B1-0 B4+0

     D: D1+0                     then on the free move D1-0 D4+0
   A CGEvent poster that sets click state, deltas and pressure gave the same for B.
   -> With synthesized HID input, AppKit generated no mouseEntered/mouseExited during a drag for any column, with or
      without .enabledDuringMouseDrag, and none on mouseUp. NSTrackingArea.h says otherwise for hardware input:
      "NSTrackingEnabledDuringMouseDrag: If set, mouseEntered events will be generated as mouse is dragged. If not set,
      mouseEntered events will be generated as mouse is moved, and on mouseUp after a drag. mouseExited events are
      paired with mouseEntered events [...] a mouseExited event will be generated whether the mouse is being moved or
      dragged, independent of this flag." The platform behaviour with the flag is therefore read from the SDK header,
      not observed here; no hardware mouse was used.
