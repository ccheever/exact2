== Cause in the clone
The real-input session (6bac646cc): the right-click turned "theme" into "Theme" with the insertion point at 5,0 and a
highlight over the word (AppKit's correction indicator), and Cut left the field and clipboard unchanged. The shell's
input menu selects the word under the pointer (setSelectedRange) as Chromium does. Read from the session's observations:
on a field editor with automatic spelling correction on, AppKit accepted its pending correction for the word just typed
as the selection moved (this Mac has 'Capitalize words automatically' on), so the word became "Theme" and the selection
collapsed to 5,0: Cut had nothing to cut. AppKit has no capitalization switch on NSTextView: capitalization comes back from the spell checker as a
correction, which a text view asks for only with automatic spelling correction on:
   NSSpellChecker.isAutomaticCapitalizationEnabled (this Mac's setting): true
   NSTextView responds to a capitalization switch of its own: false
   field editor, AppKit's defaults: 8961 = orthography + replacement + correction + text completion
   field editor, autocorrect="off" (ExactKit applyTextChecking + keepsTypedText): 8193 = orthography + text completion
   NSSpellChecker on "i am here ": ["512 = correction {0, 1} -> I"]
The Settings field (and 51 other text inputs) had no autocorrect="off": ExactKit then leaves AppKit's correction,
text replacement and (in a textarea) smart quotes on. Every text input now has autocorrect="off" (ExactKit #111: no
correction, smart quotes, dashes or text replacement), as every textarea already had; a browser input never rewrites
what is typed. The capitalization itself was not reproduced here: in a window that is not key (the headless AppKit
tests, the agent's window on a locked screen) AppKit made no automatic correction at all in these probes, even after a
typed space ("theme " stayed lowercase with correction on). It stays real-input step RG-2.
