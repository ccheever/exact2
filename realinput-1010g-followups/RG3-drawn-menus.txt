Select All) and Electron pops it with popUpMenuPositioningItem:atLocation:inView:, which adds nothing (#407 read every

'AppKit's pop-up (6bac646cc)' is the same menu popped with NSMenu.popUpContextMenu and nothing switched off, as the
session's build did. Labels only: separators draw no label.
