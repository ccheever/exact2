# @exact/login-item by hand (LLP 1133.025 D2)

The drives and the person's steps are the desktop fixture's, shared by the
three desktop packages: `packages/dock/test/desktop.mjs` (drives 4 to 6)
and `packages/dock/test/checklist.md` (Mac step 6). The runner's tests are
`contract/cli/tests/it/desktop.rs`.

A login item may be registered only for the fixture bundle, and is
unregistered at the end of every run.
