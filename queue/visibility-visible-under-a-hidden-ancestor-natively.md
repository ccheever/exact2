**`visibility: visible` under a hidden ancestor, natively** (2026-10-04, b6 review B3; LLP 1001's declared deviation). CSS paints a descendant that sets its own `visible`; Apple hides the subtree with `isHidden`, and the Linux painter draws the hidden box's whole content through `Unpainted` (`paint.rs`, `drawn`). Linux could restore its backend before the children and skip only the box's own paint; Apple needs a view's own drawing (fills, borders, text, controls) hidden apart from its subviews, which `isHidden` and layer opacity cannot say. No app has needed it yet.
