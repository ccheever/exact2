**Keys, what native hosts still lack** (2026-10-04, x2apps calc F3 / minesweeper F7; docs/contract-grammar.md#keys). `key` bubbles and `preventDefault()` claims a key on every host now, but on macOS and iOS a scroller does not scroll by arrow, Space or page keys (the web's default), and iOS carries no `aria-keyshortcuts` (`UIKeyCommand`s from the focused session's buttons, as `ShortcutsMac.swift` routes them). The iOS hardware-keyboard path (`TextField.pressesBegan`) is driven only through the agent's synthesized keys; no real keyboard has pressed it.
