**Dropping the top visible row scrolls the List to follow it** (2026-09-25, the Apple reorder fix). The collection's scroll anchor is the key at the port's top (`capture_anchor`), and a reorder's data change restores it. When that key is the row just moved, the List jumps so the moved row is at the top again (seen on iOS and in `host/apple/src/arrange_tests.rs`, a `correction` of 80 points). This is the runner's, so every host does it. The anchor could skip the reorder's source.
