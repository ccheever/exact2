**`exact-update`'s `publisher::the_published_stream_is_staged_whole_by_a_client` flakes under load** (2026-10-04, the authoring bench's Hetzner box at load ~20): about 1 run in 3, `update store /tmp/exact-publisher-<pid>-stream is unavailable (exclusive owner): lock acquisition failed because the operation would block` (`update/tests/it/publisher.rs:287`). Alone it passes. Also seen on this Mac after main `c2e909694`: `store::a_valid_head_is_staged_and_selected_at_the_next_launch` refuses its reopened `exact-update-<pid>-staged` store at `support.rs:230` during the root suite; an immediate whole-binary recheck passes all 50 tests (`/tmp/exact2-garden-return-{root-tests,update-recheck}.log`). The cause is still unproven.
`a62f1e404` fixes a deterministic matching failure: a duplicated descriptor can keep the OS lock after the last `Store` or download snapshot drops. A shared guard explicitly unlocks at that boundary, preserving live-snapshot exclusion; its regression fails before the fix and passes after. All 63 update tests and the merged root suite pass. The sporadic runs' specific inherited descriptor was not captured; watch for recurrence before attributing every recorded refusal to this cause. Rivals diary 006 has the evidence.
