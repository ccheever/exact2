**Web view rows: memory against SwiftUI** (2026-09-28, LLP 1020 §10, LLP 1068 §5.2). The feed's web view rows now tie SwiftUI on the iPhone (97.6 against 97.4 fps) and beat it on the iPad (100.5 against 95.8). Footprint is still higher: 70–76 MB against SwiftUI's 38–44 on the iPhone, 61–82 against 43–50 on the iPad. The two oddities are noise. Per-run peaks on the iPad are bimodal (about 62 or 81 MB) with and without near-only leaves, so the medians of three moved with the draw. System Trace's WebContent launch counts vary by ±100 between runs of one build (SwiftUI 290 and 479). A leaf, once made, stays until its row retires two viewports away, so it cannot churn at the quarter-viewport margin. Where the extra ~30 MB lives (the app's WebKit proxies, the text rasters, the pool) is unmeasured.
