**Daily (the mail app) on Android's owner thread: what is owed** (2026-10-05, `android/damage`, Nothing Phone (4a) Pro; the reader's half is a bench patch, `~/bench/lists/exact-wt-damage/tools-dmg/reader-damage.patch`). Landed: a touch down that changes nothing is no frame (`canvas/stream.rs`: a stream whose drawing is the reader's and that defines nothing); a press wakes the GPU offscreen (the reader's `Warm`, a 1x1 renderer of its own), so a release's response no longer pays 7-10 ms of cold GPU; the reader retains the view's drawing when a stream's top level is the last one's (kept rows recorded again into their predecessor's node, changed groups only) and bounds each animated layer's node (clipped), so a toggle damages its row, not the window; `scroll > column > rows` keeps the column's children as rows. Janky frames, exact vs Views, 3 runs interleaved: tabs 7/6, nav 2/19, sheet 3/12, settings 14/3, dialog 3/13, type 71/99 (before: 9/6, 12/21, 12/12, 10/2, 12/8, 66/102). Owed: (1) settings: what is left is SurfaceFlinger's, not the app's: a first frame after ~200 ms idle presents a vsync late ("Display HAL", then "Unknown Jank" and "SurfaceFlinger Scheduling"), 1-5 such per run (each three janky frames) against Views' 0-1; not damage (retain=0 the same), not the release's wait (wait=0 the same), not a window frame at the press (worse). (2) Tabs p90 latency (44-61 vs Views 42) is the first visit of Starred (the runner mounts the list, 17-21 ms in `exact touch`), and in about half the runs with the press warm-up one frame after it takes 30-35 ms of GPU (never with warm=0); unexplained. (3) Colour transitions (`background-color`, `color`) are still painted every frame: the tab labels' 250 ms redraw the window (the top level changes); lowering them, or a top level split into nodes, would make those frames partial. (4) The type scenario's ~95 ms for Views is the metric's (the driver dispatches keys inside a Choreographer callback).
