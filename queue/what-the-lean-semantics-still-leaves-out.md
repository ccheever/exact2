**What the Lean semantics still leaves out** (2026-10-04, semantics/README.md). Routes landed; `t(...)`, the `format*` entries, geometry reads, frame tasks, a mutation's `then`, form-control payloads and the `navigate` event remain. `difftest explore apps/*/app.contract` reports Caltrain (`formatTime`), Messages (frame tasks) and RealWorld (`then`) as outside it. A virtualized list's rows are left out of the observation (the runner windows them by layout), so a scripted tap on one is not compared meaningfully.
