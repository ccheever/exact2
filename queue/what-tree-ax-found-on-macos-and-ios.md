**What `tree --ax` found on macOS and iOS** (2026-10-03, LLP 1080.002 §7). AppKit exposes the accessibility fixture's `aria-hidden` decoration and the button inside its `inert` column (`exposed-hidden` ×2, LLP 1001's "AppKit/Linux subtree enforcement remains open"), and its checkbox and password field are exposed through cells that answer no name (the label is set on the control view; whether VoiceOver speaks it is unverified). On iOS a `role="img"` column exposes its inner button and no image (the web exposes `image "Chart"`). Not built: a projected segment's join to its tab, and the macOS window toolbar's walk (both reported as coverage limits). On iOS the read needs UIKit's accessibility runtime: `xcrun simctl spawn <udid> defaults write com.apple.Accessibility ApplicationAccessibilityEnabled -bool true` on a simulator, then relaunch; the driver could set it for an `ax` drive.
