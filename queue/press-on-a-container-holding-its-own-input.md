**`press` on a container holding its own input** (2026-09-23, buttons lane): Messages' two emoji-picker columns (`apps/messages/app.contract` `column press=showEmojiPicker`, lines 817 and 837) put `press` on a container that holds the picker's full-size transparent `input`, so neither can be a `button` (an input inside a `<button>` is interactive content in interactive content). Once they are restructured — the input could take the tap itself — `press` can belong to `button` and `link` alone and be a compile error elsewhere, where today it makes a `<div>` with a click listener and no keyboard access.
