**npm in `app.ts`, and backend SDKs** (2026-10-05, Depot feedback: three handwritten wire adapters, Supabase's session persistence and token refresh by hand). Two problems. Bundling: the bake refuses outside imports "until dependency capture is implemented" (`js/bake/src/lib.rs`, `sources`), and LLP 1091 D10's package linking for Contract is most of that capture. The model: an SDK lives between answers (refresh timers, a socket it writes, `localStorage`), and a data module runs only inside one, refusing a `fetch` or storage call outside it. Pure packages work once bundling does; an SDK needs a client lifetime beyond one answer or a first-party adapter. An RFC with supabase-js and the Convex client as the cases; offline (snapshot cache, purge on sign-out, the persisted half of the keep-last-value line below) follows from it.
