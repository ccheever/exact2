**The document window, what the studio pass left** (2026-10-04, fix/macdocs; LLP 1069.010 "As built: the document window"). A ⌘Q that a window's `beforeunload` keeps open is cancelled, not resumed once the app's question is answered (NSDocument's review continues the quit); `wheel` and `drop` have no iPadOS form (a trackpad's scroll, a drag from Files) and `title` no `UIToolTipInteraction`; a command's menu is by chord only, so Zoom to Fit ⌘9 stays in File beside View's zooms; the bake does not warn when a declared extension already belongs to another app's type on the Mac (Freeform's `.board`); repeating gradients and length stops are refused everywhere rather than painted natively (the kernel takes percentage stops only).
