**Style names and their kinds in `schema.json`** (2026-10-03, [LLP 1081](../llp/1081-names-exact-invents.rfc.md) Q5). LLP 1081 puts the author style names, with provenance, in Contract's `style_names.rs`. Moving them into the schema is a mapping design, not two new fields: names and rows are many to many (`gap` sets two rows, `translate` sets `Translate` and `TranslateZ`, `flex` sets three different values).
