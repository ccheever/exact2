**Pointer events, what is left** (2026-10-04, [LLP 1056](../llp/1056-canvas-2d.rfc.md) §8.6; the paint diary's F1). No `coalesced` list: a stroke is a polyline at the display's rate, and DOM's `getCoalescedEvents()` as a field needs a second record type (a record cannot list itself in the plan's types). An iPad's hovering pointer and pencil send no free `pointermove` (UIKit's hover recognizer). Linux delivers every move, as its `pan` does, not one a frame. The Mac agent's held drags read `pressedMouseButtons` beside the event's own button; a synthesized pen is never a tablet event, so pen pressure on macOS is untested by script. `exact new`'s template now passes the module's canvas roster; apps made before it (x2apps) have to add `.with_canvas_surfaces(CANVAS_SURFACES)` to their `apple/` and `web/` crates by hand.
