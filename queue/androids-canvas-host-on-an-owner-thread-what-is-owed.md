**Android's Canvas host on an owner thread: what is owed** (2026-10-04, LLP 1072 on Android; the reader is `~/bench/lists/exact/android/canvas/.../Owner.kt`, outside the repo; `owner=0` is the old main-thread host). exact2 runs on an `exact` HandlerThread from boot (its looper takes executor and image fds; its Choreographer the turns); each op stream is copied and published, the main thread ingests and replays it, and a scroll translates the feed's group on main at once (the group holds every mounted row, so the mounted window is the coverage). Fling fps now ties or beats Views at every speed on all four lists on a Pixel 10 Pro XL, a Pixel 10a and a Nothing Phone (4a) Pro (xheavy 24k: 101–106 vs Views 79–83 on the Pixels, 74 vs 63 on the Nothing; owner=0 60–71). Owed: (1) CPU: more ms/s than owner=0 on light lists (easy +10–25%), fewer Mcycles on the Pixel, more on the Nothing; the exact thread is not boosted as main and RenderThread are (ADPF hint sessions measured no better). (2) Input: a press waits ≤5 ms and a key ≤2 ms for the answer within the frame; a 16 ms wait held frames (9–15 janky per daily scenario); a tab switch's ~14 ms of `pointer_up` arrives a frame or two late (39 vs Views' 16 ms). (3) WebView rows: `WebViewFunctor::drawVk` costs 2.3× Views' per draw (12.7 vs 5.5 ms) inside HWUI's `flush layers`, with `initVk` and shader compiles each draw (Views pays the init too). (4) Memory after a crypto fling on the Pixel: 319 vs 197 MB (graphics 200 vs 153, native heap 76 vs 32, Java 19 vs 6); recycling the stream copies did not move the Java heap. (5) Slicing a collection pass between frames on main (GapWorker-style, `refine(limit, velocity)`) painted every frame and cost fps; the limit plumbing stays for fills.
