**Large static worlds still re-encode Rapier's snapshot every capture** (2026-10-05, game diary 005): entries, hole verification and their digests and saves now cost what changed (warm 100k-tree hash 9.9 → 1.4 ms, physics save 78 → 2.7 ms). What remains is O(colliders): Rapier's bytes are re-encoded and digested whole after each step (about 5 ms at 100k, three quarters the broad-phase BVH, whose nodes are Parry's private fields and whose change flags shift its length every step), the first save after a load encodes every entry page, and cold hashing. Changing either needs the BVH's own record of changed nodes.
