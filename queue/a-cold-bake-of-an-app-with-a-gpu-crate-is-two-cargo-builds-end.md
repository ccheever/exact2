**A cold bake of an app with a GPU crate is two Cargo builds end to end: measured, not built** (2026-10-04, Cargo's `--timings`, Caltrain on the ten-core M4, before the registry crates were kept; LLP 1036.000 §7). The GPU crate first, 39 s for 146 unit-seconds, because it is one chain (naga 14 s → wgpu-core 13 s → wgpu 7 s); then the app's archive, 49 s for 366, whose chain is the kernel (33 s) and the app (3.5 s). They are in sequence because the app's build script binds the GPU product (LLP 1009 D6), and nothing else can warm the app's dependencies meanwhile: asked for by another selection they are other units (`-p exact-apple` recompiles 25 crates in a warm target, the kernel among them; with the GPU crate, 32). Overlapping them needs the GPU crate in its own target directory and the app's dependencies compiled before its build script may run, which is a change to what `buildBake` promises on every platform. It is worth less than the chains suggest on ten cores, where ExactKit's compile already fills the GPU phase's idle ones (the three together are 660 CPU-seconds, 66 s at best), and more on eighteen. In a checkout that starts from the kept registry crates (§11) the two builds are 12.9 s (wgpu-hal 3 s → wgpu-core 9 s → wgpu 5 s, then ours) and 37 s (the kernel, 30 s), so overlapping them is worth at most those 13 s of a 58 s first build.
