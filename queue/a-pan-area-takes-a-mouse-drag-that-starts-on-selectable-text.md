**A `pan` area takes a mouse drag that starts on selectable text** (2026-10-04, fix/typo, the reader diary). The reader's paged mode pans its page area by pointer, so a mouse drag over a paragraph with `selectionchange` turns the page instead of selecting (web: `input-glue.js` `pan` prevents the pointerdown; scroll mode selects on web and macOS). A rule like rule 3's for controls — a mouse contact that starts on a `text` with `selectionchange` is the selection's, a touch still pans — is a gesture-arbitration decision (LLP 1057).
