**iOS paints at half device pixels where the browser snaps** (2026-09-26, fix/ios-pixel-parity, heavy list on the iPhone 17 Pro). Frames stay fractional (LLP 1001 §6) and UIKit composites a text bitmap or a 0.5 pt box at its fractional position; the browser snaps each painted rect and baseline to device pixels. So a row whose top lands on x.5 device pixels shows its text blurred across two rows, a 0.5 pt hairline is 2 device pixels where the browser's is 1, and edges sit one device pixel off. Snapping a view's absolute edges in the presenter (not its parent-relative frame) would match; it touches every view.
