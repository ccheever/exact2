**Game texture families in bundles and on the wire** (2026-09-23, textures lane; [LLP 1046.003](../llp/1046.003-game-engine-as-built.explainer.md#compressed-textures-2026-09-23)): every baked texture ships as `x.tex` (RGBA8), `x.bc.tex` and `x.astc.tex`, and a device fetches one — on the web. Native bundles copy all three (the Fox's 5.6 MB becomes 8.4 MB); a macOS bundle needs BC plus the fallback, an iOS device ASTC plus the fallback, but the bake's compatibility inventory lists every asset, so thinning is a bake change, not a copy filter. Separately, `host/web/serve.mjs` sends `.tex`/`.model` as uncompressed `application/octet-stream`; RGBA8 payloads gzip 70× (the Fox's 5.6 MB → 80 KB) and blocks 1.2–17× — a compressible type for game assets would cut the fallback's download.
