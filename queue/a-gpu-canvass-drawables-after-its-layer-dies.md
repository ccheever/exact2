**A GPU canvas's drawables after its layer dies** (2026-09-30, the rest lane). The Extra Heavy shader rows' purgeable GPU memory grew with the rows passed because Core Animation lets a dead `CAMetalLayer`'s drawables go late: an iPad's 19-kind fling made 37–38 layers with at most two or three alive, and the purgeable ledger held 100–121 MB at the peak. Pooling the layers (LLP 1068 §0.4: 12 made, three spares kept) took the peak 322 → 288 MB (medians of three rounds; SwiftUI's 319 is the bar). What is left: the spares' count against rows turning over faster than the pool refills (six spares measured next), and a canvas that leaves the screen still holds its layer and its three drawables until its row is retired — a target lent only while on screen (`Module::attach`/`detach`, kept on `perf/canvas-pool`) would take the shader-only feed's 12–14 alive layers down to the four on screen.
