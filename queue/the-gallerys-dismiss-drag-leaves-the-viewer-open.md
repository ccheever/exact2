**The gallery's dismiss drag leaves the viewer open** (2026-09-28, LLP 1057.003 phase 1). At Fit a far or fast release sends the photo off on its spring and the backdrop fades to 0 with it, but the viewer stays, empty, until Back. Closing it when the exit ends needs an end-of-motion event (CSS's `transitionend`) or an action that can call the `command` prop; Contract has neither. C6's thrown card waits for "Deal again" for the same reason.
