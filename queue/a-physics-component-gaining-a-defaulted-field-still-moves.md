**A physics component gaining a defaulted field still moves physics games' pins** (2026-10-05). Component rows and resources now save and hash over their type's `Default` (`Data::write_over`), but the `Physics` resource's EXPHYS snapshot keeps each body's `Body` and `Collider` in `Option`s inside `Entry`, written whole and hashed whole (`hash::of(entry)`), so a new field on either moves the snapshot bytes and the world hash. Writing an entry's payloads over `Default` changes EXPHYS bytes; weigh it with the capture-cost item below.
