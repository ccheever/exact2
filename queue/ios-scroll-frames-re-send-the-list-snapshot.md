The runner's `scroll` dispatch is 0.41–0.45 ms an event and the host's snapshot path 0.30–0.32 ms on the simulator, ~0.7 ms a scroll frame; no late frames now. Not emitting a snapshot whose only change is `scrollSequence`, or not serializing collections on a commit that touched none, is LLP 1010/1050 semantics.
