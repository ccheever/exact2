**Signposts cost nothing measurable unless something records them** (2026-09-30). An `OSSignposter` begin/end pair costs about 0.4 us with nothing recording, well under 1 ms/s at our rates. Under Time Profiler, whose kdebug session makes each emit expensive, exact2's own intervals (`scrolled`, `pump-*`, `apply`, `text-raster-*`) cost 17-29 ms/s on the iPhone. So traces overstate exact2 by that much against SwiftUI and UIKit; subtract it when comparing. Not gated. The iPhone 13 Pro Max also logs CFPrefs lookups at debug level (about 20-50 ms/s of `os_log` in every app's traces, SwiftUI's and UIKit's included), which is where the "48 ms/s of logging on main" came from. Safe-area and layout-margin updates cost 4-5 ms/s in exact2 against 3-4 in SwiftUI and UIKit: not worth a change.
