**Grouped lists, what is left** (2026-10-03, [LLP 1084](../llp/1084-native-grouped-lists.rfc.md) §4, §7). The large-title collapse follows the route's authored scroll, which a grouped list hides: `setContentScrollView` should take the collection view. A grouped list's rows take no `swipeContent` (the list configuration's own swipe providers would). The Signal Clone's `Section`/`Row`/`ActionRow` components can become a grouped list (its repository). `sidebar` styles wait for an iPad or Mac consumer.
