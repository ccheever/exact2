**Recoverable expression traps** (2026-09-27, LLP 1017.003 D3; Charlie agreed). A binding trap at run time (`IterationLimit`, `StringTooLong`, `ValueTooLarge`, `TypeMismatch`) poisons the runner, so the app stops updating. `map`/`filter` make data-dependent traps reachable: a view fine at 500 items can trap at 50,000 in production. Proposal: a trapping binding keeps its last good value (or its initial one), the journal and the agent's `state` report the trap with its pc and binding, and the rest of the tree keeps updating. Transaction work for every trap, not a map/filter change; not built in feat/contract-listmap.
