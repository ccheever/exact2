**The JS jump lands what a timer sent at its last instant** (2026-10-04, LLP 1092 "As built"). `host/web-js/agent.js`'s `clock +N` waits for every reply after a stop and runs its `then`; the wasm glue, the Linux agent and Apple stop waiting once no timer is due by the jump's end, so a request a timer sends at exactly `to` lands there on real time and its `then` waits for the next operation. Conformance avoids it (`queue.contract`'s timer sends nothing); pick one behaviour and make the other hosts follow.
