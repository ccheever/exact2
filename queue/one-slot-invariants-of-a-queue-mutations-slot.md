**One-slot invariants of a queue mutation's slot** (2026-10-05, LLP 1092 D12, semantics/README.md "Queued sends and gated tasks"). `Reachable.slotIn` and its siblings are proved of slots no drain writes (`isQueue p x = false`). For a queue mutation's slot, carry the property of the answers its waiting sends will land (`∀ q ∈ c.queued, q.1 = x → ∀ v, V (.some v)`) through `runAction` and `nextCommit`. Lean's `pending(m)` of a waiting queue is false (the runner's is true): model it if a program needs it.
