**Glass groups, what is owed** (2026-10-02, [LLP 1053.000.000](../llp/1053.000.000-glass-groups.rfc.md) §5, built on `lane/glass-group`). (1) The press: interactive glass swelling across the spacing into a neighbour (Messages' inbox bar is grouped at `glassGroup=8` for it), seen on a phone or with the driver's held contact, which moves the Mac's pointer. (2) From James's prototype: `auto` spacing from the row's `gap`, and a morph when a member is replaced. (3) Whether a `style` class may carry host-policy props (`backgroundMaterial`, `glassGroup`): today "a style holds style rows only", where James's style-row spelling could be put in a class. (4) A UIKit glass made another way (a stock glass button) must register with `GlassGroups` to be isolated.
