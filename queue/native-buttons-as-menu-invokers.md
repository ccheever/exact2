**Native buttons as menu invokers** (2026-10-02, [LLP 1069.011.000](../llp/1069.011.000-native-buttons-everywhere.rfc.md) D5, both reviews of its r2). A native button with an opening `popovertarget`/`commandfor` is refused (one that only closes, a confirmation's row, is admitted): its activation (an own or ancestor handler, the menu's opening, a confirmation, Enter/Space, shortcuts, the agent) needs one routine, and today `Presenter.press` opens the macOS menu before any revalidation (`PresenterMac.swift:684-689`) and the iOS deferred provider and `openConfirmation` press only the invoker's own handler (`MenusIOS.swift:118-129`, `:248`). The reviews' requirements: resolve and press the target once, revalidate after the synchronous batch, then open exactly once from the control; no second provider run under `presentMenu`; agent mode keeps the painted popover.
