**Clipboard events in native text fields** (2026-10-04, fix/a11y): `copy`, `cut` and `paste` reach a focused box on every host and a field on the web, but a macOS or iOS text field's or textarea's own editing fires none of them (the field editor and UITextField take the Edit actions). A field editor subclass (AppKit) and `UITextField`/`UITextView` overrides would deliver them before the platform's own paste.
