**Fragmented text: multi-column, `widows`/`orphans`, `break-*`** (2026-10-04, fix/typo, the reader diary). Refused by name now (`contract_lower::tags::fragmentation`, LLP 1001 §6): the kernel has no fragmentation, so one paragraph cannot continue from one box into the next. What would buy it: a kernel "fragmentainer" for a fixed-height block whose children (and a child paragraph's lines, cut at a line box) flow through N column boxes of `column-width` with `column-gap`, every native host painting a paragraph's line ranges at per-fragment origins (Apple's `Paragraph.origins`, Linux's flow fragments already do this for exclusions), and `widows`/`orphans` as the cut rule. The reader's two-page spread and pagination would be one flow. That design is [LLP 1093](../llp/1093-text-that-flows-across-boxes.rfc.md) (r2, admitted in DEFERRED.md); the refusals name it and stay until its stages land.
