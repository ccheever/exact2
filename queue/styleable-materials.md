**Styleable materials** (2026-10-03, [LLP 1081](../llp/1081-names-exact-invents.rfc.md) Q3). A `style` class cannot carry `backgroundMaterial` or `glassGroup` because they are props; LLP 1069.011 D12 already makes `buttonStyle` a styleable prop, and these two can join it. WebKit's `-apple-visual-effect` is not a browser name under LLP 1081 D1 (it is behind `useSystemAppearance`), so there is nothing to adopt.
