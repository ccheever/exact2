**Android steady-state CPU, what is left after `android/cpu`** (2026-10-05, merged as 98b178bd2; Pixel 10a: the exact thread −3 to −20%, fps unchanged, still more total CPU than Views at every app and speed). (1) The text cache's trim runs after every paint, walking ~450 entries with a sort: 18–22 ms/s on crypto; once per batch of new texts would save most of it (its tests check trim timing). (2) xheavy at 12k/24k: MapLibre's "Worker" threads take ~1,100–1,400 ms/s against Views' ~300 (simpleperf: all inside libmaplibre.so, stripped). Ruled out on 2026-10-05: style loading (the reader's pool makes only 2 MapViews a fling), pooled maps left running (stopping them on reset: no change), maps of rows off screen (stopping them when their row is a quarter screen away, 104 transitions a fling: no change), the span (0.02° in both). Left: the camera (bounds fit vs Views' `newLatLngZoom`), marker churn (`clear()` + `addMarker` per bind vs Views' one marker), and the two instances rebinding to a new place every map row (Views keeps more holders). (3) xheavy at rest: ~130 ms/s submitting and presenting the shader canvas through wgpu/Vulkan every frame, mostly in the driver; drawing it in the reader's own RenderThread pass would avoid it. (4) heavy: the picture budget is smaller than a fling's working set, so pictures decode again (`imgvp=24`: decode −45% at 24k, PSS 344 → 506 MB); avatars decoded at a power-of-two scale would be cheaper. (5) crypto at rest: the bench's source clones the 5,000-row list each 10 Hz tick (~26 ms/s) and `update_data` diffs it (~15 ms/s); a source that says which rows changed (as the iOS crypto note above asks) removes both. (6) Per-row construction: rows are now rebound, not built, in the Canvas host (LLP 1078, `android/reuse`: the `exact` thread −17 to −28% on easy at 6k–24k, −8 to −16% on crypto, heavy and xheavy within noise). What a rebound row still costs is its content: layout of its changed text, binding evaluation (crypto's chart `points`), recording it again, and for heavy/xheavy shaping and pictures; easy's process CPU ties Views at 24k and is ~8% over at 12k, crypto's ~60% over. Rows holding a refused leaf (canvas, map, video, web view, inner list) are still built; the Apple and web hosts do not opt in yet. (7) scudo was ~60 ms/s; mimalloc 2.x is now the default (2026-10-05, dd34e6948; `EXACT_MALLOC=system` to compare).
