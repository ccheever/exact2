**Kept answers: repeated activation and slow first answers** (2026-10-02, [LLP 1027.005](../llp/1027.005-resource-identity-and-request-context.rfc.md) D4/D5). Two issues reproduced during review, neither part of the `with` landing. (1) Consecutive `data_ready()` calls before an asynchronous reply issued tickets 1 and 2: a forced ask bypasses `enqueue`'s identical-request rule. No host calls it twice today (Apple and Linux guard with `data_activated`, wasm activates once per boot), so the fix is small and only for a future host. (2) Kept answers are admitted only for a source unready at boot and then remain visible while its asynchronous reply is pending: on the JS runtime, Fieldnotes's real TypeScript source showed the bake's "Opening your notebook…" until a controlled SQLite reply, and the runner does the same for any source ready at boot. The JS target also keeps nothing for a Rust module loaded after first paint. Whether a consumer needs either closed is follow-up 3 of the RFC.
