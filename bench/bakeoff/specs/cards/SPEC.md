# Cards

Arabic and English flashcards for spaced repetition, designed to expose right-to-left and mixed-direction text.

Data: `data/cards.json`, 140 seeded cards. Arabic fronts are marked `direction: rtl`; English fronts are `ltr`.

1. The study screen shows one card at a time, with the front right-aligned for Arabic and left-aligned for English.
   Mixed Arabic, Latin names, numbers and punctuation keep each run in its natural direction.
2. Tap to reveal the back, then choose Again, Hard, Good or Easy. The next due time and interval update immediately,
   and the next card is selected without losing the current direction.
3. A Browse screen lists every card with its due date and direction. Search works across Arabic and English text.
4. Add, edit and delete a card in a sheet. Keep the original text as entered, including Arabic diacritics and Latin
   URLs, and expose a useful VoiceOver label for each card and rating button.
5. Changes survive relaunching. The layout remains usable in dark mode and at the largest text size without clipping,
   overlap or a forced left-to-right alignment.
