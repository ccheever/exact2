# Recipes

An iPhone recipe browser.

Data: `data/recipes.json`, 160 recipes (`id`, `title`, `category`, `minutes`, `servings`, `rating`, `ingredients`, `steps`). Ship it with the app.

1. The main screen lists all recipes with title, category, time and rating.
2. Filter by category (all nine, plus All) and search by title or ingredient; both work together.
3. Sort by name, time or rating.
4. A recipe's screen shows its time, servings, ingredients and numbered steps. A servings stepper scales the ingredient quantities shown.
5. Save a recipe to Favorites and remove it. A Favorites tab lists saved recipes; it says so helpfully when empty.
6. Favorites survive relaunching the app.
