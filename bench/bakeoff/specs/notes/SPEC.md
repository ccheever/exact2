# Notes

An iPhone notes app.

Data: `data/notes.json`, 90 notes (`id`, `title`, `body`, `updated`, `pinned`). Ship them as the starting notes.

1. The main screen lists notes most recently updated first, with title, a one-line preview of the body and a relative or short date. Pinned notes come first in their own section.
2. Search filters notes by title or body as you type.
3. Tap a note to edit its title and body; edits save without a Save button and move the note to the top.
4. Create a new note; an empty new note is discarded when you leave it.
5. Pin and unpin a note, and delete a note, from the list.
6. A count of notes is shown.
7. Everything survives relaunching the app.
