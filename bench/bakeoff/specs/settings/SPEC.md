# Settings

The settings screen of an iPhone reading app, "Paperback", with no other screens than settings.

There is no data file.

1. A long grouped settings screen with these sections: Account (name, email, a Sign Out button that asks to confirm), Reading (font size slider, font picker with five fonts, line spacing segmented control, a live preview paragraph), Appearance (theme: System, Light, Dark, Sepia), Library (sort order picker, show finished books toggle, download over cellular toggle), Notifications (a master toggle; when on, reveal daily reminder time and three more toggles), Storage (used space shown, Clear Cache with a confirmation), and About (version, links to Privacy and Terms opening simple screens, Rate the App).
2. Pickers open their choices in the way an iPhone settings screen would.
3. The preview paragraph reflects font, size and spacing as they change.
4. Every setting survives relaunching the app.
5. Reset All Settings at the bottom, with confirmation, restores the defaults.
