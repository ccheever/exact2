   generated shell, and release semantic flags in gpu-dev. Linux repin requires

   A selected fixed consumer still hashes its own authored sources, excluding its
   own tests/proof/pins. The tests assert both sides of that distinction.

   and one drained `{requests, retired}` asset result. Adapt staged web reloads
