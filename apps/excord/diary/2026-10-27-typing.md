### Rough

- "… is typing…" went by one timer, `after(10000)` from whenever it was armed, so it
  counted from the timer, not from each typer: with Ada typing at 0 s and Grace at 4 s,
  "Ada and Grace are typing…" stayed until 14 s, typing that began in a channel left and
  opened again stayed a fresh ten seconds, and a typer's message left the others a fresh ten
  seconds. A timer's interval has to be a literal (`docs/contract-grammar.md`), so it
  cannot be set to the next expiry.
- The line is now its own resource, `typing`, read with the page's time: when the channel
  changes, when it opens, and once a second while anyone types, so each typer goes within a
  second of their own ten seconds. Reading the channel's record once a second instead would
  also find its first unread message in the cached transcript each time (Astra: about 10,000
  row reads a second with a long history), and the record was read with the data module's
  clock, which only a heartbeat or a send moves.
