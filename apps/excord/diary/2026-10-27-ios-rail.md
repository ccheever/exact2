### Rough

  white strip. The rail is a scroller beside the channel list, so under LLP 1123 neither
  reaches the route's bottom edge and the route pads the home indicator. Laying the rail

  sections' insets then narrowed each row by the padding twice, because the host reported

  applies it inside the padded box. Astra found that and fixed both in the host.

  rail until the padding went on them too. And a section's leading inset lands on the left
  in a right-to-left list as well; mapping the padding by direction put it on the wrong
  side, which only a test in a scene's window showed.
- The rail is not the route's primary scroller, so UIKit gives it no bottom inset: with
  enough servers to scroll, the last one can rest under the home indicator.
