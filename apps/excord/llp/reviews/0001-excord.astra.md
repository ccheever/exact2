1. **§2 is enough for a disposable-account experiment, not for qualifying the main account as safe. Confidence: 0.97.** Human-driven writes and rate limits reduce accidental abuse. They do not establish the enforcement boundary that “behaviour Discord tolerates” assumes. A clean week of read-only activity on another account supplies no meaningful assurance about subsequent writes or future enforcement. This matters because losing the account can defeat the purpose of the client. I would keep all development on an expendable account, remove the one-week promotion criterion, and treat main-account use as an independent acceptance of unquantified risk. [§2](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1141-excord.rfc.md:41)

2. **Choose §4.2(b); the proposed 1 ms exception is insufficient justification for (a). Confidence: 0.95.** Native stream answers are [serialized](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/js/src/prelude.js:1037), and socket snapshots currently [commit individually](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1016.000-answers-that-keep-coming.rfc.md:327). Publishing the whole store therefore couples transcript responsiveness to total cached data and account-wide event traffic, including unrelated channels. A measurement at 5,000 messages does not bound either quantity; list virtualization does not remove that work. Keep one normalized store in TypeScript, publish small versions, and expose bounded window resources only where consumed. Measure the complete update path under bursts while typing and scrolling. That preserves a defensible performance boundary.

3. **The iOS gate can pass without proving the project’s defining quality claim.** [Stage 1](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1141-excord.rfc.md:242) accepts simulator tests or filmed drives for §3.14’s physical-device frame-rate requirement. Under the deterministic agent clock, [`perf frames` reports virtual](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1079-speed-hygiene-in-the-agents-hands.rfc.md:377); screenshots every 50 ms cannot establish 120 Hz performance. Stage 3’s device gate checks service writes, while accessibility appears in the macOS gate. The plan can therefore advance without establishing excellent iOS interaction. Make physical-device, real-clock validation part of iOS completion: concurrent traffic, typing, flinging and keyboard movement, plus VoiceOver and Dynamic Type on iOS.

4. **§6 assumes a more complicated list design than message jumps require.** Discontiguous history belongs in the cache; the displayed list can contain one contiguous resident window. Jumping loads and selects the destination window, then positions its target; “Jump to present” selects the tail. Exact already [supports keyed jumps with measurement and correction](/Users/ccheever/projects/exact2/llp/1070.000-scroll-into-view.rfc.md:38), including previously unmeasured rows. I would use that design and fix any demonstrated replacement-and-positioning defect. Adding discontiguous-window semantics to the virtualizer would expand the framework across hosts without being necessary for §3.6.
